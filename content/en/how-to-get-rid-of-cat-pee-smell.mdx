To effectively get rid of cat pee smell, immediate action is crucial: blot up fresh urine and then saturate the area with an enzymatic cleaner. These specialized cleaners break down the uric acid crystals responsible for the persistent, foul odor. For deep-set or stubborn smells, repeated treatments or professional intervention may be necessary to achieve lasting freshness.

## What You Need

- Paper towels or absorbent cloths
- Rubber gloves
- Enzymatic pet odor remover
- White vinegar
- Baking soda
- Spray bottle
- Stiff brush (for carpets)
- Black light (optional, for locating dried stains)
- Plastic wrap or foil (optional, to keep cleaner wet)

## Steps

### Step 1: Locate the Source

Before you can clean, you must find all affected areas. Use your nose to sniff out the spots, or for dried, invisible stains, turn off the lights and use a black light (UV flashlight) to pinpoint them. Dried urine stains will fluoresce under UV light, making them easier to identify on carpets, upholstery, or hard surfaces.

### Step 2: Blot Fresh Urine Immediately

For wet spots, act fast! Place several layers of paper towels or a clean, old towel over the urine and press down firmly to absorb as much liquid as possible. Stand on the towels to apply pressure. Avoid rubbing, as this can spread the urine and push it deeper into the material fibers. Repeat this process with fresh towels until no more moisture transfers.

### Step 3: Apply an Enzymatic Cleaner

Generously saturate the soiled area with a high-quality enzymatic pet odor cleaner. These cleaners contain beneficial enzymes that break down the uric acid crystals found in cat urine, which are the primary source of the pungent, lingering smell. Ensure the cleaner penetrates as deeply as the urine did. Follow the product's instructions carefully for dwell time and reapplication, as this is the most critical step for truly permanent odor removal. For a comprehensive approach to lasting freshness, consider checking out our guide on [How to Get Rid of Cat Pee Smell Permanently & Effectively](/en/how-to-remove-cat-urine-odor).

### Step 4: Allow to Dwell and Dry Completely

Let the enzymatic cleaner work for the recommended time, which can range from several hours to overnight, depending on the product and severity of the stain. You can cover the treated area with plastic wrap or foil to prevent the cleaner from drying out too quickly, allowing the enzymes more time to break down the urine. Once the dwell time is complete, remove any covering and allow the area to air dry completely, which may take a day or two.

### Step 5: Neutralize with Vinegar (Optional Pre-Treatment)

For very strong or old smells, after blotting up any initial liquid, you can pre-treat the area with a solution of equal parts white vinegar and water in a spray bottle. Spray generously, let it sit for 15-30 minutes, then blot dry before applying the enzymatic cleaner. Vinegar is an excellent natural deodorizer that helps neutralize the alkaline salts in urine, though it won't break down uric acid crystals like an enzyme cleaner.

### Step 6: Deodorize with Baking Soda

Once the area is completely dry from the enzymatic cleaner, sprinkle a generous amount of baking soda over the treated spot. Baking soda is a natural and effective deodorizer that can absorb any lingering odors that might remain. Let it sit for several hours, or even overnight, to allow it ample time to work its magic and absorb odors effectively.

### Step 7: Vacuum Thoroughly

After the baking soda has had sufficient time to absorb odors, vacuum the area thoroughly to remove all powder and any remaining residue. Ensure your vacuum has strong suction to lift all the baking soda from carpet fibers or crevices. ## Tips & Tricks

- Always test any cleaning solution on an inconspicuous area first to ensure it doesn't cause discoloration or damage to your specific material.
- Never use ammonia-based cleaners, as their scent mimics urine and can attract your cat to urinate in the same spot again.
- Address the underlying reason for your cat's inappropriate urination (e.g., litter box issues, stress, medical problems) to prevent future accidents.
- Ensure good ventilation when cleaning by opening windows or using fans to help air out the space and speed up drying.
- For severe or widespread saturation, especially in carpets with padding or upholstered furniture, professional cleaning or replacement of affected materials might be the only permanent solution.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Always wear gloves when cleaning cat urine to protect your hands from bacteria and harsh cleaning agents.
- ⚠️ Never mix different cleaning chemicals, especially ammonia-based products with bleach, as this can produce toxic and dangerous fumes.
- ⚠️ Ensure the area is well-ventilated during and after cleaning to avoid inhaling strong odors or chemical fumes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does cat pee smell so bad and linger?**

Cat urine contains highly concentrated uric acid crystals that do not dissolve easily in water. As these crystals dry, they release a strong, pungent ammonia-like odor that can reactivate with humidity, causing the smell to reappear even after initial cleaning attempts.

**What is the best cleaner for cat pee?**

Enzymatic cleaners specifically formulated for pet urine are generally considered the most effective. These cleaners use enzymes to break down the uric acid crystals and proteins, thus eliminating the odor at its source rather than just masking it.

**Can I use bleach to clean cat urine?**

No, you should never use bleach to clean cat urine. Mixing bleach with ammonia (which is present in urine) creates toxic chloramine gas, which is extremely dangerous to inhale and can cause severe respiratory issues. Bleach can also damage many surfaces and fabrics.

**How do I get cat pee smell out of a couch or mattress?**

For couches or mattresses, blot thoroughly, then saturate the affected area with an enzymatic cleaner, ensuring it penetrates as deeply as the urine did. Allow it to air dry completely, possibly repeating the process. For very deep saturation, professional upholstery cleaning may be required.

**How do I get cat pee smell out of hardwood floors?**

For hardwood floors, clean the surface thoroughly with an enzymatic cleaner, making sure to get into any cracks or seams where urine might have seeped. You may need to scrub gently with a soft brush. For deep stains or odors that have penetrated the wood, sanding and resealing might be necessary to fully eliminate the smell.

**How long does cat pee smell last?**

If not properly cleaned, cat pee smell can last indefinitely, especially in humid conditions or when the uric acid crystals are reactivated. With effective enzymatic cleaning that breaks down these crystals, the odor can be eliminated permanently within days.

**Will the smell ever truly go away?**

Yes, with the correct cleaning methods, particularly using high-quality enzymatic cleaners and addressing the source thoroughly, the cat pee smell can be completely and permanently eliminated. Persistence and proper application are key to success.
