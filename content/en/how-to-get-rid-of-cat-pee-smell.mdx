To get rid of cat pee smell, act quickly by blotting the area thoroughly, then treat it with an enzymatic cleaner to break down the uric acid crystals responsible for the odor. For fresh stains, a combination of baking soda and white vinegar can also help neutralize odors effectively. For a comprehensive approach to tackling this common problem, consult our [How to Get Rid of Cat Pee Smell: A Complete Guide](/en/how-to-get-rid-of-cat-pee-smell-guide).

## What You Need

- Paper towels or clean absorbent cloths
- Enzymatic cleaner for pet urine
- White vinegar
- Baking soda
- Spray bottle
- Gloves
- Stiff brush (for carpets)
- UV black light (optional, for locating old stains)
- Wet/dry vacuum or carpet cleaner (optional)

## Steps

### Step 1: Locate the Source of the Smell

Before you can eliminate the odor, you must pinpoint its exact location. For fresh stains, this is usually obvious, but old, dried stains can be harder to find. Use your nose to sniff out the strongest odor, or a UV black light in a darkened room to reveal dried urine, which will glow yellowish-green. Circle the affected areas with chalk or tape for easy reference.

### Step 2: Blot Up Fresh Urine Immediately

For fresh accidents, speed is crucial. Place several layers of paper towels or a clean, absorbent cloth over the wet area and press down firmly to soak up as much urine as possible. Do not rub, as this can spread the urine and push it deeper into fibers. Repeat this process with fresh towels until no more moisture transfers.

### Step 3: Apply an Enzymatic Cleaner

Enzymatic cleaners are essential for breaking down uric acid crystals, which are the main culprits behind the lingering cat pee smell. Saturate the stained area completely with the enzymatic cleaner, ensuring it penetrates as deeply as the urine did. Follow the product's instructions carefully for dwell time and application methods. This step is key to effectively and fast removing the odor, as detailed in our guide on [How to Get Rid of Cat Pee Smell Effectively & Fast](/en/how-to-get-rid-of-cat-pee-smell-fast).

### Step 4: Allow the Cleaner to Dwell and Dry

For the enzymatic cleaner to work its magic, it needs time. Allow the cleaner to dwell on the stain for the time recommended on the product label, which can range from 15 minutes to several hours. For deep stains, you might cover the area with plastic wrap to prevent the cleaner from drying out too quickly, allowing enzymes more time to break down the urine.

### Step 5: Rinse and Re-Treat as Needed

After the dwell time, blot up any excess cleaner. For carpets or upholstery, you might lightly rinse the area with clean water and then blot dry again. If the odor persists after the area is completely dry, you may need to repeat the enzymatic cleaning process. Stubborn smells often require multiple applications to fully eradicate.

### Step 6: Deodorize with Baking Soda (Optional)

Once the area is completely dry, sprinkle a generous amount of baking soda over the treated spot. Baking soda is a natural deodorizer that can help absorb any remaining faint odors. Let it sit for several hours, or even overnight, to maximize its effectiveness. Afterwards, vacuum up the baking soda thoroughly.

### Step 7: Address Stubborn or Old Stains

For particularly stubborn or old stains, you might need a more intensive approach. Consider using a wet/dry vacuum or carpet cleaner with the enzymatic solution to extract more deeply embedded urine. In some cases, professional cleaning may be necessary to fully eliminate the odor from deep within carpet padding or furniture. For persistent issues, refer to our guide on [How to Get Rid of Cat Pee Smell Permanently & Effectively](/en/how-to-get-rid-of-cat-pee-smell-permanently).


## Tips & Tricks

- Always test cleaning solutions in an inconspicuous area first to ensure they don't cause discoloration or damage.
- Ensure good ventilation while cleaning by opening windows or using fans.
- Address the underlying cause of your cat's inappropriate urination to prevent future accidents.
- Avoid using steam cleaners on cat urine, as the heat can permanently set the odor and stain.
- For porous surfaces like concrete, you may need to apply the enzymatic cleaner multiple times and allow it to penetrate deeply.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Never mix cleaning products, especially ammonia-based cleaners with bleach, as this can create dangerous toxic fumes.
- ⚠️ Always wear gloves when cleaning cat urine to protect your hands from bacteria and cleaning agents.
- ⚠️ Keep pets and children away from treated areas until they are completely dry and any cleaning residue has been removed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does cat pee smell so strong and linger?**

Cat pee contains uric acid crystals that don't dissolve easily in water and reactivate with moisture. It also contains pheromones and bacteria that contribute to its pungent, long-lasting odor, especially as it breaks down over time.

**What is the best type of product to get rid of cat pee smell?**

Enzymatic cleaners specifically designed for pet urine are generally the most effective. These cleaners contain beneficial bacteria and enzymes that break down the uric acid crystals and other odor-causing components in cat urine, eliminating the smell at its source.

**Can I use bleach to clean cat urine?**

No, you should never use bleach to clean cat urine. Bleach can react with ammonia in urine to create toxic fumes. Additionally, bleach can damage surfaces, and its strong odor might even encourage your cat to mark the same spot again.

**How do I get cat pee smell out of a couch or mattress?**

For couches and mattresses, follow the same steps: blot thoroughly, then saturate the area with an enzymatic cleaner, ensuring it penetrates as deeply as the urine did. Allow it to air dry completely, potentially using fans to speed up the process. Multiple treatments might be necessary.

**Does white vinegar effectively remove cat pee smell?**

White vinegar can help neutralize the alkaline salts in cat urine and act as a deodorizer due to its acidity. However, it doesn't break down uric acid crystals like enzymatic cleaners do. It can be a good interim solution or used in conjunction with enzymatic cleaners.

**How can I prevent my cat from peeing outside the litter box?**

Address the root cause by consulting a vet to rule out medical issues. Ensure the litter box is clean, appealing (type of litter, size, location), and consider adding more litter boxes if you have multiple cats or a large home. Reducing stress in the cat's environment can also help.

**Is it possible to completely get rid of old, dried cat pee smell?**

Yes, it is possible, but it requires patience and consistent application of enzymatic cleaners. Old stains often require repeated treatments to fully break down all the embedded uric acid crystals. For very deep or extensive old stains, professional cleaning might be the most effective solution.
