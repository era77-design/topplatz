To fix a clogged toilet, first stop flushing to prevent overflow. Then, you can use a plunger for minor clogs or a combination of dish soap and hot water for more stubborn blockages before resorting to a toilet auger.

## What You Need

- Rubber gloves
- Old towels or newspaper
- Toilet plunger (flange-style)
- Liquid dish soap
- Hot water (not boiling)
- Bucket
- Toilet auger (closet snake) (optional)
- Protective eyewear (optional)
- Baking soda and vinegar (optional alternative)

## Steps

### Step 1: Assess and Prepare

Do not flush the toilet again, as this is crucial to prevent the bowl from overflowing. Lay down old towels or newspaper around the base of the toilet to catch any potential spills. For hygiene, put on a pair of rubber gloves before you begin.

### Step 2: Plunge the Clog

Position your toilet plunger firmly over the drain hole, ensuring you create a tight seal. Plunge vigorously with an up-and-down motion for 15-20 seconds, maintaining the seal throughout the process. On the last stroke, pull the plunger up sharply to break the vacuum and dislodge the clog. Repeat this process if the clog persists.

### Step 3: Try Dish Soap and Hot Water (No Plunger Method)

If you don't have a plunger or if plunging didn't work, pour about half a cup of liquid dish soap into the toilet bowl. Follow this by slowly pouring a gallon of hot (but not boiling) water from a bucket into the bowl. Allow this mixture to sit for 20-30 minutes; the dish soap helps lubricate the clog, and the hot water assists in breaking it down.

### Step 4: Use a Toilet Auger (Snake)

For more stubborn clogs, especially those located further down the drain, a toilet auger (also known as a closet snake) is highly effective. Carefully insert the auger's coiled cable into the drain opening, feeding it slowly while turning the handle. Once you feel resistance, you've reached the clog; rotate and push the auger through to break it apart or hook onto it and pull it out.

### Step 5: Flush and Clean Up

Once you believe the clog is clear, carefully flush the toilet to check if the water drains normally. If it does, the clog is gone; flush a couple more times to ensure the drain is completely clear. Afterward, thoroughly clean your plunger, auger, and any spilled water or debris with a disinfectant to maintain hygiene.

### Step 6: Consider Chemical Drain Cleaners (with Caution)

As a last resort before contacting a professional, you might consider using a toilet-safe chemical drain cleaner. Always follow the product instructions precisely and ensure the bathroom is well-ventilated. Be aware that these chemicals can be harsh on plumbing pipes and the environment, so use them sparingly and with care.


## Tips & Tricks

- Never flush anything other than toilet paper and human waste to prevent future clogs.
- Keep a flange-style toilet plunger readily accessible in every bathroom for quick clog resolution.
- For persistent or recurring clogs, there might be a larger underlying issue with your plumbing system that requires professional attention.
- Regularly clean your toilet bowl to prevent mineral buildup that can contribute to blockages over time.
- Consider using an enzymatic cleaner periodically to help break down organic matter in your pipes naturally.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Never mix different chemical drain cleaners, as this can create dangerous fumes or hazardous chemical reactions.
- ⚠️ Avoid using excessive force with a toilet auger, as it can scratch or cause damage to the porcelain bowl.
- ⚠️ Do not pour boiling water into your toilet, as the sudden temperature change can cause the ceramic bowl to crack.
- ⚠️ Always wear rubber gloves and consider protective eyewear when dealing with toilet clogs or using harsh chemicals.
- ⚠️ If water is still rising in the bowl after attempting to clear a clog, do not flush again to prevent an overflow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do you fix a clogged toilet without a plunger?**

You can often fix a clogged toilet without a plunger by using a combination of dish soap and hot water. Pour about half a cup of dish soap into the bowl, followed by a gallon of hot (not boiling) water, and let it sit for 20-30 minutes to help lubricate and break down the clog.

**How do you fix a clogged toilet with poop?**

To fix a clogged toilet with poop, start by using a plunger vigorously. If that doesn't work, try the dish soap and hot water method. For stubborn clogs, a toilet auger can effectively break up or retrieve the solid waste causing the blockage.

**What causes a toilet to clog easily?**

Toilets often clog easily due to flushing non-flushable items like wipes, excessive toilet paper, feminine hygiene products, or paper towels. Sometimes, a weak flush, old plumbing, or a partial blockage further down the drain line can also be the cause.

**When should I call a professional plumber for a clogged toilet?**

You should call a professional plumber if your toilet remains clogged after multiple attempts with a plunger, dish soap, or a toilet auger. Also, if multiple drains in your home are slow or clogged, it could indicate a more serious main sewer line issue requiring expert attention.

**Are chemical drain cleaners safe for toilets?**

While some chemical drain cleaners are formulated for toilets, they should be used with caution and as a last resort. They can be harsh on pipes, especially older ones, and are not always effective against all types of clogs. Always follow product instructions carefully and ensure good ventilation.

**How can I prevent my toilet from clogging in the future?**

To prevent future clogs, only flush human waste and toilet paper. Avoid flushing "flushable" wipes, paper towels, cotton balls, or hygiene products. Consider using less toilet paper per flush and educating household members on proper toilet usage.

**Can baking soda and vinegar unclog a toilet?**

Yes, baking soda and vinegar can sometimes help with minor clogs. Pour one cup of baking soda into the toilet bowl, followed by two cups of white vinegar. Let the mixture fizz and work for at least 30 minutes (or even overnight) before flushing with hot water.
