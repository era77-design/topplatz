To fix a clogged toilet, first stop the water flow to prevent overflow. Then, use a plunger with strong, consistent thrusts to dislodge the blockage, or try a homemade solution of hot water and dish soap. For stubborn clogs, a toilet auger can be an effective tool. ## What You Need

- Flange plunger
- Rubber gloves
- Old towels or newspaper
- Bucket (optional, for scooping water)
- Liquid dish soap
- Hot tap water (not boiling)
- Toilet auger/snake (optional, for stubborn clogs)
- Disinfectant cleaner

## Steps

### Step 1: Stop the Water Flow

The immediate concern with a clogged toilet is preventing an overflow. If the water level in the bowl is rising, quickly lift the tank lid and push down the flapper valve, or turn the shut-off valve located behind the toilet counter-clockwise. This vital first step will stop the water from filling the bowl further and potentially spilling onto your floor.

### Step 2: Assess and Protect the Area

Before you begin, take a moment to assess the water level in the bowl. If it's too high, scoop some out into a bucket to avoid splashing during the unclogging process. Lay down old towels or newspapers around the base of the toilet to protect your floor from any spills or splashes. Don your rubber gloves for hygiene and protection.

### Step 3: Plunge with Purpose

A flange plunger is ideal for toilets as its extended rubber flap creates a better seal than a standard cup plunger. Position the plunger over the drain hole, ensuring it completely covers the opening. Push down gently to release any trapped air, then plunge vigorously with strong, consistent up-and-down thrusts for 15-20 seconds. Break the seal quickly on the final pull to create suction and dislodge the clog. Repeat if necessary.

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure there's enough water in the bowl to cover the plunger's cup for effective suction.

### Step 4: Try Dish Soap and Hot Water

If a plunger isn't available or hasn't worked, this method can often loosen clogs. Pour about half a cup of liquid dish soap directly into the toilet bowl. Follow this by slowly pouring a gallon of hot (not boiling) tap water into the bowl. Let it sit for 20-30 minutes; the soap acts as a lubricant, and the hot water helps break down the blockage. After waiting, try flushing. This method is particularly useful if you need to know [How to Fix a Clogged Toilet: Easy Steps & Solutions](/en/how-to-fix-a-clogged-toilet-easy-steps) without relying solely on a plunger.

### Step 5: Use a Toilet Auger (for Stubborn Clogs)

For persistent clogs that resist plunging and the soap method, a toilet auger (also known as a closet auger or snake) is your next best tool. Gently insert the auger's coiled cable into the toilet drain, feeding it until you feel resistance. Crank the handle to extend the cable and break up the obstruction. Once the clog is cleared, retract the auger and flush the toilet.

### Step 6: Flush and Clean Up

Once you believe the clog is clear, flush the toilet to confirm. If the water drains normally, you've succeeded. Thoroughly clean your plunger, auger, and any tools used with disinfectant, as they have been in contact with waste. Dispose of the soiled towels and newspapers, and clean the floor around the toilet to maintain a hygienic bathroom environment.


## Tips & Tricks

- Always use a flange plunger for toilets; its design creates a superior seal for effective unclogging.
- Avoid flushing anything other than human waste and toilet paper to prevent future clogs.
- If the clog is particularly stubborn or if multiple drains are affected, it might indicate a deeper plumbing issue requiring a professional plumber.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Never use boiling water to unclog a toilet, as the extreme temperature can crack the porcelain bowl.
- ⚠️ Avoid harsh chemical drain cleaners, which can damage pipes, harm the environment, and pose health risks.
- ⚠️ Exercise caution when using a toilet auger to prevent scratching or damaging the toilet's porcelain finish.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I fix a clogged toilet without a plunger?**

You can often fix a clogged toilet without a plunger by using a combination of dish soap and hot water. Pour about half a cup of dish soap into the bowl, followed by a gallon of hot (not boiling) water, and let it sit for 20-30 minutes before attempting to flush. Alternatively, a toilet auger can effectively clear deeper clogs.

**What should I do if my toilet is clogged with poop?**

If your toilet is clogged with poop, first try plunging vigorously with a flange plunger to break up and dislodge the material. If plunging isn't effective, the dish soap and hot water method can help lubricate and dissolve the waste. For stubborn blockages, a toilet auger is usually very effective at clearing organic matter.

**Can I use boiling water to unclog a toilet?**

No, you should never use boiling water to unclog a toilet. The sudden extreme temperature change can crack the porcelain of your toilet bowl, leading to costly damage and potential leaks. Always use hot tap water, not boiling, if attempting the hot water and dish soap method.

**How do I prevent my toilet from clogging in the future?**

To prevent future clogs, only flush human waste and toilet paper. Avoid flushing items like wipes (even "flushable" ones), feminine hygiene products, cotton balls, or excessive amounts of toilet paper. Consider flushing twice for larger flushes to ensure everything clears the trap effectively.

**When should I call a plumber for a clogged toilet?**

You should call a plumber if you've tried all DIY methods (plunging, soap/water, auger) and the toilet remains clogged. Also, if multiple toilets or drains in your home are backing up, it could indicate a more serious main sewer line issue requiring professional attention.

**What kind of plunger is best for toilets?**

The best type of plunger for toilets is a flange plunger. Unlike a cup plunger, a flange plunger has an extended rubber flap that creates a much tighter seal over the toilet's drain hole, allowing for more effective suction and pressure to clear clogs, making it significantly more efficient.
