To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, select the main checkbox at the top left, and then click the "Mark as read" icon (an open envelope). This action quickly clears unread notifications, helping you efficiently manage a cluttered inbox.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Begin by logging in to your Gmail account using a web browser on your computer or the official Gmail app on your mobile device. Ensure you are signed into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read. If you're new to Gmail and haven't set up an account yet, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Navigate to Your Desired Inbox View

Once logged in, you'll typically land in your Primary inbox. If you want to mark emails as read in other categories like Social, Promotions, or specific labels you've created, click on those tabs or labels in the left sidebar to access them. This allows you to target specific sections of your email.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails on the Page

Locate the small checkbox at the very top of your email list, positioned just above the first email in your current view. Click this checkbox to select all conversations currently displayed on that particular page of your inbox. This is your first step towards a bulk selection.

### Step 4: Select *All* Conversations (If Applicable)

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear at the top, such as 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' If you have more than 50 unread emails, a crucial link will also appear: 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to expand your selection to *every* unread email in your current inbox view or label.

### Step 5: Mark the Selected Emails as Read

With all your desired emails now selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your inbox. Click the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. This action will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read, removing the bold formatting and unread count.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Gmail will process your request, which usually takes only a moment. A small notification will briefly appear at the bottom left of your screen confirming 'Conversations marked as read.' Your inbox should now reflect the change, with all previously selected emails no longer appearing bold or contributing to your unread count.

### Step 7: Repeat for Other Categories or Labels

If you have unread emails in other tabs (e.g., Social, Promotions) or custom labels within your Gmail account, you will need to repeat steps 2-6 for each category or label. This ensures that all emails across your entire Gmail account are marked as read, helping you maintain a truly clean and organized inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use the search bar: Type `is:unread` in the Gmail search bar to quickly see all unread emails across your entire account, regardless of category or label.
- Undo accidental actions: If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' button that briefly appears at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Enable keyboard shortcuts in Gmail settings (Settings > See all settings > Keyboard shortcuts) to use `shift + i` to mark selected emails as read even faster.
- Mobile app: The process is similar on the Gmail mobile app: select emails by tapping their sender icon, then use the three-dot menu or an icon at the top to mark as read.
- Filter for specific senders: If you only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read, search for `from:sender@example.com is:unread`, then select all and mark as read.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking a large volume of emails as read cannot be easily undone without immediately clicking the 'Undo' button that appears. Be sure you want to mark *all* selected emails as read before confirming.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the mobile app. Tap the sender icon next to the emails you want to select, then use the 'Mark as read' option, usually found in the three-dot menu or as an icon at the top of the screen.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

You can achieve this using the Gmail search bar. Type `from:sender@example.com is:unread` (replacing with the actual email address), then select all the results and click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Will marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete or archive them; they will remain in your inbox or their respective labels, just without the unread indicator.

**How do I find all unread emails after marking some as read?**

You can always use the search operator `is:unread` in the Gmail search bar. This will display all remaining unread emails across your entire account, regardless of their category or label.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users do this to achieve 'Inbox Zero,' clear out old unread notifications that have accumulated, or simply get a fresh start, allowing them to focus only on new, incoming emails without distraction.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Yes, you can set up filters in Gmail to automatically mark incoming emails from specific senders or with certain keywords as read. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses to create new filters for this purpose.

**What if I accidentally mark emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button appears at the bottom left of the screen. Click it quickly to reverse the action. If you miss this brief window, there isn't a simple 'mark all as unread' button for a large selection.
