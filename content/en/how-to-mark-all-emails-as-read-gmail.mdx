To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you need to first select all emails visible on your current page, then use the option to select all conversations across all pages. Once selected, click the 'Mark as read' icon. This simple process helps you quickly clear your inbox of unread messages, making it easier to focus on new or important communications.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Enter your Gmail email address or phone number and your password to sign in. If you're already logged in, you will be directed straight to your inbox. Ensure you're viewing the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail profiles.

### Step 2: Select All Emails on the Current Page

In your Gmail inbox, locate the checkbox at the top left, just above your list of emails. Click this checkbox. This action will select all the emails currently visible on that specific page of your inbox. You'll see a notification indicating how many conversations are selected.

### Step 3: Expand Selection to All Conversations

After selecting the emails on the current page, a banner will appear at the top of your inbox. This banner typically says something like 'All X conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also offer an option: 'Select all Y conversations in [Primary/Inbox].' Click on this link to extend your selection to every email in your chosen folder, not just those on the first page.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all conversations are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click on this icon. Gmail will then begin processing your request to mark all the selected emails as read. This might take a moment if you have a very large number of emails.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

A confirmation pop-up or message will usually appear, asking if you want to confirm marking all conversations as read. Confirm this action to proceed. Gmail will then apply the 'read' status to all the selected messages. You may see a loading indicator while the changes are being applied across your inbox.

### Step 6: Check Other Categories or Labels

If you use Gmail's category tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums), you will need to repeat this process for each category separately if you wish to mark all emails in them as read. Navigate to each tab, select all conversations, and then mark them as read. The same applies to any custom labels or folders you might have.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly find unread emails after marking all as read, use the search bar at the top of Gmail and type 'is:unread' to filter your view.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you maintain inbox zero, making it easier to spot new and important messages as they arrive.
- Consider setting up filters for incoming emails to automatically mark certain types of messages as read or move them to specific folders, further streamlining your inbox management.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark emails as read on my phone using the Gmail app?**

Yes, the Gmail app on both Android and iOS devices allows you to mark multiple emails as read. You typically long-press on one email to select it, then tap on others to select more, and finally use the 'Mark as read' option from the top menu.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

Don't worry, you can easily undo this action. Immediately after marking emails as read, look for the 'Undo' notification that appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click 'Undo' to revert the change. If you miss the undo option, you can search for the email and manually mark it as unread again.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read.' It does not delete them or move them to the trash. Your emails will remain in your inbox or their respective folders unless you explicitly choose to delete or archive them.

**Can I mark emails as read in specific categories only?**

Yes, you can. When you click on a specific category tab (e.g., 'Promotions' or 'Social'), the 'Select all conversations' option will apply only to that particular category. This allows you to manage different sections of your inbox independently.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is often done to achieve 'inbox zero' or to clear out old, accumulated unread messages that are no longer relevant. It helps declutter your inbox and provides a fresh starting point for managing new incoming mail effectively.

**What if I don't have a Gmail account yet?**

If you don't have a Gmail account, you'll need to create one before you can manage emails within the platform. The process is straightforward and free. You can learn how to set one up by following guides like [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

**How do I mark specific emails as unread after doing this?**

To mark specific emails as unread, simply find the email(s) you want to change, select them by clicking the checkbox next to them, and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (which often looks like a closed envelope) in the toolbar above your emails.
