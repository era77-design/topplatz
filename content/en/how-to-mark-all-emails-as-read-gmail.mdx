To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first log in to your account through a web browser. Use the top-left checkbox to select all emails on the current page, then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link to extend the selection. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Internet access
- Web browser or Gmail app

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Enter your Gmail email address or phone number and password to log in. Ensure you are viewing your primary inbox or the specific category you wish to clear.

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

Locate the small square checkbox at the top-left of your inbox, positioned directly above your list of emails. Click this checkbox once. This action will select all the emails currently displayed on the page you are viewing (typically 50 emails).

### Step 3: Select All Matching Conversations

After selecting emails on the current page, a message will appear above your email list. It usually states, 'All X conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately next to this, you will see a crucial link that reads, 'Select all Y conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection beyond the current page, encompassing all emails in your current view (e.g., all emails in your Primary tab).

### Step 4: Click 'Mark as Read'

With all your desired emails now selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appears above your inbox. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click it to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

A brief notification will appear at the bottom-left of your screen, confirming that 'Conversations marked as read.' Depending on the total number of emails, this process might take a few moments to complete. Your unread email count should now be significantly reduced or cleared.

### Step 6: Use Search Filters for Targeted Marking (Optional)

If you only wish to mark specific types of emails as read (e.g., only 'Promotions' or emails from a particular sender), utilize the Gmail search bar at the top. Type in your desired filter (e.g., `category:promotions` or `from:example.com`). Once the filtered results appear, follow steps 2-5 to select and mark only those specific emails as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly undo the 'Mark as read' action, look for the 'Undo' button that briefly appears in the notification bar at the bottom-left of your screen right after the action is completed. Click it before it disappears.
- If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, simply click the individual checkbox next to each email you want to select, then click the 'Mark as read' icon.
- Regularly marking emails as read, especially from categories like Promotions or Social, can help you keep your inbox tidy without deleting important messages.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I undo marking emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button will appear in the notification bar at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it within a few seconds to revert the action.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status. It does not delete them from your inbox. The emails will still be accessible, just without the 'unread' highlight.

**How do I mark only certain emails as read?**

You can use Gmail's search filters to narrow down your selection. For instance, search for `is:unread from:sender@example.com` or `category:updates`. Then, follow the steps to select all matching conversations and mark them as read.

**Why would someone want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to achieve 'inbox zero,' declutter their inbox, or clear a large backlog of old unread messages. It's particularly useful for new accounts that receive many initial system emails. If you've just created a new account, you might find our guide on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) helpful for future setup.

**Can I do this on the Gmail mobile app?**

The Gmail mobile app allows you to mark multiple emails as read by long-pressing on one email, then tapping other emails to select them. However, there isn't a direct 'select all conversations' button for your entire inbox like on the desktop version.

**What if I don't see the 'Select all conversations' option?**

Ensure you have clicked the main 'select all' checkbox above your emails first. The 'Select all conversations that match this search' link only appears *after* you've selected the emails on the current page.

**Does this affect emails in other folders like 'Sent' or 'Drafts'?**

No, marking emails as read using this method only affects emails in the specific view or label you are currently in (e.g., your Primary inbox, Promotions tab, or a custom label). It does not impact emails in 'Sent,' 'Drafts,' or 'Spam' folders.
