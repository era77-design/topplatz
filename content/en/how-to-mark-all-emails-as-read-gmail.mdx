To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first open your inbox in a web browser. Then, click the 'Select all' checkbox at the top of your email list. If you have more than 50 emails, a banner will appear allowing you to select all conversations in your current view. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the toolbar.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Computer with internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Navigate to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser. Ensure you are logged into the Gmail account you wish to manage. This method is most effective on a desktop browser due to the selection options available.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox View

Decide which emails you want to mark as read. You can go to your 'Primary', 'Social', or 'Promotions' tab. Alternatively, use the search bar to filter emails by specific criteria, such as 'is:unread' to only show unread messages, or search for emails from a particular sender or with a specific label.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on your screen, which typically amounts to 50 conversations per page.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations in the Current View

If you have more than 50 emails in your current view (e.g., in your 'Primary' tab or search results), a yellow banner will appear at the top of your screen. This banner will state, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all [number] conversations in [current view]'. Click the second part of this message to select every single email in that specific view, not just the ones on the current page.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your emails. Find the 'Mark as read' icon, which looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action will apply to all emails you've selected, whether on the current page or across the entire view.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action and Verify

After clicking 'Mark as read', a small notification will briefly appear at the bottom-left of your screen, confirming that the emails have been marked as read. You can then observe your inbox to see that the selected emails no longer appear in bold, indicating their new read status. If you want to mark emails in other tabs as read, repeat these steps for each tab.

### Step 7: Undo if Necessary

If you accidentally marked emails as read or wish to revert the action, look for the 'Undo' button in the notification that appears immediately after marking emails as read. This undo option is only available for a few seconds. If you miss it, you can manually select specific emails and click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) to change their status back.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark only unread emails as read, type 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar, then follow the steps to select all and mark as read.
- Marking emails as read does not delete them; they will remain in your inbox or their respective folders.
- This process works for all main Gmail categories: Primary, Social, Promotions, and Updates.
- Consider setting up filters for certain types of emails to automatically mark them as read upon arrival, or to move them to specific labels.
- On the Gmail mobile app, you can select multiple emails by long-pressing one, then tapping others. Then, tap the three-dot menu and choose 'Mark as read'.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark only unread emails as read?**

Yes, you can. Simply type 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar. This will filter your inbox to show only unread emails, allowing you to select them all and mark them as read.

**Does marking emails as read delete them from my inbox?**

No, marking emails as read does not delete them. It simply changes their status from unread to read. They will remain in your inbox or any labels they are assigned to, and you can still access them later.

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, but the process is slightly different. On the mobile app, you typically need to long-press on one email to select it, then tap other emails to select them individually or in groups. There isn't a direct 'select all conversations in this view' button like on the desktop.

**What if I accidentally mark emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button will appear in a notification at the bottom-left of your screen. Click this 'Undo' button quickly to revert the action. If you miss the undo window, you can manually select the emails and click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).

**How do I mark emails as unread again?**

To mark emails as unread, select the desired emails by clicking their checkboxes. Then, from the toolbar above your email list, click the 'Mark as unread' icon, which typically looks like a closed envelope.

**Does this method work for all my Gmail tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions)?**

Yes, this method works for all your Gmail tabs. You simply need to navigate to each tab (e.g., 'Social' or 'Promotions'), and then follow the steps to select all emails in that specific tab and mark them as read.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to declutter their inbox, achieve 'inbox zero,' or simply to clear notifications. It can help focus on newly arriving emails or provide a fresh start for managing your correspondence.
