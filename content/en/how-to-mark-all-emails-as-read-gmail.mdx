To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox in a web browser, select the checkbox at the top-left of your email list, and then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the toolbar that appears.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari) and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in using your Google account credentials if you aren't already signed in. Ensure you are viewing the inbox or specific label you wish to manage.

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

In your Gmail interface, locate the small checkbox positioned at the top-left corner of your email list, directly above the first email. Clicking this checkbox will instantly select all emails currently visible on that page, which typically defaults to 50 conversations.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations Across Pages

If you have more than 50 emails and intend to mark *all* of them as read, a message will appear above your emails after you click the initial checkbox. This message will state something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately next to this, you'll see a clickable link: 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection to every email in your current view or search results.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once your desired emails are selected, a new toolbar will appear above your email list. Look for the 'Mark as read' icon within this toolbar. It typically resembles an open envelope, sometimes with a checkmark or a line through it. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action (If Prompted)

For very large batches of emails, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up to ensure you want to proceed. This message might ask 'Are you sure you want to mark all conversations in this search as read?' or a similar query. Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to finalize the action. The process may take a few moments depending on the total number of emails being updated.

### Step 6: Use Search Filters for Targeted Marking (Optional)

If your goal is to mark only specific emails as read, rather than your entire inbox, leverage Gmail's powerful search bar. For instance, type `is:unread from:sender@example.com` to find all unread emails from a particular sender, or `is:unread older_than:30d` for unread emails older than 30 days. After applying your filter, follow steps 2-5 to select and mark only those filtered results as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- Regularly marking emails as read can help you maintain an organized inbox and focus on new, unread messages.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, you can quickly reverse the action by selecting them and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) in the toolbar.
- Consider using Gmail's 'Categories' (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) to automatically sort incoming mail, which can make managing unread messages easier.
- For an even cleaner inbox, explore archiving or deleting emails you no longer need after marking them as read.
- If you're new to Gmail and haven't set up an account yet, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking a large number of emails as read is irreversible in a single 'undo' click. While you can mark them unread again, it might be tedious to find specific ones if you act too quickly.
- ⚠️ Ensure you are logged into the correct Gmail account before performing this action, especially if you manage multiple accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read can help declutter your inbox, reduce notification fatigue, and allow you to start fresh, focusing only on new incoming messages. It's often used after returning from a vacation or after a period of intense email accumulation.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, you can undo this action immediately after performing it by clicking the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom-left of your screen. If you miss the undo option, you would need to manually select emails and mark them as unread again.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read; it does not delete them. The emails will remain in your inbox, archive, or any labels they were assigned to.

**How do I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, you can select multiple emails by tapping and holding one email, then tapping other emails to select them. Once selected, tap the three-dot menu (or the envelope icon) and choose 'Mark as read'. There isn't a single 'select all' button for the entire inbox like on the desktop version.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

You can use Gmail's search bar to filter emails from a specific sender (e.g., `from:example@domain.com`). Once the search results appear, you can then use the 'select all' checkbox and the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link to mark only those specific emails as read.

**Will marking emails as read affect archived emails?**

This process primarily affects emails in your current view (e.g., inbox, a specific label, or search results). If archived emails are included in your search results (e.g., if you search 'all mail'), then they can also be marked as read. Otherwise, archived emails are typically already considered read.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read upon arrival?**

Yes, you can set up a filter in Gmail to automatically mark certain incoming emails as read. Go to Gmail Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses, then 'Create a new filter' based on sender, subject, or other criteria, and select the 'Mark as read' action.
