To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, click the 'Select all' checkbox, and then choose 'Mark as read' from the toolbar. For emails spanning multiple pages, click the additional link that appears to select all conversations matching the current view before marking them.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Your Gmail Inbox

Navigate to mail.google.com in your web browser or open the Gmail app on your mobile device. Ensure you are logged into the Gmail account you wish to manage. If you need to set up a new account, you can find guides like [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

In your Gmail inbox, locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner, just above your list of emails. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on the first page of your inbox.

### Step 3: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once emails are selected, a new toolbar will appear above your inbox. Look for the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope or a small 'eye' symbol. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations (If Applicable)

If you have more than 50 emails (the default number displayed per page), after clicking the initial 'Select all' checkbox, a message will appear at the top of your inbox stating, "All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all [number] conversations in [folder/label]." Click the second part of this message (e.g., "Select all 1,234 conversations in Inbox") to select *all* emails in your current view, not just those on the first page.

### Step 5: Apply the 'Mark as Read' Action to All

After selecting all conversations across all pages (as described in Step 4), the 'Mark as read' icon will still be present in the toolbar. Click this icon again. A confirmation pop-up may appear asking if you want to apply this action to all selected conversations. Confirm to proceed.

### Step 6: Use Search Operators for Specific Unread Emails (Optional)

If you only want to mark specific unread emails as read (e.g., only those in a certain category or from a particular sender), you can use Gmail's search bar. Type `is:unread` to display all unread emails. You can combine this with other operators like `is:unread in:promotions` or `is:unread from:sender@example.com`.

### Step 7: Mark Searched Unread Emails as Read

After performing your search (e.g., `is:unread`), follow steps 2-5 again. Click the 'Select all' checkbox, then the "Select all conversations matching this search" link, and finally the 'Mark as read' icon. This allows for more targeted management of your unread messages.


## Tips & Tricks

- You can undo the 'Mark as read' action immediately after performing it by clicking the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom left of your screen.
- Consider using Gmail's filtering system to automatically mark certain incoming emails as read or categorize them into different folders to better manage your inbox.
- Regularly archiving or deleting emails you no longer need can also help reduce inbox clutter and make managing unread messages easier.
- On mobile, you can often swipe right or left on an email in the list to quickly mark it as read or unread, depending on your settings.
- Use the 'Mark as unread' option if you accidentally mark an important email as read and want to revisit it later.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when performing bulk actions like 'Mark as read' or 'Delete' to ensure you don't accidentally miss important information or remove emails you might need later.
- ⚠️ Applying 'Mark as read' to thousands of emails at once might take a moment to process, especially on slower internet connections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read in the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar. In the Gmail app, tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines), go to your desired folder (e.g., Inbox), then tap and hold an email to select it. Once one is selected, you can tap the checkbox at the top to select all visible, then tap the three dots menu and choose 'Mark as read'.

**What happens if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

If you accidentally mark an important email as read, you can quickly undo the action. Immediately after marking them, a small 'Undo' notification appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click it, or find the email and manually mark it as unread using the open envelope icon.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, archive them, or move them from your inbox. They will remain in their current folder or label.

**How do I find all my unread emails in Gmail?**

To find all your unread emails, simply type `is:unread` into the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface and press Enter. This will display a list of all emails that are currently marked as unread across all your folders.

**Can I mark emails as read only from a specific sender?**

Yes, you can. Use the search bar to filter emails from a specific sender (e.g., `from:example@domain.com is:unread`). Once the search results appear, select all the emails and then click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Why do I still have unread emails after using the 'Mark all as read' option?**

This usually happens if you didn't select *all* conversations across *all pages*. After clicking the initial 'Select all' checkbox, make sure to also click the "Select all [number] conversations in [folder/label]" link that appears to truly select every email in that view before marking them as read.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Yes, you can create filters in Gmail to automatically mark incoming emails from specific senders or with certain keywords as read. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses to set up a new filter.
