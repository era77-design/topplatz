To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the search bar to find all unread messages, then select them all and choose the 'Mark as read' option. This process allows you to quickly clear your unread count and manage your inbox more effectively. It's a great way to achieve inbox zero without individually opening every email.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device with internet access
- Your Gmail account login credentials

## Steps

### Step 1: Log in to Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com, or launch the Gmail app on your mobile device. Enter your email address and password to log in. If you don't have an account, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure you are logging into the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail addresses.

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail inbox, type `is:unread` and press Enter or click the search icon. This action will display all emails that are currently marked as unread in your inbox and other folders.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can combine this with other search operators, like `is:unread in:inbox`, to refine your search to specific locations.

### Step 3: Select All Displayed Emails

Once the search results appear, look for the checkbox located at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all the emails currently visible on that page.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you have more than 50 unread emails, Gmail typically displays them in pages. This initial selection only covers the current view.

### Step 4: Select All Matching Conversations (Important!)

After selecting the current page's emails, a message will appear above your email list stating, "All X conversations on this page are selected." Crucially, click on the text that says, "Select all X conversations that match this search." This will ensure you mark *every* unread email as read, regardless of how many pages there are.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is vital for clearing all unread emails across your entire account, not just those visible on the first page of results.

### Step 5: Mark Emails as Read

With all matching unread conversations selected, locate the "Mark as read" icon (an open envelope) in the toolbar above your emails. If you don't see it directly, click the "More" menu (often represented by three vertical dots) and then select "Mark as read" from the dropdown menu.

> 💡 **Tip:** The open envelope icon is a direct shortcut for marking emails as read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Gmail will process your request. You should see a confirmation message, such as "X conversations marked as read," at the bottom left of your screen. Your inbox will now reflect the change, and the unread count should decrease or disappear.

> 💡 **Tip:** Refresh your browser or app if the changes aren't immediately visible.


## Tips & Tricks

- Regularly archive or delete emails you no longer need to keep your inbox manageable.
- Utilize Gmail's categories (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) to automatically sort incoming mail and reduce clutter.
- Consider setting up filters to automatically mark certain types of emails as read or move them to specific folders upon arrival.
- For mobile users, the steps are similar; look for the search icon and the three-dot menu for options.
- If you accidentally mark an email as read, you can quickly find it by searching `is:read` and then selecting it to 'Mark as unread'.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be careful when selecting 'Select all X conversations that match this search,' as this action cannot be easily undone for a large number of emails. While you can mark them as unread again, it might be time-consuming.
- ⚠️ Ensure you are logged into the correct Gmail account before performing this action, especially if you manage multiple accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking them as read, you can often click the "Undo" button that appears briefly at the bottom of the screen. Alternatively, you can search for `is:read` and then select the relevant emails to mark them as unread again, though this might be more time-consuming for a large batch.

**Will marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete or archive them. Your emails will remain in their respective folders, just without the unread indicator.

**Does this work on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app for both Android and iOS. You'll use the search bar to find `is:unread`, then select all, and look for the "Mark as read" option, often under a three-dot menu or an envelope icon.

**How do I mark only emails in a specific folder or label as read?**

To mark emails in a specific folder (label) as read, navigate to that label first by clicking on it in the left sidebar. Then, use the `is:unread` search operator within that label to filter, and proceed with selecting all and marking them as read.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is useful for decluttering your inbox, achieving "inbox zero," or when you return from vacation with a large backlog of unread messages that you've already reviewed elsewhere or deemed non-urgent. It helps you focus on truly new incoming mail.

**What if I have more than 50 unread emails?**

If you have more than 50 unread emails, after clicking the initial checkbox to select the first page, a message will appear asking if you want to "Select all X conversations that match this search." Clicking this text is crucial to select all unread emails across all pages.

**Can I mark only emails from a specific sender as read?**

Yes, you can combine search operators. For example, search `from:sender@example.com is:unread` to find all unread emails from a particular sender. Then, follow the steps to select all and mark them as read.
