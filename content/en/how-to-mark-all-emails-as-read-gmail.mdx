To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the desktop interface to select all conversations and apply the 'Mark as read' action. This is particularly useful for clearing out a cluttered inbox or after returning from a long break. The process is quick and helps you achieve a more organized email experience.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Begin by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on a computer. Make sure you are signed into the Gmail account you wish to manage. If you need assistance setting up your account, you can find help with [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account). Once logged in, your primary inbox will be displayed, showing both read and unread messages.

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure you're logged into the correct Gmail account before proceeding to avoid marking emails in the wrong inbox.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails on the Page

In your Gmail inbox, locate the small checkbox positioned at the top left, just above your list of emails. Clicking this checkbox will instantly select all the emails currently visible on the page (typically 50 conversations). You'll notice a banner appearing above your emails indicating how many conversations are selected, for example, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.'

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in Your Current View

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear at the top of your inbox, often saying 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, next to this, there will be an option to 'Select all [number] conversations in Primary' (or whichever label you are viewing). Click on this link to extend your selection to *all* emails in your current view, not just those on the first page. This ensures you're marking everything as read across your entire inbox or selected folder.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all desired conversations selected, look for the 'More' actions button, which typically looks like three vertical dots ( ⋮ ) or sometimes an open book icon, located in the toolbar above your emails. Click this button to reveal a dropdown menu. From this menu, choose the 'Mark as read' option. This action will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action and Update Inbox

Gmail will process your request, and you might see a brief notification confirming that the conversations have been marked as read. The bold formatting of unread emails will disappear, indicating their new status. Depending on the number of emails, this might take a moment to fully update across your inbox. You can then navigate to other labels or categories to repeat the process if needed.

### Step 6: Mark All Emails as Read Using the Gmail Mobile App

If you're using the Gmail app on your smartphone or tablet, the process is slightly different. Open the Gmail app, then tap and hold on the first email you want to mark as read. This will select it and bring up an action bar at the top. Continue tapping on other emails to select them individually. Once selected, tap the envelope icon with an open top (Mark as read) in the action bar to update their status. For bulk actions, some views may offer a 'Select all' option within the menu after selecting one email.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail's search bar with 'is:unread' to quickly find all unread emails across your entire account, then apply the 'Mark as read' action.
- Be aware that marking all emails as read does not archive or delete them; it simply changes their status. They will still occupy space in your inbox.
- If you only want to mark emails in specific categories (like Promotions or Social) as read, navigate to that category first before following the steps for selecting all emails.
- You can quickly mark a single email as read or unread by clicking the open/closed envelope icon that appears when you hover over it in the inbox list (desktop only).
- If you accidentally mark important emails as read, you can usually undo the action within a few seconds using the 'Undo' notification that appears at the bottom left of your screen.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Before marking all emails as read, quickly scan your inbox to ensure you don't inadvertently miss any urgent or important messages you haven't fully reviewed yet. This action cannot be easily reversed for a very large number of emails after the 'Undo' window closes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification usually appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click it within a few seconds to revert the action.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete, archive, or move them from your inbox or current folder.

**How do I mark specific emails as unread again?**

To mark specific emails as unread, select them individually or in a group, then click the 'More' actions button (three dots) and choose 'Mark as unread' from the dropdown menu. On mobile, look for a closed envelope icon.

**Can I mark emails as read in other Gmail folders (e.g., Promotions)?**

Yes, the same steps apply to any folder or label in Gmail. Navigate to the desired folder (e.g., 'Promotions'), then select all emails and choose 'Mark as read.'

**Why are some emails still unread after I marked them all?**

This usually happens if you only marked emails on the current page as read, or if some unread emails were in different labels or categories. Ensure you click the 'Select all [number] conversations in Primary' link after selecting the first page.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut for marking emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in Gmail settings, you can select emails and then press 'Shift + I' to mark them as read. This can be very efficient for power users.

**Does marking all emails as read affect linked accounts in Gmail?**

The 'Mark as read' action only applies to the specific Gmail account you are currently viewing and managing. It will not affect emails in other linked or forwarded accounts unless you are actively viewing that linked account's inbox within Gmail.
