To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the 'Select all' checkbox and then the 'Mark as read' option. This process helps you quickly clear your unread count and achieve an organized inbox, especially when dealing with a large volume of messages.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Start by opening your web browser and navigating to gmail.com. Log in with your credentials if you aren't already signed in. Ensure you are viewing the inbox or specific label where you want to mark emails as read. If you need to create a new Gmail account, you can find a step-by-step guide here: [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

In your Gmail inbox, locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner, just above your email list. Click this checkbox. This action will select all the emails currently visible on that page (usually 50 or 100, depending on your display settings).

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in Your Inbox

If you have more emails than are displayed on the current page, after clicking the top-left checkbox, a message will appear just above your emails saying, 'All [number] conversations on this page are selected.' Next to this, you'll see a link: 'Select all [total number] conversations in Primary.' Click this link to extend your selection to *all* emails in your current view, not just those on the first page.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears just above your emails. Find the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope, or click the 'More' icon (three vertical dots) and then select 'Mark as read.' Gmail will then process your request, changing the status of all selected emails.

> 💡 **Tip:** This action will remove the bold styling from unread emails, indicating they have been read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails, Gmail might take a moment to update. You may see a notification at the bottom-left of your screen confirming that the conversations have been marked as read. Your inbox will now reflect the updated status, showing fewer or no unread emails.

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Labels (Optional)

If you wish to mark emails as read in other labels like 'Promotions,' 'Social,' or custom folders, navigate to each specific label. Then, repeat the selection and 'Mark as read' process as described in the previous steps for each label you want to clear.

> 💡 **Tip:** This method allows for targeted inbox management beyond just your primary inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail's search filters (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com`) to target specific groups of unread emails before marking them as read.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' option at the bottom-left of your screen immediately after the action. It's only available for a few seconds.
- Consider archiving emails you've marked as read to further declutter your inbox without deleting them. Select the emails and click the 'Archive' icon (a box with a downward arrow).



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to quickly achieve inbox zero, reduce visual clutter, or simply clear a large backlog of notifications that they don't need to individually open. It's a quick way to reset your inbox view.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification usually appears at the bottom-left of your screen. Clicking this within a few seconds will revert the action. Otherwise, you would need to manually mark specific emails as unread again.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete, archive, or move them from your inbox or any specific label. Your emails remain safely stored.

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can select multiple emails by tapping and holding one, then tapping others. After selection, tap the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the top toolbar. There isn't a direct 'select all' button for the entire inbox like on desktop, but you can quickly select many.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

You can use Gmail's search bar to filter emails by sender (e.g., `from:sender@example.com`). Once the search results are displayed, you can then use the 'Select all' checkbox and 'Mark as read' option to apply the action only to those filtered emails.

**Will this method work for emails across all my Gmail tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions)?**

The 'Select all conversations' option typically applies to the *current* tab or label you are viewing. To mark emails as read in other tabs like 'Social' or 'Promotions,' you'll need to navigate to each tab and repeat the process for that specific section.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Yes, you can set up Gmail filters. Go to Gmail settings, then 'See all settings,' and select 'Filters and Blocked Addresses.' Create a new filter based on criteria (e.g., sender, subject) and choose the action 'Mark as read.' This will apply to future incoming emails.
