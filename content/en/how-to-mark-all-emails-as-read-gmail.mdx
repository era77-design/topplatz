To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically use the search bar to find all unread messages, select them all, and then apply the 'Mark as read' action. This process allows you to quickly clear your unread count and achieve an organized inbox with minimal effort.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device with internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. If you're not already signed in, enter your Gmail email address or phone number and password to log in. This will take you directly to your main inbox view, where you can begin the process of managing your emails.

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure you are logging into the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail addresses. If you need to [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account), check out our guide.

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar located at the very top of your Gmail interface, type `is:unread` and then press Enter or click the search icon. This specific command filters your entire inbox, displaying only messages that are currently marked as unread. This is a crucial step for isolating all the emails you wish to mark as read.

### Step 3: Select All Displayed Unread Emails

Once the search results load, locate the 'Select' checkbox at the top-left of your email list. This checkbox is usually found just above the first email displayed. Click this checkbox to select all the unread emails currently visible on the page, which typically defaults to 50 conversations at a time.

### Step 4: Select All Matching Unread Emails (Beyond the First Page)

After clicking the main 'Select' checkbox, a small banner will appear above your emails, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link that says 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to ensure *all* unread emails in your entire Gmail account, across all pages, are selected for the action.

### Step 5: Mark as Read

With all unread emails now selected, look for the 'More' menu icon (represented by three vertical dots) in the toolbar located just above your emails. Click on this icon, and from the dropdown menu that appears, choose the option 'Mark as read'. A confirmation message might briefly appear, indicating that the action is being processed, and your selected emails will change status.

### Step 6: Confirm Your Inbox Status

After the process completes, you should observe your unread count decrease significantly, or even disappear entirely if all emails were marked. You can then remove the `is:unread` filter from the search bar to return to your regular inbox view. This allows you to verify that your unread messages are now marked as read, leaving your inbox feeling much tidier and more organized.


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark only specific unread emails as read, simply select them individually using their checkboxes and then click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) in the toolbar.
- This method works across all Gmail labels and folders, not just your primary inbox, as long as the `is:unread` search includes them.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, you can quickly undo the action by clicking the 'Undo' notification that appears briefly after the action, or by selecting the emails and choosing 'Mark as unread'.
- Regularly clearing your unread emails can help reduce digital clutter and make it easier to focus on new, important messages.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when using 'Select all conversations that match this search' as it applies the action to potentially thousands of emails. Ensure you truly want to mark *all* unread emails as read before proceeding, as this action cannot be easily undone for a very large number of emails.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can select multiple emails by tapping and holding one, then tapping others. To select all, tap the checkbox at the top. Then, tap the three-dot menu and choose 'Mark as read'.

**What if I only want to mark unread emails in a specific folder or label?**

To do this, first navigate to that specific label or folder within Gmail. Then, use the `is:unread` search query within that label. The 'Select all conversations that match this search' option will then apply only to emails within that particular label.

**Will marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete or archive them in any way. They will remain in your inbox or their respective labels, simply without the 'unread' designation.

**How can I quickly find my unread emails after marking them all as read?**

Even after marking them as read, you can still find specific emails using Gmail's powerful search features. You can search by sender, subject, keywords, or date ranges to locate any messages you're looking for, regardless of their read status.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Gmail's filters can be set up to automatically mark incoming emails as read based on specific criteria like sender, subject, or keywords. You can create new filters by going to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses.

**What does 'achieve inbox zero' mean?**

'Inbox zero' is a popular email management technique where the goal is to keep your inbox empty or with very few emails at all times. It involves processing emails promptly by responding, archiving, deleting, or marking them as read, to reduce mental clutter.

**Can I mark emails as unread again if I make a mistake?**

Yes, if you accidentally mark emails as read, you can quickly undo the action using the 'Undo' notification that appears at the bottom-left of your screen for a few seconds. Alternatively, you can select the emails and click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).
