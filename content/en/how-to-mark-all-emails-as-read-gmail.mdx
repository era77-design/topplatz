To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the "Select all" checkbox and then click the "Mark as read" button. This action helps declutter your inbox and manage your email efficiently, ensuring you only see new, unread messages that require your attention.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- Internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account using your credentials. If you are using a mobile device, launch the Gmail application.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Folder or Label

From the left-hand sidebar, click on the inbox, a specific label (such as 'Promotions' or 'Social'), or 'All Mail' where you wish to mark emails as read. This step ensures you are targeting the correct set of emails.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

On your desktop, locate and click the small checkbox positioned at the top-left corner of your email list, directly above the first email. This action will select all emails currently displayed on that page, typically 50 or 100 messages.

### Step 4: Select All Emails in the Folder/Label

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear above your email list, indicating how many conversations are selected on the current page. To select *all* emails within that specific folder or label, click the link that says 'Select all conversations that match this search'.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all your desired emails selected, find the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar located just above your emails. This icon typically resembles an open envelope. Click on it to change the status of all selected emails to 'read'.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Gmail might prompt you to confirm this bulk action, especially if you are marking a large volume of emails. Review the confirmation message and then click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. All selected emails will then be updated to 'read' status.

### Step 7: Repeat for Other Labels (Optional)

If you have unread emails distributed across various labels or categories, you may need to repeat steps 2-6 for each one. This ensures that all desired emails across your entire Gmail account are marked as read, helping you maintain a tidy inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- Utilize Gmail's powerful search bar to filter emails by specific criteria (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com`) before selecting them to mark as read, for more precise control.
- Regularly manage your spam and trash folders; emails here do not count towards your main inbox unread count and are often automatically purged after a set period.
- Consider activating Gmail's 'Categories' (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) to automatically sort incoming mail. This makes it simpler to manage and mark specific types of emails as read.
- If you've recently [created a new Gmail account](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account), familiarizing yourself with these bulk actions can significantly enhance your email management efficiency from the very beginning.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Exercise caution when marking a large number of emails as read, especially if you haven't thoroughly reviewed them. Once marked, it can be more challenging to quickly identify truly important messages that you might have overlooked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps achieve 'Inbox Zero' and reduces visual clutter. It allows you to focus solely on new, unread messages that genuinely require your attention, streamlining your email workflow.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only alters their status from unread to read. The emails remain safely stored in your inbox or their respective labels and are not removed from your account.

**Can I undo the action of marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after performing the action, a small 'Undo' notification typically appears at the bottom-left of your screen. Click this promptly to revert the change. Otherwise, you would need to manually locate and mark specific emails as unread again.

**How do I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the mobile app, there isn't a direct 'select all' button for an entire folder like on desktop. You generally need to long-press an email to select it, then tap additional emails individually. Alternatively, use the search bar with `is:unread` to filter, then select them.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

You can use the search bar to filter your emails precisely. For instance, type `from:sender@example.com is:unread` to display only unread emails from that sender. Then, follow the steps to select and mark only those filtered messages as read.

**Will this affect emails in my Spam or Trash folders?**

No, the 'Select all' function and 'Mark as read' action typically apply only to the emails within the specific label or folder you are currently viewing (e.g., Inbox, Promotions, Sent). Emails in Spam or Trash are managed separately by Gmail's automated systems.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut for marking emails as read?**

Yes, if keyboard shortcuts are enabled in your Gmail settings, you can select one or more emails and then press `Shift + I` to mark them as read. To enable shortcuts, go to Settings (gear icon) > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts.
