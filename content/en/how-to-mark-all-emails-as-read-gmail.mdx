To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first log into your account and navigate to your inbox. Use the 'Select all' checkbox at the top, then click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) to instantly clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- Internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Log In to Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and go to gmail.com. Enter your email address or phone number and password to log in. Ensure you're accessing the correct account where you want to mark emails as read. If you're new to Gmail or need to set up a new account, you can find help with our guide on [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox Tab

Once logged in, you'll typically land in your 'Primary' inbox. If you want to mark emails as read in other categories like 'Social' or 'Promotions', click on the respective tab at the top of your inbox. This action will display only the emails within that specific category.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox. This will select all emails currently visible on the page, typically 50 or 100 emails, depending on your display settings.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations (If Applicable)

If you have more emails than are displayed on the current page (e.g., hundreds or thousands), after clicking the initial checkbox, a message will appear above your emails stating, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately following this, there will be a link that says, 'Select all X conversations in Primary' (or whichever tab you are in). Click this link to select every email in that category, not just those on the current page.

### Step 5: Mark Emails as Read

After selecting all desired emails, look for the toolbar just above your email list. Click the 'Mark as read' icon. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. A confirmation message will briefly appear, letting you know the emails have been marked as read.

### Step 6: Verify Your Inbox

Once the process is complete, you should notice that all the selected emails no longer appear in bold text, indicating they are now marked as read. The unread count for that specific inbox category (e.g., Primary, Social) should also decrease or disappear entirely. You can repeat this process for other inbox tabs if needed.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark specific emails as read without selecting all, hover over the email in your inbox and click the 'Mark as read' icon (open envelope) that appears to the far right.
- Consider using Gmail's search filters to find specific groups of emails (e.g., emails from a particular sender, or emails older than a certain date) and then mark only those as read.
- Regularly marking emails as read helps keep your inbox organized and reduces visual clutter, making it easier to spot new, important messages.
- You can also use keyboard shortcuts. After selecting emails, press 'Shift + I' to mark them as read.
- If you accidentally mark an important email as read, look for the 'Undo' notification that briefly appears after the action, or manually mark it as unread by selecting it and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (closed envelope).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be careful when marking all emails as read, especially if you have a very full inbox. Ensure you don't miss any critical unread messages that require your immediate attention.
- ⚠️ This action cannot be undone for individual emails if the 'Undo' notification disappears. You would have to manually mark specific emails as unread again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all my emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps declutter your inbox, reduces the visual distraction of bold unread messages, and can make it easier to identify truly new and important communications as they arrive.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom-left of your screen. Clicking this will revert the action. If you miss this, you would need to manually mark specific emails as unread again.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete or archive them. The emails will remain in your inbox or their respective labels.

**How do I mark specific emails as unread after doing this?**

To mark a specific email as unread, select it by clicking its checkbox, then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) in the toolbar above your emails. This will revert its status.

**Does this method work on the Gmail mobile app?**

The general concept works on the Gmail mobile app, but the steps are slightly different. You typically long-press an email to select it, then tap the three dots or a similar menu icon to find the 'Mark as read' option. There isn't usually a 'select all conversations' option for thousands of emails at once like on desktop.

**Can I mark emails in a specific label or folder as read?**

Yes, you can. Navigate to the desired label or folder in the left sidebar. Once inside, the 'Select all' checkbox and 'Mark as read' icon will function the same way as in your main inbox, allowing you to mark all emails within that specific label as read.

**What if I have emails across multiple pages in my inbox?**

If you have more than one page of emails, after selecting the first 50 or 100 emails on the visible page, a prompt will appear saying 'Select all X conversations in [Inbox Tab]'. Clicking this prompt is crucial to mark all emails across all pages as read.
