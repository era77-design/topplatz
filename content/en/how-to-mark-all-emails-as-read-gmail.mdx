To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the search bar to filter unread messages and then select them all for a bulk action. This process helps you quickly clear your inbox of unread notifications, especially after a long break or when managing a large volume of emails.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Computer or mobile device with internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Start by navigating to gmail.com on your computer's web browser. Ensure you are logged into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read. If you haven't already, you might need to sign in with your Google credentials.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you need to create a new Gmail account, you can find a guide on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface, type `is:unread` and press Enter. This command will filter your inbox to display only messages that you have not yet opened or marked as read. It's crucial for efficiently identifying all unread items across your entire inbox.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Unread Emails

Look for the checkbox located at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all the unread emails currently visible on your screen. Gmail usually displays 50 conversations per page by default, so this will select those.

### Step 4: Select All Unread Emails (Across Pages)

After clicking the checkbox to select visible emails, a notification will appear above your email list stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, you will also see an option to 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to ensure *all* unread emails in your entire inbox (not just the current page) are selected.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all unread emails selected, find the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your email list. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click on this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read, clearing your unread count.

### Step 6: Confirm and Clear Your Inbox

Gmail will process your request, and you might see a brief loading message or a confirmation notification. Once complete, your inbox should no longer show any unread messages, or at least significantly fewer, depending on what you selected. You can confirm by removing the `is:unread` filter from the search bar.


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark unread emails in a specific folder, first navigate to that folder (e.g., 'Promotions') and then use the `is:unread` search filter.
- Regularly check your spam and promotions folders, as important emails can sometimes end up there and contribute to your unread count.
- Consider setting up filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read if you know they don't require immediate attention.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking all emails as read, especially if you haven't reviewed them. Important information or urgent tasks could be missed if not properly scanned beforehand.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can search for `is:unread`, then tap the checkbox at the top to select all visible emails. After that, tap the three-dot menu icon and select 'Mark as read' to clear them.

**What if I only want to mark unread emails in a specific folder or label?**

Navigate to the specific folder or label first (e.g., 'Updates' or a custom label). Then, use the `is:unread` search filter within that view. This will ensure only emails within that specific section are displayed and marked as read.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, you might see an 'Undo' option appear briefly at the bottom-left of your screen. If you click it in time, the action will be reversed. Otherwise, there isn't a direct 'unmark all as read' function, and you would need to manually mark specific emails as unread again.

**Why do I have so many unread emails?**

A high volume of unread emails often results from subscriptions, promotional emails, or alerts that accumulate over time without being opened. It can also happen after a period of inactivity or if you're subscribed to many newsletters.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read simply changes their status from 'unread' to 'read' within your inbox. The emails remain in your account and can still be accessed, searched for, or moved to other folders.

**How do I mark an individual email as read?**

To mark an individual email as read, simply open it. Alternatively, in the inbox view, you can hover over the email and click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) that appears, or select the email with a checkbox and use the toolbar icon.

**What's the difference between 'Mark as read' and 'Archive'?**

Marking as read changes the email's status but keeps it in your inbox. Archiving removes the email from your inbox view but doesn't delete it; it can still be found by searching or in the 'All Mail' folder. Archiving helps declutter your inbox without deleting information.
