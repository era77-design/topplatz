To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, click the top-left checkbox to select all visible emails, then click the banner that appears to select all conversations. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the toolbar.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- Internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Your Gmail Inbox

Start by navigating to mail.google.com in your web browser. If you're not already signed in, enter your Gmail username and password to access your inbox. Make sure you are viewing the inbox or a specific label where you want to mark emails as read.

> 💡 **Tip:** This method works best on a desktop browser for full functionality, though similar steps apply to the Gmail mobile app.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the 'Compose' button and below the search bar. Click this checkbox once. This action will instantly select all emails currently displayed on your screen, which typically includes up to 50 conversations per page.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in Your Inbox (If Applicable)

After selecting the visible emails, a yellow banner will appear at the top of your inbox. This banner will say something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link: 'Select all X conversations in Primary' (or whatever category/label you are viewing). Click this link to select every single email within that specific view, not just those on the current page.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your email list. Among the icons, find the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon. This action will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read, removing the bold formatting and unread count.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

After clicking 'Mark as read,' Gmail will process your request. A small notification will usually appear in the bottom-left corner of your screen, indicating that the conversations have been marked as read. Depending on the number of emails, this process might take a few moments to complete.

### Step 6: Optional: Mark Specific Categories or Labels as Read

If you only want to mark emails in a specific category (like 'Promotions' or 'Social') or a custom label as read, first navigate to that category or label. Then, follow steps 2 through 4 within that specific view. You can also use the Gmail search bar with terms like 'category:promotions is:unread' to refine your selection before marking them as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly undo marking emails as read, look for the 'Undo' button that appears briefly after the action in the bottom-left corner of your screen.
- For new Gmail users, understanding inbox management from the start can be very helpful. If you're just getting started, you might want to review how to set up your account efficiently: [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).
- If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, simply click the checkbox next to each individual email, then click the 'Mark as read' icon.
- Utilize Gmail's search operators (e.g., `is:unread`, `label:work`, `before:2023/01/01`) to precisely filter emails before marking them as read.
- Be aware that marking all emails as read will clear your unread count. Ensure you don't need to review any of these emails before performing this action.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking all emails as read cannot be undone if you close your browser or navigate away from the page after the 'Undo' notification disappears. Be certain before proceeding.
- ⚠️ This action does not delete emails; it only changes their status from unread to read. The emails will remain in your inbox or their respective labels/categories.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to declutter their inbox, reduce the visual noise of unread counts, or get a fresh start, especially after being away or when dealing with a large backlog of non-urgent emails.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button will appear in the bottom-left corner of your Gmail screen. Click it quickly to revert the action. However, this option is only available for a short period.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not move them to the trash, archive them, or delete them in any way. They will remain in their original location within your Gmail account.

**How can I mark only emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

Navigate to the desired category (e.g., click 'Promotions' in the left sidebar). Then, follow the same steps: click the top-left checkbox, then click 'Select all X conversations in Promotions,' and finally click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Will this method work on the Gmail mobile app?**

The Gmail mobile app has similar functionality. You can long-press an email to select it, then tap the checkbox at the top to 'Select all' on the screen. However, selecting *all* conversations beyond the current screen might require a bit more scrolling and selecting, or using the desktop version for bulk actions.

**What if I have tens of thousands of unread emails?**

The method of selecting all conversations via the banner works regardless of the total number of emails. Gmail will process them in batches, and it might take a moment, but it will eventually mark all specified emails as read. Just be patient after clicking the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Can I mark emails as unread again if I made a mistake?**

Yes, if you marked emails as read by mistake and the 'Undo' option is gone, you can select them again (either individually or by using search queries like `after:2024/01/01 is:read`) and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) from the toolbar.
