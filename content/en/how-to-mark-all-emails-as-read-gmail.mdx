To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first navigate to your inbox or a specific label. Then, use the "Select all" checkbox at the top left of your email list, and if prompted, click the link to select all conversations in that view. Finally, click the "Mark as read" icon (an open envelope) from the toolbar that appears to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser on your computer or launch the Gmail app on your mobile device. Navigate to mail.google.com and log in with your credentials if you haven't already. Ensure you are in the correct Gmail account you wish to manage.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired View

Go to the specific section of Gmail where you want to mark emails as read. This could be your main Inbox, a specific category like 'Promotions' or 'Social', or a custom label you've created. You can also perform a search (e.g., `is:unread`) to target a specific set of emails across your account.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small square checkbox positioned at the top-left of your email list, usually just above the first message. Click this checkbox once. This action will select all emails currently visible on the page, typically 50 conversations, highlighting them for further action.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations in the View

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear above your email list, stating something like "All 50 conversations on this page are selected." Crucially, if you intend to mark *all* emails in that particular inbox, label, or search result as read, click the blue link within this message that says, "Select all [number] conversations in [Inbox/Label Name]." This ensures the action applies to everything, not just the current page.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all your desired emails are selected (either on the page or throughout the view), a new toolbar will become visible above your email list. Look for the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically resembles an open envelope. Click this icon to initiate the process of changing their status.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

After clicking the 'Mark as read' icon, a confirmation message, such as "Conversations marked as read," will briefly appear at the bottom-left of your screen. This indicates that the selected emails have successfully been updated to a read status.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox

The emails you selected should now appear without bold text, signifying they are no longer considered unread. Take a moment to scan your inbox or the relevant label to confirm the change. If you still see unread emails, they might be in another category or label, requiring you to repeat the process for those sections.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use search filters for precision: To mark specific types of emails as read (e.g., all emails older than a year, or from a particular sender), utilize Gmail's powerful search bar. For example, type `is:unread older_than:1y` or `is:unread from:newsletter@example.com` then follow the selection and mark-as-read steps.
- Undo if needed: If you accidentally mark emails as read, a small 'Undo' button typically appears at the bottom-left of your screen for a few seconds after the action. Click it quickly to revert the change.
- Consider using filters for new accounts: If you're just starting with Gmail or have recently [created a new Gmail account](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account), setting up filters from the beginning can help maintain an organized inbox. You can configure filters to automatically mark certain incoming emails (like newsletters or promotional mail) as read, preventing a backlog from forming.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking a large number of emails as read. While convenient, ensure you don't inadvertently miss important messages that you intended to read carefully. Always double-check your selection before confirming the 'Mark as read' action.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark only unread emails as read?**

Yes, you can specifically target unread emails. Type `is:unread` in the Gmail search bar, then select all the results and proceed to mark them as read. This is an efficient way to clear all unread messages across your entire account or a specific label.

**What if I only want to mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

Navigate directly to that specific category tab (e.g., Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) within your Gmail inbox. Once there, use the 'Select all' checkbox and the subsequent 'Select all conversations in [Category Name]' link before clicking the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut to mark emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in your Gmail settings. After selecting the desired emails using the checkbox, you can simply press `Shift + I` on your keyboard to mark them as read, offering a quick alternative to clicking the icon.

**How do I undo marking emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen. Click this button quickly to revert the action. If you miss this brief window, you will need to manually select the emails and mark them as unread again.

**Will marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. This action does not delete them, archive them, or move them to another folder. Your emails will remain in their original location within your Gmail account.

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can long-press on an email to select it, then tap additional emails to select more. After selecting, tap the three-dot menu icon or the open envelope icon (depending on your app version) and choose 'Mark as read' from the options.

**Why do I still see unread emails after marking them all as read?**

This often occurs if you have emails in different categories (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) or if you only marked emails within a specific label or search result. You need to repeat the 'select all' and 'mark as read' process for each category or label where unread emails are present to clear them all.
