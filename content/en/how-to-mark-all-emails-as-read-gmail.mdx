To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox on a desktop browser, select all visible emails using the top-left checkbox, and then click the "Select all conversations that match this search" link if many emails exist. Finally, use the "Mark as read" icon to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account access

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox on Desktop

Begin by opening your web browser and navigating to gmail.com. Log in to your Gmail account if you haven't already. Ensure you are viewing your primary inbox or the specific label/folder where you wish to mark emails as read. This method offers the most comprehensive options on a desktop computer for full functionality.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Once in your inbox, locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner, just above your email list. Clicking this checkbox will select all emails currently visible on the page, which usually comprises 50 conversations. You'll see a notification confirming how many conversations are selected.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations That Match Your Search (If Applicable)

If you have more than 50 unread emails, after clicking the top checkbox, a message will appear above your emails stating, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately following this, you'll see a crucial link that says, 'Select all [number] conversations that match this search.' Click this link to select every single email in your current view, not just those on the first page.

### Step 4: Mark Emails as Read

With all desired emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar located just above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click on it. Gmail will process your request, and you'll usually see a confirmation message at the bottom-left of your screen, indicating that the action has been successfully completed.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action and Observe Changes

Depending on the number of emails being processed, Gmail might ask for a final confirmation before marking a large batch as read. If prompted, confirm the action. Allow a few moments for Gmail to update your inbox. Your unread email count should significantly decrease or disappear entirely from the label or category you've just processed, giving you a cleaner inbox view.

### Step 6: (Optional) Repeat for Other Labels or Categories

If you utilize Gmail's default categories (such as Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) or have created custom labels, you might need to repeat this process for each section. Navigate to the specific category or label you want to clear, then follow steps 2-5 again. This ensures that all your emails across different sections of your Gmail account are marked as read.

### Step 7: Mark All as Read Using the Gmail Mobile App

On the Gmail mobile app, the process is slightly different. Open the app, then long-press on one unread email to select it. Once selected, tap the checkbox icon at the top of the screen to select all visible emails on that page. Then, tap the three-dot menu icon (More options) and choose 'Mark as read' from the dropdown. You might need to scroll down and repeat this for multiple pages of emails as there isn't a 'select all conversations' option like on desktop.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use the search bar: To mark specific types of emails as read, use the search bar (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com`) and then apply the 'select all' method.
- Check other folders: Don't forget to check your 'Promotions,' 'Social,' and other custom labels or folders if you want to clear all unread counts across your entire account.
- Gmail keyboard shortcuts: Enable keyboard shortcuts in Gmail settings (Settings > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts on) to quickly select emails (e.g., `*a` to select all, then `Shift+i` to mark as read).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking large batches of emails as read, especially if you haven't reviewed them recently. You might miss important information if you mark everything as read without a quick scan.
- ⚠️ Marking emails as read does not delete them; they will still be in your inbox or archives, just with their status changed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to achieve 'inbox zero,' reduce digital clutter, or reset their unread count after a vacation or a period of high email volume. It helps in focusing on new incoming emails without old ones distracting you.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, you'll see an 'Undo' option at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it quickly to revert the action. After this short window, you would need to manually mark specific emails as unread again if you change your mind.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read.' It does not delete or archive them. All your emails will remain safely in your inbox or the respective labels/folders, accessible whenever you need them.

**Can I mark emails as read in specific labels or categories only?**

Absolutely. Navigate to the specific label or category (like 'Promotions' or a custom label you created) in your Gmail sidebar. Then, follow the same steps to select and mark all emails as read within that particular section, leaving other parts of your inbox untouched.

**How do I mark specific emails as unread again?**

To mark individual emails as unread, simply select the email(s) by checking the box next to them. Then, click the 'Mark as unread' icon in the toolbar, which typically looks like a closed envelope. This will revert their status back to unread.

**Is there a way to automatically mark emails as read?**

While there isn't a direct 'mark all future emails as read' setting, you can create filters in Gmail to automatically mark certain incoming emails as read, archive them, or move them to specific labels upon arrival. This is useful for newsletters or notifications you don't need to actively read.

**Does this method work on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, you can mark multiple emails as read on the Gmail mobile app. However, the 'Select all conversations that match this search' option for bulk selection across multiple pages is generally a desktop-only feature. On mobile, you typically select visible emails in batches and repeat the process for additional pages.
