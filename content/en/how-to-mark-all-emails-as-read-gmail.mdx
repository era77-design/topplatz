Marking all emails as read in Gmail is a simple process that can help you clear your inbox clutter and feel more organized. This guide will walk you through the steps to quickly achieve inbox zero, whether you want to clear a few pages or your entire email history.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser on your computer or launch the Gmail app on your mobile device. Navigate to mail.google.com and log in with your credentials if prompted. Ensure you are logged into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

Once in your Gmail inbox, locate the small checkbox at the top-left of your email list, usually just above your emails and below the search bar. Clicking this checkbox will select all visible emails on the current page of your inbox. A banner will appear at the top confirming the number of selected conversations.

### Step 3: Select All Emails in Your Inbox

After selecting the emails on the current page, a banner will appear at the top of your inbox, stating, for example, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, to mark *all* emails (not just those visible), click the link immediately next to this message that says, 'Select all X conversations in Primary' (or whichever tab you are currently viewing, like Social or Promotions). This action extends your selection to every email within that category.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all desired emails selected, look for the toolbar located above your emails. Click on the 'More' icon, which typically looks like three vertical dots. From the dropdown menu that appears, choose the 'Mark as read' option. This action tells Gmail to change the status of all selected emails.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails you've selected, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up asking if you are sure you want to proceed. Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to finalize the process. It may take a few moments for Gmail to process the request, especially if you are marking thousands of emails as read. Be patient as the changes are applied to your inbox.

### Step 6: Verify Your Inbox Status

Once the process is complete, navigate through your inbox or other labels to confirm that the selected emails now appear as read. The bold formatting typically associated with unread emails should be gone. If you acted in error, a small 'Undo' notification briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen, allowing you to revert the change immediately.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark specific emails as read without selecting all, hover over the email and click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) that appears on the right.
- You can use Gmail's search bar to filter emails by specific criteria (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com`) before selecting and marking them as read.
- Consider setting up Gmail filters to automatically mark certain types of emails as read upon arrival, helping to manage your inbox proactively.
- Regularly marking emails as read can contribute to a more organized inbox, making it easier to find important messages later. If you're new to Gmail, learning these management tricks can be very helpful after you [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).
- If you want to quickly clear a specific folder or label, navigate to that label, select all, and then mark as read using the same steps.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking a large number of emails as read cannot be easily undone after the 'Undo' notification disappears. Ensure you want to mark all selected emails as read before confirming.
- ⚠️ This action only marks emails as read; it does not delete them or move them to another folder. They will remain in your inbox or current label.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What happens after I mark all emails as read?**

After you mark all emails as read, they will no longer appear in bold text in your inbox. This action helps clear your unread count, making it easier to identify new incoming messages.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom-left of your screen. You have a few seconds to click this to revert the action. If you miss this window, there is no direct 'undo all' option.

**Does this delete my emails?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete, archive, or move your emails to any other folder. All your messages will remain in their original locations.

**How do I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the square icon at the top to select all. For more options, tap the three dots menu and choose 'Mark as read'. There isn't a direct 'select all conversations' link like on desktop, so you'll typically select visible emails page by page.

**Can I mark emails in specific categories (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

Yes, you can. Navigate to the specific tab (e.g., Promotions, Social) in your Gmail inbox, then follow the same steps to select all emails on the page and then the link to 'Select all conversations in Promotions' before marking them as read.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to achieve 'inbox zero,' reduce visual clutter, or simply clear a backlog of old, less important emails. It helps in focusing on new or truly important messages.
