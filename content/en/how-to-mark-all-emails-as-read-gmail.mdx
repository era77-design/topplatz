Clearing your unread email count in Gmail is a simple task that can help declutter your inbox. Whether you're managing a long-standing account or just learning the ropes after you [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account), this guide will show you how to mark all your emails as read quickly and efficiently. By following a few straightforward steps, you can achieve inbox zero and bring order to your digital communication.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device with internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser on your computer or launch the Gmail app on your mobile device. Navigate to mail.google.com if you're using a browser. Ensure you are logged into the specific Gmail account where you wish to mark emails as read. If you're not logged in, you'll be prompted to enter your credentials.

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

Locate the search bar at the very top of your Gmail interface. In this search bar, type the command `is:unread` and then press Enter on your keyboard or tap the search icon on mobile. This action will filter your entire Gmail account to display only messages that are currently marked as unread, regardless of their label or folder.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Unread Emails

Once your unread emails are displayed, look for a small checkbox. On desktop, this checkbox is usually located at the top of the email list, directly above the first message. Click this checkbox to select all emails currently visible on the page. Gmail typically displays 50 or 100 conversations per page by default.

### Step 4: Select All Emails Matching the Search

After clicking the initial checkbox, a banner will appear at the top of your screen. This banner often states something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link or option that says 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection beyond the current page, ensuring every single unread email in your account is included.

### Step 5: Mark Emails as Read

With all your unread emails now selected, locate the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. On desktop, this icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action will begin the process of clearing your unread count.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action (If Prompted)

If you are marking a very large number of emails as read, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up window. This is a safety measure to prevent accidental bulk actions. Read the confirmation message carefully and then click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed with marking all the selected emails as read. The process may take a few moments depending on the volume of emails.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox

After the action is complete, your unread email count should be significantly reduced or completely gone. You can remove the `is:unread` filter from the search bar by clicking the 'X' next to it or clearing the search bar entirely. This will return you to your regular inbox view, where you can confirm that your unread messages are now marked as read, helping you achieve a cleaner inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark only unread emails within your primary inbox (excluding other categories like Promotions or Social), use the search query `in:inbox is:unread`.
- If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, you can select them individually by checking their respective boxes and then clicking the 'Mark as read' icon.
- Regularly using this method can help you maintain an organized inbox and reduce the mental clutter associated with a high unread count.
- Consider setting up Gmail filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read or even archive them upon arrival, reducing future manual effort.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Always double-check your selection before performing bulk actions like marking all emails as read. Ensure you don't accidentally mark important messages that you still need to review as read.
- ⚠️ Marking emails as read does not delete them. They will remain in your inbox or their respective folders/labels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process on the Gmail mobile app is very similar. Use the search bar for `is:unread`, then select the first email, tap the 'Select all' option (often found under a three-dot menu), and finally, tap the 'Mark as read' icon (usually an open envelope).

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

Don't worry, marking an email as read doesn't delete it. You can still easily find it by searching for it or browsing your inbox. If needed, you can simply open the email and click the 'Mark as unread' icon to revert its status.

**Does marking emails as read delete them from my account?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read.' It does not delete them, move them to the trash, or archive them. All your emails remain accessible in your account.

**How can I mark only emails from a specific category (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

You can combine search operators for more specific actions. For example, type `category:promotions is:unread` in the search bar. Then, follow the same steps to select all matching conversations and mark them as read.

**Why would someone want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users do this to achieve 'inbox zero,' declutter their email view, or clear a large backlog of unread messages they may have already glanced at or deemed unimportant. It can provide a fresh start and reduce digital stress.

**Is there a way to prevent emails from being marked as unread in the first place?**

While you can't prevent all emails from arriving as unread, you can set up powerful Gmail filters. These filters can automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read, archive them, or move them to specific labels upon arrival, streamlining your inbox management.

**I clicked the checkbox, but I don't see the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link. What should I do?**

If this link doesn't appear, it likely means you have fewer unread emails than the default number displayed per page (e.g., 50). In this case, clicking the initial checkbox already selects all of your unread emails, and you can proceed directly to clicking the 'Mark as read' icon.
