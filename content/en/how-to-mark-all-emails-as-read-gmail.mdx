To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the 'Select all' checkbox and then the 'Mark as read' button in your inbox. This action can be applied to all visible emails on a page or extended to all emails within a specific label or category.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Internet access
- Web browser or Gmail app

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account if you haven't already. Ensure you are viewing the inbox or specific label where you want to mark emails as read. This is the starting point for managing your email statuses.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the checkbox at the top of your inbox, usually above your emails on the left side. Click this checkbox to select all emails currently displayed on the page. A message will appear indicating how many conversations are selected, typically 50 or 100 depending on your display settings.

### Step 3: Select All Emails in a Category/Label (Optional)

After selecting all visible emails, a notification will appear at the top, such as 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' If you wish to mark *all* emails in that specific category (e.g., Primary, Promotions) or the entire inbox as read, click the text that says 'Select all [number] conversations in [category/label/inbox].' This extends the selection beyond the current page, allowing for a comprehensive update.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With your desired emails selected, look for the 'More' menu (three vertical dots) or the 'Mark as read' icon. The 'Mark as read' icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read, effectively clearing their unread status.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Gmail will process your request, and the selected emails will now appear as read (usually indicated by a lighter font or absence of bolding). You might see a confirmation message, 'Conversations marked as read,' at the bottom left of your screen. This helps declutter your inbox and allows you to focus on new, incoming messages.

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Categories (If Necessary)

If you use different inbox categories (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums), you will need to repeat this process for each category individually. Navigate to each tab and follow steps 2-5 to mark all emails within those sections as read. This ensures your entire Gmail account is updated to reflect only truly new or unread messages.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use the search bar with `is:unread` to quickly find all unread emails across your entire Gmail account, then apply the 'select all' and 'mark as read' action.
- Consider setting up filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read if they don't require your immediate attention.
- Regularly marking emails as read helps maintain an organized inbox and reduces visual clutter, contributing to a more efficient workflow.
- If you're new to Gmail and need to get started, learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be careful when marking a large number of emails as read, especially if you haven't reviewed them. You might inadvertently miss important information.
- ⚠️ Once emails are marked as read, there isn't an 'undo all' button for this specific action across many emails, though you can manually mark individual emails as unread again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. You can tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the selection box at the top to select all visible, and then use the three-dot menu or the 'Mark as read' icon.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

If you accidentally mark an important email as read, you can easily mark it as unread again. Just open the email or select its checkbox in the inbox, then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (usually an enclosed envelope or a three-dot menu option).

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them or move them from your inbox. They remain in their original location within your Gmail account.

**How can I find unread emails after marking everything as read?**

You can use Gmail's search bar to find unread emails. Simply type `is:unread` into the search bar and press Enter. This will display all emails that are still marked as unread in your account, regardless of their location.

**Can I mark only emails from a specific sender as read?**

Yes, you can. First, search for emails from that specific sender using their email address in the search bar. Once the search results appear, you can then use the 'Select all' checkbox and the 'Mark as read' button to process only those emails.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read can help you achieve 'inbox zero,' reducing visual clutter and mental overhead. It's particularly useful for clearing out old, less important notifications or promotional emails that you've decided not to review individually, allowing you to focus on new messages.
