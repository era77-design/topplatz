Marking all emails as read in Gmail is an effective way to clear clutter from your inbox, helping you focus on new, incoming messages and achieve inbox zero. This action changes the status of selected emails from unread to read, making them appear regular text instead of bold.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- An active Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Log In to Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and go to mail.google.com, or launch the Gmail app on your mobile device. Ensure you are logged into the correct Gmail account where you wish to mark emails as read. If you're new to Gmail, you might find it helpful to learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) first.

### Step 2: Navigate to Your Desired Folder or Inbox

Once logged in, navigate to the specific folder or label (e.g., Primary Inbox, Promotions, Social, or a custom label) where you want to mark emails as read. This method works for any email list within Gmail, allowing you to target specific categories of messages.

### Step 3: Select Emails on the Current Page

In the web browser, locate the 'Select all' checkbox. This is typically found at the very top of your email list, just above the first email. Clicking this checkbox will select all visible emails on the current page of your inbox or folder, highlighting them.

### Step 4: Mark All Selected Emails as Read

After selecting the emails, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appears above your email list. This icon usually looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Mark All Emails in the Entire Folder (Advanced)

If you want to mark *all* emails in a specific folder (not just the current page) as read, first click the 'Select all' checkbox as described in Step 3. A banner will then appear at the top of your email list stating, 'All X conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately following this, you'll see a clickable phrase like 'Select all X conversations in [folder name]'. Click this second phrase to extend your selection to all emails in that folder, regardless of how many pages there are.

### Step 6: Confirm and Execute the Action

Once you've selected all the desired emails (either on the page or in the entire folder), click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar. A small confirmation notification will appear at the bottom left of your screen, letting you know the action was successful. All selected emails will now appear as read.

### Step 7: Review and Undo (If Necessary)

Quickly scan your inbox to ensure no important unread emails were accidentally marked. If you made a mistake, you can often click the 'Undo' button that appears in the confirmation banner at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action. Alternatively, you can manually select specific emails and choose 'Mark as unread' from the toolbar.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail's search filters (e.g., `is:unread in:inbox`) to quickly locate and mark specific sets of unread emails.
- Be cautious when marking 'all conversations' as read in a large folder, especially if you rely on the unread status to prioritize emails.
- This action is reversible. You can always select an email and choose 'Mark as unread' if you need to revisit it.
- Consider using Gmail's archiving feature for emails you've read and no longer need in your inbox, but don't want to delete.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on my phone using the Gmail app?**

Yes, you can. In the Gmail app, select multiple emails by tapping their profile pictures, then tap the three-dot menu icon at the top right and choose 'Mark as read'. There isn't a direct 'select all' button for an entire folder like on desktop, so you'll need to select emails in batches.

**What happens if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

If you accidentally mark an important email as read, you can usually click the 'Undo' button that appears briefly after the action. If you miss that, simply locate the email, select it, and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (often a closed envelope) in the toolbar to revert its status.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read does not delete them. It only changes their status from unread to read. The emails will still be in your inbox or the folder you marked them in, just without the bolding or highlight that indicates they are unread.

**How do I mark only specific emails as read, not all of them?**

To mark only specific emails as read, simply click the checkbox next to each individual email you wish to select. Once all desired emails are checked, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appears above your email list.

**Is there a way to automatically mark emails as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a direct 'auto-mark as read' feature for all incoming emails. However, you can create filters that automatically mark certain types of emails as read upon arrival. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses to set up new filters.

**How can I find unread emails after marking everything as read?**

You can easily find unread emails using Gmail's search bar. Simply type `is:unread` into the search bar and press Enter. This will display all emails that are currently marked as unread across your entire Gmail account.

**Why would someone want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is often done to achieve 'inbox zero' or to quickly clear out old, non-urgent unread messages. It helps declutter the inbox visually, making it easier to identify and prioritize truly new and important communications.
