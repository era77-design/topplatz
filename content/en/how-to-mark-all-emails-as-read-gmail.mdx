To mark all emails as read in Gmail, use the 'Select all' checkbox at the top of your inbox, then click 'Select all conversations that match this search' and finally the 'Mark as read' icon. This method efficiently clears your unread count across your primary inbox, specific labels, or search results.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Computer or mobile device with internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Begin by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on your computer. Log in to your Gmail account if you haven't already. This will take you to your main inbox view, where all your emails are displayed.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Email View

You can mark all emails as read in your primary inbox, a specific category (like Social or Promotions), or a custom label. Click on the relevant category tab (e.g., Primary, Social, Promotions) or a label from the left sidebar to view the emails you wish to mark as read. If you're new to Gmail, you might find it helpful to review [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account) first to understand how your inbox is organized.

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure you're logged into the correct account, especially if you manage multiple Gmail addresses.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the very top of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all the emails currently displayed on the page (usually 50 conversations). A message will appear confirming the selection.

> 💡 **Tip:** This initial selection only applies to the emails visible on your current screen.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations That Match Your View

After clicking the top checkbox, a message will appear above your emails, stating something like "All 50 conversations on this page are selected." Crucially, you'll see an option: "Select all conversations that match this search." Click this text to extend your selection to *all* emails in that specific view, not just the visible ones.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is vital for marking *all* emails in a category or search result as read, not just the first page.

### Step 5: Click the "Mark as Read" Icon

With all desired conversations selected, look for the "Mark as read" icon in the toolbar that appears above your email list. This icon typically looks like an open envelope or a closed envelope with a checkmark. Click on it. Gmail will process the request, and the selected emails will now appear as read.

> 💡 **Tip:** The toolbar provides various actions like archiving, deleting, and moving emails, so make sure you click the correct icon.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action (If Prompted)

For large selections, Gmail might ask for confirmation, especially when performing a bulk action. A pop-up or notification might appear asking, "Are you sure you want to apply this action to all X conversations?" Click "OK" or "Confirm" to proceed. The unread count for that category or label should now disappear or significantly decrease.

> 💡 **Tip:** This confirmation step helps prevent accidental bulk actions.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox Status

After the process completes, refresh your browser or navigate to a different section and then back to your inbox or label. You should see that all the previously unread emails in your selected view are now marked as read, indicated by their lighter font color and the absence of the unread badge.

> 💡 **Tip:** If some emails still appear unread, double-check that you selected *all* conversations matching the view in Step 4.


## Tips & Tricks

- **Utilize Gmail's Search Bar for Precision:** To mark only specific emails as read (e.g., from a particular sender or containing certain keywords), use the search bar at the top. After performing your search, follow steps 3-5 to select and mark all results as read.
- **Mobile App Differences:** While the Gmail mobile app allows you to select multiple emails, marking *all* emails in a category as read simultaneously is generally easier and more efficient using the desktop web interface.
- **Use the Undo Button:** If you accidentally mark emails as read that you intended to keep unread, look for the "Undo" notification that briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen after the action. Click it quickly to revert the change.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read in Gmail?**

Marking all emails as read helps declutter your inbox and achieve "inbox zero," reducing visual noise. It's especially useful for clearing out old, less important notifications or promotional emails that you've glanced at but don't need to keep as unread reminders.

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

While the Gmail mobile app allows you to select multiple emails and mark them as read, it doesn't offer a direct "select all conversations that match this search" option like the desktop version. For bulk actions across an entire category or label, the desktop interface is more efficient.

**What if I only want to mark emails in a specific tab (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

Simply navigate to the desired tab (e.g., "Promotions") in your Gmail inbox. Then, follow the same steps: click the top-left checkbox, then click "Select all conversations that match this search," and finally the "Mark as read" icon.

**How do I undo marking emails as read if I made a mistake?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small "Undo" notification will appear at the bottom-left of your screen. Click this "Undo" button quickly to revert the action. If the notification disappears, you'll have to manually mark them as unread again.

**Will marking emails as read delete them from my inbox?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete, archive, or move them from your inbox or any labels they are currently in. They will simply appear as regular, opened emails.

**Can I mark emails as read without opening them individually?**

Yes, the method described in this guide allows you to mark multiple or all emails as read without opening them one by one. This is the primary purpose of using the "select all" and "mark as read" bulk actions.

**What's the difference between "Mark as Read" and "Archive" in Gmail?**

"Mark as Read" simply changes an email's status to read, keeping it in your inbox or current label. "Archive" removes the email from your inbox view but doesn't delete it; it moves it to "All Mail" for future reference, essentially decluttering your main inbox.
