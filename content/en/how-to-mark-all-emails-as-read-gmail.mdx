To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, click the 'Select all' checkbox, then click the 'Select all X conversations in [inbox/label]' link that appears, and finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon. This action helps clear your unread count and tidy up your inbox.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Enter your Gmail email address or phone number and password to log in. Ensure you're logged into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read. If you're new to Gmail, you might find it helpful to learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) first.

> 💡 **Tip:** For security, always log out of your Gmail account when using a public or shared computer.

### Step 2: Navigate to Your Inbox or a Specific Label

Once logged in, you'll typically land on your primary inbox. If you want to mark emails as read in a specific folder or label (e.g., 'Promotions,' 'Social,' or a custom label), click on that label in the left-hand sidebar. This ensures you're only targeting emails within that particular section.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can also use the search bar at the top to find specific emails or categories before marking them as read.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on the page (usually 50 or 100, depending on your display settings). You'll see a message indicating how many conversations are selected.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, simply check the box next to each individual email.

### Step 4: Select All Emails in the Current View/Label

After selecting all visible emails, a message will appear above your emails, typically stating 'All X conversations on this page are selected. Select all Y conversations in [Inbox/Label].' Click on this second part of the message (e.g., 'Select all 3,500 conversations in Primary'). This crucial step extends your selection to *all* emails within that specific inbox or label, not just those visible on the current page.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is essential for marking *all* emails as read, not just the first page.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click on it. Gmail will then process your request, marking all selected conversations as read. You might see a confirmation message briefly appear at the bottom-left of your screen.

> 💡 **Tip:** The 'Mark as read' icon will only appear in the toolbar after you have selected at least one email.

### Step 6: Verify the Action

After the process completes, your unread email count for that inbox or label should significantly decrease or disappear entirely. Scroll through your emails to confirm that they no longer appear bold (which indicates an unread message). This confirms that the operation was successful and your inbox is now free of unread notifications.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you accidentally marked an important email as read, you can quickly find it by searching for 'is:read' and then marking it as unread manually.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark individual emails as read without opening them, hover over the email in your inbox and click the 'Mark as read' icon that appears on the right side.
- Utilize Gmail's search operators like 'is:unread' to quickly find all unread messages across your entire account, then apply the 'Mark as read' action.
- Consider creating filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails (e.g., newsletters you don't always need to read immediately) as read upon arrival.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking a large number of emails as read, especially if you haven't reviewed them. You might accidentally overlook important messages or crucial updates.
- ⚠️ This action is largely irreversible for individual emails once you leave the page or refresh, though you can manually mark specific emails as unread later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I undo marking emails as read in Gmail?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, you'll see an 'Undo' option at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it quickly to revert the action. If you've navigated away or refreshed, you'll need to manually mark specific emails as unread.

**How do I mark only a specific category of emails as read?**

Navigate to the desired category or label (e.g., 'Promotions,' 'Social,' or a custom label) in the left sidebar. Then, follow the steps to select all emails within that specific view and mark them as read. This will only affect emails in that chosen section.

**Can I mark emails as read on my mobile device?**

Yes, the Gmail app for iOS and Android allows you to mark emails as read. You can usually select multiple emails by long-pressing one, then tapping others, and finally tapping the 'Mark as read' icon (often an open envelope or three dots menu option).

**What's the difference between 'Archive' and 'Mark as Read'?**

'Mark as Read' simply changes the status of an email from unread to read, removing the bold formatting and reducing your unread count. 'Archive' removes the email from your inbox view but keeps it accessible in 'All Mail,' effectively decluttering your inbox without deleting the message.

**Will marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read does not delete them. It only changes their status from unread to read. The emails will remain in your inbox or their respective labels/folders unless you explicitly choose to delete or archive them.

**How do I automatically mark certain emails as read upon arrival?**

You can set up a filter in Gmail. Go to 'Settings' > 'See all settings' > 'Filters and Blocked Addresses' > 'Create a new filter.' Define criteria for the emails you want to target, and then select the 'Mark as read' action for those filtered messages. This can be very useful for newsletters or notifications.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read can help you achieve 'Inbox Zero' and reduce digital clutter, making it easier to focus on new, incoming messages. It's often used when you're overwhelmed by a high volume of unread emails and want to start fresh without deleting anything important.
