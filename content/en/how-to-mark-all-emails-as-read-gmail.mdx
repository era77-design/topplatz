To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically use the 'Select all' checkbox, then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link, and finally choose the 'Mark as read' option from the toolbar. This process helps quickly clear your inbox of unread messages. It's an efficient way to manage a cluttered inbox and focus on new communications.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- An active Gmail account (If you don't have one, learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account))
- Internet connection

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser or App

Begin by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on a desktop computer. If you are using a mobile device, open the official Gmail app. Ensure you are logged into the Gmail account you wish to manage, as this action will affect the currently active inbox.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

In the desktop version, locate the small checkbox at the top-left of your inbox, just above your emails. Click this box to select all conversations currently displayed on the page. On mobile, you typically long-press one email to select it, then tap the selection circle for other emails to select multiple.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in Your Current View

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear at the top of your inbox, stating 'All X conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, you will see a link next to it that says, 'Select all X conversations that match this search.' Click this link to ensure *all* emails in your current view (e.g., your entire Primary inbox, or a specific label) are selected, not just those on the first page.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is vital for marking more than just the first page of emails as read. Skipping it will only affect the emails currently visible on your screen.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all relevant emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your inbox. Among the various icons, find the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read, effectively clearing their unread status.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails, Gmail might ask for confirmation or display a notification that the action is being processed. Allow a few moments for Gmail to apply the 'read' status to all the selected messages. For very large quantities of emails, this process might take slightly longer to complete.

### Step 6: Verify Your Inbox Status

After the operation is complete, refresh your inbox if necessary. You should now see that all previously unread emails in that specific view or label no longer appear in bold or with the unread indicator. This confirms that they have all been successfully marked as read, helping you achieve a cleaner and more organized inbox. If you still see unread emails, check other labels or categories like 'Social' or 'Promotions' and repeat the process for those sections.


## Tips & Tricks

- This method works for any label or search result in Gmail, not just your primary inbox. You can apply it to your 'Promotions,' 'Social,' or custom labels.
- To quickly find unread emails across all categories, type `is:unread` into the Gmail search bar, then apply the 'mark as read' action to the results.
- If you accidentally mark important emails as read, you can quickly find them using the search operator `is:read after:YYYY/MM/DD` (replace with the date you marked them as read) and then mark them as unread again.
- Consider using Gmail filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read, especially for newsletters or notifications you don't need to actively open.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you focus on truly new and important messages, improving your overall email management efficiency.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking all emails as read, especially if you have a very large and unorganized inbox. You might inadvertently overlook important messages that you haven't opened yet.
- ⚠️ This action is generally irreversible in bulk. While you can individually mark emails as unread, reverting a bulk 'mark as read' for thousands of emails can be time-consuming.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, while the interface is slightly different, you can achieve this on the mobile app. Long-press one email to select it, then tap the 'Select all' option (often a checkbox icon) at the top, and finally tap the 'Mark as read' icon.

**What if I only want to mark emails in a specific category as read?**

Navigate to the specific category (e.g., 'Promotions,' 'Social') or a custom label. Once you're in that view, follow the same steps to select all emails and then mark them as read. This will only affect emails within that specific category.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not move them to the trash or archive them. The emails will remain in your inbox or their respective labels.

**How do I mark a single email as unread again?**

To mark a single email as unread, open the email or select it from your inbox. Then, look for the 'Mark as unread' icon in the toolbar, which typically looks like an unopened envelope. Click this icon to revert its status.

**Why do I still see unread emails after following the steps?**

This usually happens if you only marked emails in one specific tab (like 'Primary') or if new emails arrived while you were performing the action. Check other tabs (Social, Promotions) or use the `is:unread` search operator to find any remaining unread messages.

**Can I schedule emails to be automatically marked as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a built-in feature to schedule emails to be marked as read at a future time. However, you can create filters that automatically mark *incoming* emails from specific senders or with certain keywords as read upon arrival.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut for marking emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in Gmail settings, you can select emails and then press `Shift + I` to mark them as read. To enable shortcuts, go to Settings (gear icon) > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts.
