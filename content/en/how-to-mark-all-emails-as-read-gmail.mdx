To mark all emails as read in Gmail, navigate to your inbox, select all visible emails using the top-left checkbox, then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link if prompted. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar. This process efficiently clears your unread count across your inbox or specific categories.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Your Gmail Inbox

Begin by navigating to mail.google.com in your web browser and logging into your Gmail account. Ensure you are in the inbox or the specific folder (like 'Promotions' or 'Updates') where you wish to mark emails as read. This initial step sets the stage for managing your email view.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on your screen, which typically amounts to 50 conversations per page. This is the first step in a bulk selection.

### Step 3: Select All Matching Conversations (If Applicable)

After selecting the visible emails, a yellow bar will appear at the top of your inbox. It will state something like, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' If you have more than 50 emails in that view, a link will also appear: 'Select all [number] conversations that match this search.' Click this link to select every email in your current view, whether it's your entire inbox or a specific label.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all desired emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appears above your email list. This icon typically looks like an open envelope or a closed envelope with a checkmark. Click on this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Gmail will often ask for confirmation, especially if you're marking a large number of emails as read. A small pop-up might appear asking, 'Are you sure you want to mark all conversations as read?' Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. The unread count for your inbox or label should now drop to zero, indicating success.

### Step 6: Mark Emails in a Specific Label as Read

To mark emails within a particular label (e.g., 'Social,' 'Promotions,' or a custom label) as read, first click on that label in the left sidebar. Once the label's contents are displayed, follow steps 2-5 to select all emails within that specific label and mark them as read. This is useful for clearing notifications for specific categories without affecting your primary inbox.

### Step 7: Mark Emails from a Search Query as Read

If you want to mark emails matching a specific search (e.g., 'from:sender@example.com' or 'subject:newsletter') as read, type your query into the Gmail search bar and press Enter. Once the results load, follow steps 2-5 to select all conversations that match your search and mark them as read. This is a powerful way to manage specific email threads or senders efficiently.


## Tips & Tricks

- Regularly clear your inbox: Make it a habit to process emails daily to prevent an overwhelming unread count.
- Use keyboard shortcuts: Enable keyboard shortcuts in Gmail settings, then press `x` to select an email and `Shift + i` to mark it as read.
- Filter and label: Create filters to automatically categorize incoming emails and apply labels, making it easier to manage large volumes.
- Undo accidentally marked emails: If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' notification at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action to revert it.
- Check other folders: Don't forget to check your Spam and Trash folders periodically, though emails there are often automatically marked as read or deleted.
- Consider 'Unread first' inbox: In Gmail settings, you can change your inbox type to 'Unread first' to always see your unread messages at the top, helping you prioritize.
- For those just beginning their Gmail journey, understanding [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) might be helpful to manage initial setups.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when selecting 'Select all conversations that match this search,' as it applies to *all* emails in your current view, which could be thousands. Ensure you only want to mark all of them as read before confirming, as undoing a massive action can be tedious.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, you can. On the mobile app, tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the selection circle for other emails you want to include. Finally, tap the three-dot menu icon and choose 'Mark as read.' Note that there isn't a single 'select all' button for the entire inbox like on the desktop version.

**What happens if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom left of your screen. Click it quickly to revert the action. If you miss the undo option, you'll need to manually search for those emails using the search bar (e.g., 'is:read from:sender@example.com') and mark them as unread again.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them or move them to the trash. They will remain in your inbox or their respective labels, simply without the 'unread' highlight.

**How do I find unread emails after marking everything as read?**

You can easily find any remaining unread emails by typing 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar and pressing Enter. This will display all emails that are currently marked as unread in your account, allowing you to quickly locate them.

**Can I mark specific emails as unread after marking them as read?**

Yes, absolutely. To mark specific emails as unread, simply select the email(s) you wish to change (using the checkbox next to them) and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon in the toolbar. This icon typically looks like a closed envelope.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is useful for 'clearing the slate' and getting your inbox count to zero, especially if you've been away, have a large backlog, or want to focus only on new incoming messages. It helps reduce mental clutter and provides a fresh starting point for managing your inbox.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Yes, you can create filters in Gmail to automatically mark incoming emails as read based on specific criteria such as the sender, subject, or keywords. To set this up, go to Gmail Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses, then click 'Create a new filter'.
