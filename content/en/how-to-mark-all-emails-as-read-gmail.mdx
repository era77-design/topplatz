To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first open your inbox. Select the checkbox at the top left to select all visible messages, then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link to include all emails across pages. Finally, use the 'Mark as read' button to clear your unread count instantly.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Enter your email address and password to log into your Gmail account. If you're using a new device or haven't logged in recently, you might need to complete a two-factor authentication step. Ensure you are logged into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read. If you don't have an account yet, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails in Your Current View

Once in your Gmail inbox, locate the small checkbox icon at the top left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all emails currently visible on the page. This action will typically select up to 50 conversations, depending on your display settings. A message will appear confirming the number of selected conversations.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations Across All Pages

After selecting all visible emails, a new line of text will appear above your emails, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link: 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection beyond the current page, encompassing every email in your current inbox view or category. This ensures you mark truly *all* emails as read.

### Step 4: Mark the Selected Emails as Read

With all desired emails selected, look for the toolbar located just above your email list. Among the various icons, find the 'Mark as read' button, which looks like an open envelope icon. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action will typically be instantaneous, and you'll notice the bold formatting of unread emails disappear.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action and Clear Unread Count

After clicking 'Mark as read,' a small notification will briefly appear at the bottom left of your screen, confirming 'X conversations marked as read.' Your unread count in the left sidebar next to 'Inbox' should now reflect the change, often dropping to zero if you've marked everything. You can scroll through your inbox to visually confirm that all previously unread emails no longer appear in bold text. This effectively clears your inbox's unread status.

### Step 6: Mark Emails as Read in Specific Categories or Search Results

If you only want to mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions, Social) or from a particular sender, navigate to that category or use the search bar at the top of Gmail. For example, type 'is:unread from:example.com' to find unread emails from a specific sender. Once the search results or category are displayed, follow steps 2-4 (Select All Visible, Select All Conversations, Mark as Read) to apply the action only to those filtered emails. This allows for more granular control over your inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly unmark emails as read, select them and click the closed envelope icon (Mark as unread) in the toolbar.
- Be careful when using 'Select all conversations that match this search' as it applies to ALL emails in that view, even those you might have missed.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' notification at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action. Click it to revert.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you manage inbox clutter and focus on new, important messages.
- Consider using Gmail's 'Categories' (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) to organize your inbox and make bulk actions easier.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking all emails as read is irreversible without the 'Undo' option. Ensure you truly want to mark all selected emails as read before proceeding, especially if you have important unread messages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, navigate to your inbox, then tap and hold an email to select it. Once selected, you'll see a checkbox appear on other emails. Tap the checkbox at the top left (or select multiple emails individually), then tap the three-dot menu icon and choose 'Mark as read.' There isn't a direct 'select all across pages' option like on desktop, so you'll need to scroll and select multiple batches.

**Can I mark emails as read without opening them?**

Yes, absolutely. You can select emails using the checkboxes next to them in your inbox list and then click the 'Mark as read' icon from the toolbar without ever opening the individual emails. This is the primary method for bulk marking emails as read.

**What's the difference between 'Mark as read' and 'Archive'?**

'Mark as read' simply changes the status of an email from unread to read, but it keeps the email in your inbox. 'Archive' removes the email from your inbox view and places it in 'All Mail,' effectively hiding it while still allowing you to search for it later. Archiving is a way to declutter your inbox without deleting.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is useful for clearing your unread count and achieving 'inbox zero' if you've been away, accumulated a large number of unimportant unread emails, or simply want a fresh start. It helps reduce mental clutter and allows you to focus on new incoming messages.

**Is there a shortcut to mark emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in Gmail settings, you can select emails using the 'x' key, and then press 'Shift + i' to mark them as read. This can be a very efficient way to manage your inbox once you're familiar with the shortcuts.

**Can I prevent certain emails from being marked as read automatically?**

Gmail does not have a feature to prevent specific emails from being marked as read when you perform a bulk action. However, you can use filters to move important emails to a specific label or star them, making them easier to find later even if their read status changes.

**How do I find unread emails after marking everything as read?**

Even after marking everything as read, you can still easily find any emails that were previously unread by using Gmail's search operators. Simply type 'is:unread' into the search bar at the top of your Gmail window, and it will display all emails that are currently marked as unread. This is useful if you mistakenly marked something important.
