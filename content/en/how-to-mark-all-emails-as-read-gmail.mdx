To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically use the search bar to filter unread messages, select all visible emails, and then click the 'Mark as read' button. This process helps you quickly clear your inbox and maintain better email organization.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in with your Google account credentials if you aren't already signed in. If you're new to Gmail, you might want to learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) to get started.

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface, type `is:unread` and press Enter. This action will filter your inbox to display only messages that you have not yet opened or marked as read. You can also combine this with other search operators, like `label:promotions is:unread` to target specific categories.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Unread Emails

Once the unread emails are displayed, locate the 'Select' checkbox at the top of your email list, just below the search bar. Click this checkbox to select all emails currently visible on the page (usually up to 50 emails per page).

### Step 4: Select ALL Matching Unread Emails

After clicking the 'Select' checkbox, a message will appear above your emails, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, you'll also see a link that says, 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to select every single unread email in your entire inbox that matches your search criteria, not just those on the current page.

### Step 5: Mark Emails as Read

With all unread emails selected, look for the 'More' (three vertical dots) or the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) in the toolbar above your email list. Click this icon. A confirmation message will appear, and all selected emails will be marked as read, disappearing from your unread filter.

### Step 6: Confirm the Change

After the operation, you should see a notification confirming that the emails have been marked as read. You can then clear the `is:unread` search query from the search bar to return to your full inbox view. Your inbox should now show no unread messages, or significantly fewer if you only marked a subset.


## Tips & Tricks

- **Use Specific Filters**: If you only want to mark unread emails in a specific folder or category (e.g., 'Promotions' or 'Social'), combine `is:unread` with `category:promotions` or `label:yourlabel`.
- **Undo Mistakes**: If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' notification that briefly appears at the bottom left of your screen after the action. Click it quickly to revert the change.
- **Mobile App**: The process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. Search for `is:unread`, then tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the 'select all' option (usually three dots or a checkbox at the top), and finally, tap the 'Mark as read' icon.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Enable keyboard shortcuts in Gmail settings (Settings > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts) to quickly select all (Shift+A) and mark as read (Shift+I) within a filtered view.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when selecting 'Select all conversations that match this search' as it will apply the action to *all* emails matching your filter, which could be thousands. Double-check your search query if you only intend to mark a subset.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark only a specific category of emails as read?**

Yes, you can. In the search bar, combine `is:unread` with the category name, for example, `is:unread category:promotions`. This will filter only unread emails within that specific category, allowing you to mark them as read independently.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification appears at the bottom left of your Gmail screen for a few seconds. Click it quickly to reverse the action. If you miss it, you'll have to manually find and mark those emails as unread again.

**Does this process work on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the principle is the same. On the mobile app, you'll search for `is:unread`, then typically long-press one email to enable selection, then look for an option to select all (often a checkbox or three dots), and finally, tap the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users do this to achieve 'inbox zero' or to clear out old, non-essential unread messages, especially after a vacation or a period of high email volume. This helps them focus on new, incoming emails without distraction from older unread items.

**Can I mark emails as read in bulk without selecting them?**

No, you must select the emails first before applying the 'Mark as read' action. The search and select functionality is designed to help you efficiently select a large number of emails at once for bulk actions.

**How do I find emails I've already marked as read?**

You can find read emails by simply navigating through your inbox or specific labels. If you need to find *only* read emails that you've previously opened or marked, you can use the search operator `is:read` in the Gmail search bar.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

While not directly 'mark as read' upon arrival, you can set up filters to automatically archive or delete certain incoming emails. This effectively prevents them from appearing as unread in your primary inbox. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses to create new filters.
