To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox and click the checkbox at the top left to select all visible emails. If you wish to select all conversations beyond the current view, click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' prompt. Then, click the 'Mark as read' icon, resembling an open envelope, from the toolbar to quickly declutter your inbox.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- Gmail account (If you don't have one yet, learn how to get started with [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).)

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail and Sign In

Begin by opening your web browser and navigating to gmail.com. If you're not already signed in, enter your email address or phone number and your password to access your inbox. Ensure you're logging into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Access Your Desired Inbox View

Once signed in, you'll typically land on your Primary inbox. You can choose to mark emails as read in this main view, or switch to other categories like Social, Promotions, or Updates by clicking on their respective tabs at the top of your inbox. This allows for targeted inbox management.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Look for the square checkbox located at the top of your email list, just below the search bar. Click this checkbox once. This action will instantly select all emails currently displayed on your screen, typically around 50 conversations per page.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations (If Applicable)

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear just above your email list, stating 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, if you want to mark *all* emails in that category or search result as read, click the blue text link that says 'Select all conversations that match this search.' This ensures even emails not currently displayed are included.

### Step 5: Click "Mark as read" Icon

With your emails selected, a new toolbar will appear above your email list. Locate the 'Mark as read' icon, which looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. Gmail will process this request.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails and your settings, Gmail may ask for confirmation, especially for large batches. A small pop-up might appear at the bottom left of your screen confirming the action, like 'Conversations marked as read.' This indicates the process is complete.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox

Take a moment to check your inbox. All the emails you selected should now appear in their read state, usually without bold text or a highlighted background. This helps you quickly identify any new, truly unread messages that arrive subsequently.


## Tips & Tricks

- **Use Search Operators for Specificity**: If you only want to mark emails from a particular sender or with a specific keyword as read, use Gmail's search bar (e.g., `from:sender@example.com is:unread` or `label:promotions is:unread`). Then, apply the 'Mark as read' action to the search results.
- **Undo If You Made a Mistake**: If you accidentally mark an important email as read, look for the 'Undo' button in the pop-up notification at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action. This button usually appears for a few seconds and can revert the change.
- **Regularly Tidy Your Inbox**: Making a habit of marking irrelevant emails as read or archiving them can significantly reduce inbox clutter. Consider setting aside a few minutes each day or week to manage your email volume effectively.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking all emails as read, especially if you have a very large inbox. Important unread messages might get overlooked if you don't review them beforehand. It's often better to sort by 'unread' first if you're looking for specific items.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark emails as read on my phone using the Gmail app?**

Yes, the Gmail app for both Android and iOS allows you to mark multiple emails as read. Simply tap and hold an email to select it, then tap additional emails, and finally, tap the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the top toolbar.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

If you realize immediately, look for the 'Undo' option that appears briefly at the bottom of your screen. If you miss it, you can manually find the email and mark it as unread again by selecting it and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).

**How do I mark emails as unread again in Gmail?**

To mark an email as unread, simply select the email (or multiple emails) in your inbox. Then, click the 'Mark as unread' icon, which looks like a closed envelope, in the toolbar above your email list. This will revert their status.

**Can I mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

Absolutely. Navigate to the desired category tab (like 'Promotions' or 'Social'). Then, follow the same steps: select all visible emails using the top checkbox, click 'Select all conversations that match this search,' and finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Does marking emails as read delete them from my inbox?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read' within your inbox. It does not delete or archive them. The emails remain in your inbox or their respective categories until you choose to delete or archive them manually.

**Why would someone want to mark all emails as read?**

People often do this to achieve 'inbox zero,' declutter their email, or clear out a backlog of old, less important emails that they've already seen but haven't formally marked as read. It helps create a clean slate for new incoming messages.

**Is there a way to automate marking emails as read in Gmail?**

While there's no direct 'mark all future emails as read' setting, you can use Gmail filters. Create a filter that matches certain criteria (e.g., sender, subject) and set the action to 'Mark as read' for incoming messages. This can help manage specific types of emails automatically.
