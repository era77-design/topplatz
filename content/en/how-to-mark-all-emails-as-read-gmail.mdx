To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, click the 'Select all' checkbox at the top left, and then click the 'Select all X conversations in Primary' link that appears. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the toolbar above your emails. This efficiently clears your unread count.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Start by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on a computer. While similar functionality exists on mobile, the desktop interface offers the most straightforward way to perform bulk actions like marking all emails as read. Ensure you are logged into the Gmail account you wish to manage. If you need to set up an account, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox or Label

By default, you'll land in your 'Primary' inbox. If you want to mark all emails as read in a different category (e.g., Social, Promotions) or a specific label, click on that category tab or label from the left-hand menu. This ensures that the subsequent selection applies only to the emails within that particular view.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Look for the small square checkbox located at the top-left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on your screen, which typically includes the first 50 conversations in that view.

### Step 4: Select All Matching Emails in the View

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear just above your email list, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link: 'Select all X conversations in Primary' (or your current label/category). Click this link to extend your selection to *all* emails matching your current view, not just those on the first page.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all relevant emails are selected, a new toolbar will appear above your email list. Look for the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope or a closed envelope with a checkmark. Click this icon. This action will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action (If Prompted)

For very large selections, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up asking if you are sure you want to perform this action on all selected conversations. If you see this, click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. The process may take a few moments to complete, depending on the number of emails being updated.

### Step 7: Review Your Inbox

After the process is complete, your unread email count for that inbox or label should significantly decrease or disappear entirely. You can scroll through your emails to verify that they no longer appear bold (which indicates an unread status). This provides a clean slate for managing new incoming messages.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark specific individual emails as read, simply hover over the email in your list and click the 'Mark as read' eye icon that appears on the right.
- Use Gmail's powerful search operators (e.g., `is:unread`, `label:promotions is:unread`) to filter emails before selecting them, allowing for more targeted bulk actions.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, you can quickly undo the action by clicking the 'Undo' notification that briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen after the action.
- Periodically marking all emails as read can help you achieve 'inbox zero' and focus on new, urgent communications without the distraction of a high unread count.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Once you mark a large number of emails as read, it can be challenging to distinguish truly unread messages from those you haven't yet processed. Use this feature judiciously.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, you can. On the mobile app, you need to long-press an email to select it, then tap the checkbox icon to select multiple. However, there isn't a single 'select all in this view' button like on desktop; you must manually select emails in batches.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

If you act quickly, Gmail provides an 'Undo' option at the bottom-left of your screen immediately after the action. If you miss the undo, you'll need to manually find and mark those specific emails as unread by selecting them and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete, archive, or move them from your inbox. They will remain in their current location unless you take further action.

**How can I mark only unread emails as read?**

When you use the 'Select all X conversations in Primary' link, Gmail primarily focuses on the current view. If you want to specifically target *only* unread emails, first type `is:unread` into the search bar, then follow the steps to select all and mark as read.

**Will this work for archived emails?**

This method works for emails within your current view or search results. If you want to mark archived emails as read, you would first need to search for them (e.g., `in:archive is:unread`) and then apply the 'mark as read' action to those search results.

**Is there a way to mark all emails as unread again?**

Yes, the process is similar. Select the emails you wish to change (either individually or in bulk using the 'select all' method), and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon, which typically looks like a closed envelope.

**Can I mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

Absolutely. Use Gmail's search bar to filter emails from a specific sender (e.g., `from:sender@example.com`). Once the search results are displayed, you can then use the 'select all' and 'mark as read' functions to apply the action only to those emails.
