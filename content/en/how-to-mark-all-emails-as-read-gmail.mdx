To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the search bar to filter unread messages and then select them all to change their status. This process works efficiently on both desktop and mobile versions of Gmail, helping you quickly clear your inbox.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Start by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on your computer. Make sure you are logged into the Gmail account you wish to manage. If you haven't created an account yet, you can learn how by following our guide on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account). Ensure you're accessing the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail addresses.

> 💡 **Tip:** Always double-check you are logged into the correct Gmail account before performing bulk actions.

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface, type `is:unread` and press Enter. This command will filter your inbox to display only the emails that are currently marked as unread. This is the crucial first step to identify all messages that need their status changed. You can combine this with other search operators, like `in:inbox is:unread`, to refine your search further if needed.

> 💡 **Tip:** Using specific search operators like `is:unread` is key to efficiently targeting unread messages.

### Step 3: Select All Displayed Unread Emails

After the search results load, locate the 'Select' checkbox. This is usually found at the top left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all the unread emails currently visible on the page. By default, Gmail displays 50 conversations per page, so this initially selects only those visible.

> 💡 **Tip:** The 'Select' checkbox is your gateway to performing bulk actions on your emails.

### Step 4: Select ALL Unread Emails (Beyond the Current Page)

If you have more than 50 unread emails, a message will appear after you've selected the current page's emails. It will say something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all X conversations that match this search.' Click on the second part of this message (e.g., 'Select all X conversations that match this search') to extend your selection to *all* unread emails in your entire inbox, regardless of how many pages they span.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is vital for ensuring you truly mark *all* unread emails as read, not just those on the first page.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all your unread emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your email list. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. The toolbar appears once emails are selected; if you don't see it, double-check your selection.

> 💡 **Tip:** The 'Mark as read' icon is your primary tool for changing email status.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails, Gmail might ask for confirmation, especially if you're processing a large batch. Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' in the pop-up dialogue box to proceed. Once confirmed, all the previously unread emails will now be marked as read, and your inbox should reflect this change by showing fewer or no unread messages. It might take a moment for Gmail to update the status of all emails, especially if you have thousands.

> 💡 **Tip:** Always confirm bulk actions to prevent accidental changes.

### Step 7: Mark All Emails as Read on Mobile (Gmail App)

For the Gmail mobile app, open the app and tap the search bar at the top. Type `is:unread` and search. Once the unread emails appear, you'll need to long-press on one email to select it, then tap the circle next to other emails to select them individually. Unfortunately, there isn't a 'select all' button for search results on mobile. After selecting your desired emails, tap the three-dot menu (more options) and choose 'Mark as read.'

> 💡 **Tip:** For extensive bulk actions on mobile, consider accessing Gmail through a mobile browser and requesting the desktop site for full functionality.


## Tips & Tricks

- Regularly manage your inbox by marking emails as read or archiving them to maintain a clean and organized workspace.
- Utilize Gmail's powerful search operators (e.g., `from:sender@example.com`, `has:attachment`) to quickly find specific emails.
- Set up filters to automatically mark certain emails as read, archive them, or move them to specific labels upon arrival.
- Consider using the 'Inbox Zero' method to keep your inbox empty by processing or archiving all emails.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, you can quickly undo the action using the 'Undo' notification that appears briefly after the change, or by searching for them and marking them as unread again.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking a large number of emails as read, especially if you use your inbox to track pending actions. Once marked as read, they might be overlooked.
- ⚠️ Ensure you are logged into the correct Gmail account before performing bulk actions to avoid unintended changes to another account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails in a specific label or folder as read?**

Yes, you can. First, navigate to the desired label or folder in the left sidebar. Then, use the `is:unread` search operator within that specific label, and follow the same steps to select all and mark as read.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Absolutely! You can create a filter in Gmail to automatically mark incoming emails from specific senders or with certain keywords as read. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses to set this up.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

After performing the action, a small 'Undo' notification usually appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click it quickly to revert the change. If you miss it, you'll need to manually find those emails and mark them as unread again.

**Why doesn't the 'Select all conversations that match this search' option appear for me?**

This option only appears if you have more emails matching your search criteria than are currently displayed on the page (usually 50). If you only have 50 or fewer unread emails, selecting the checkbox will select them all.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read.' It does not delete them or move them from your inbox or any labels. They will remain in your account until you explicitly delete them.

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail app more easily than individually selecting?**

Unfortunately, the Gmail mobile app doesn't have a 'select all' option for search results like the desktop version. You'll need to manually select multiple emails by long-pressing one, then tapping others. For bulk actions, the desktop interface or a mobile browser requesting the desktop site is more efficient.

**How do I find all my unread emails again after marking them as read?**

Once emails are marked as read, they will no longer appear when you search `is:unread`. To find them again, you would need to use other search criteria like sender, subject, or date, or browse your inbox/archives.
