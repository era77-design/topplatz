To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically select all visible emails in a view (like your inbox or a specific label) and then use the 'Mark as read' option from the toolbar. This helps you quickly clear notifications and organize your inbox, making it easier to focus on new messages.

## What You Need

- A Gmail account
- Internet access
- A computer or mobile device

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.) and navigate to mail.google.com. If you're not already logged in, enter your Gmail email address or phone number and password. Ensure you are in the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail profiles. If you don't have an account, you can learn [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Choose the Email View to Clear

Once logged in, decide which section of your Gmail you want to mark as read. This could be your main 'Inbox', 'All Mail', a specific 'Label' you've created, or even search results. Click on the relevant section in the left-hand sidebar to display the emails you wish to mark as read.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Look for the square checkbox icon at the top of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on that page. If you have more than one page of emails in the selected view, a yellow bar will appear at the top, offering to 'Select all conversations that match this search' to select *all* emails in that view, not just those on the current page.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

After selecting your emails, a new toolbar will appear above your email list. Look for the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon. This action will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read, removing the bold formatting and unread count.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action (if prompted)

For large batches of emails, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up or a yellow notification bar at the bottom-left of your screen. Confirm the action if prompted to proceed. It might take a moment for the changes to process and for your unread count to update, especially if you've marked thousands of emails as read.

### Step 6: Mark All Emails as Read on Gmail Mobile App

Open the Gmail app on your Android or iOS device. Navigate to the desired folder (Inbox, All Mail, etc.). To select multiple emails, tap and hold on one email, then tap on other emails to select them. Once selected, tap the three-dot menu (⋮ or ...) at the top-right, then choose 'Mark as read'. Note that there isn't a direct 'select all' button for an entire folder on mobile like on desktop, but you can select many at once.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use search operators like `is:unread` or `from:sender@example.com` in the search bar to filter emails before selecting them, allowing you to mark specific groups as read.
- Regularly archiving or deleting old emails can also help keep your inbox tidy, complementing the 'mark as read' function by reducing clutter.
- Consider using Gmail's 'Categories' and 'Tabs' (Primary, Social, Promotions) to automatically sort incoming mail, reducing the need to manually mark large batches as read.
- If you accidentally mark important emails as read, you can easily mark them back as unread by selecting them and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be careful when using the 'Select all conversations that match this search' option, as it applies the action to *all* emails in that view, not just the page you see. Ensure you don't accidentally mark important unread emails as read without reviewing them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read in the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, but it requires selecting emails individually or in batches. Tap and hold an email, then tap others to select, then use the three-dot menu to 'Mark as read'. There isn't a single 'select all' button for an entire folder like on the desktop version.

**What happens if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

Don't worry, you can easily undo this. Simply select the emails you want to change back, and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) from the toolbar that appears above your email list.

**How do I mark only unread emails as read?**

To specifically target unread emails, type `is:unread` into the Gmail search bar and press Enter. This will display only your unread messages, allowing you to use the top checkbox to select them all and mark them as read.

**Can I mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

Yes. Use the search bar to find emails from a specific sender (e.g., `from:sender@example.com`). Once the results appear, use the top checkbox to select them, and then click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Why isn't the 'Mark as read' option appearing?**

Ensure you have at least one email selected. The contextual toolbar, including the 'Mark as read' icon, only appears once one or more emails are chosen. If you've selected all emails on a page and still don't see it, try refreshing your browser or checking your Gmail settings.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them or move them to the trash. They remain in their current folder or label, just without the unread indicator.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut to mark emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in your Gmail settings. After selecting an email or multiple emails, you can press `Shift + I` to mark them as read. You can enable shortcuts in Gmail Settings > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts.
