To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, select the checkbox at the top of the email list to select all visible messages, then click the "Mark as read" icon (an open envelope). For more extensive selections, an additional prompt will appear, allowing you to select all conversations in that view.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- An internet connection
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Navigate to mail.google.com and log in to your Gmail account. If you don't have an account yet, or need a refresher on setting one up, check out our guide on [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account). Once logged in, you'll be in your primary inbox view, or whichever label you last viewed.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Look for a small checkbox at the top left of your email list, just above the first message. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on your screen, typically 50 or 100 messages depending on your display settings. This is the first step towards batch processing your emails.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in Current View (Optional)

After clicking the top checkbox, a message will appear above your emails: "All [number] conversations on this page are selected." Immediately following this, you'll see a link that says, "Select all [larger number] conversations in [Current View/Primary/Inbox]." Click this link if you want to select every email within your current view or label, not just those visible on the page.

### Step 4: Locate and Click the "Mark as Read" Icon

Once emails are selected, a new toolbar will appear above your email list. Look for the "Mark as read" icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. It's usually found among other action icons like archive, delete, report spam, and move to. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Gmail will process your request. If you selected a very large number of emails, this might take a few moments. A confirmation message will briefly appear at the bottom left of your screen, indicating how many conversations were marked as read. All selected emails will now appear as 'read' (their text will typically change from bold to regular font).

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Labels or Categories (Optional)

If you want to mark emails as read in other labels (e.g., 'Social', 'Promotions', 'Updates', or custom labels), navigate to those labels using the left sidebar. Repeat steps 2-5 within each desired label to mark their contents as read. This allows you to manage different sections of your inbox independently.


## Tips & Tricks

- **Use Search Filters:** To mark specific subsets of emails as read, use Gmail's powerful search filters (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com` or `label:promotions is:unread`). Select the results and mark them as read.
- **Undo Quickly:** If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the "Undo" button that briefly appears at the bottom left of your screen after the action. Click it immediately to revert the change.
- **Keyboard Shortcut:** For quicker actions, once emails are selected, you can press `Shift + I` (the letter 'i') on your keyboard to mark them as read. Ensure keyboard shortcuts are enabled in your Gmail settings.
- **Mobile App:** The process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. Select emails by tapping and holding one, then tap others to select multiple. Then, use the three-dot menu or the open envelope icon to mark them as read. For bulk selection, the web interface is often more efficient.
- **Batch Actions in 'All Mail':** To select all emails in your entire Gmail account (including archived messages), go to the "All Mail" label on the left sidebar and follow the steps for selecting all conversations.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ **Review Before Marking:** Be cautious when marking a large number of emails as read without reviewing them, especially if you have important messages that you still need to act upon. Once marked, they won't stand out as unread.
- ⚠️ **No Deletion:** Marking emails as read does not delete them. They remain in your inbox or respective labels. If you wish to delete them, you'll need to use the trash icon after selecting them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps declutter your inbox visually, making it easier to identify truly new or unread messages that require your immediate attention. It can also give you a sense of a "fresh start" or help you process old, already-seen messages.

**Can I undo marking emails as read if I make a mistake?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small "Undo" button will appear at the bottom left of your Gmail screen. Click it quickly (within a few seconds) to revert the action and restore the unread status to those emails.

**Does marking emails as read delete them from my account?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, archive them, or move them to any other folder. They will remain in their current location in your Gmail account.

**How do I mark only *unread* emails as read?**

You can use Gmail's powerful search bar for this. Type `is:unread` and hit Enter. This will display only your unread emails. Then, use the checkbox at the top to select all displayed messages and click the "Mark as read" icon.

**Is it possible to mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

While you can mark multiple emails as read on the Gmail mobile app by selecting them and using the 'mark as read' option, there isn't a direct 'select all conversations' link like on the desktop version. For very large batches, the web interface is generally more efficient.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

Use the search bar to filter emails from that specific sender (e.g., `from:example@domain.com`). Once the search results are displayed, you can then select all the messages and click the "Mark as read" icon to process only those emails.

**Will marking all emails as read affect my Gmail storage?**

No, marking emails as read has no impact on your Gmail storage whatsoever. Storage is consumed by the size of the emails themselves and their attachments, regardless of whether they are marked as read or unread.
