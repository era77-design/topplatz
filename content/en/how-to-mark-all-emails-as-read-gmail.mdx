To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, click the "Select all" checkbox, then click "Select all conversations that match this search" if prompted, and finally choose the "Mark as read" option. This process helps you quickly clear your unread count and manage your inbox efficiently.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in using your email address and password if you aren't already signed in. Ensure you are viewing the specific inbox, label, or category where you intend to mark emails as read. If you don't have an account yet, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox situated at the top-left corner of your email list, directly above the first email. Click this checkbox once. This action will select all conversations currently displayed on the page, which typically defaults to 50 emails.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations Across Pages

If you have more than 50 emails in your current view, a message will appear above your email list after selecting the visible emails. It will state, "All 50 conversations on this page are selected." Immediately below this, you'll see a crucial option: "Select all conversations that match this search." Click this link to extend your selection to every email in your current folder or search result, spanning multiple pages.

### Step 4: Mark as Read

With all your desired emails now selected, look for the "Mark as read" icon in the toolbar that appears above your email list. This icon commonly looks like an open envelope with a checkmark or a closed envelope being opened. Click on this icon to initiate the action.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails being processed, a confirmation pop-up or a brief notification might appear. Confirm your choice to proceed with marking the selected emails as read. Gmail will then process your request, and your unread email count should update, reflecting the change.

### Step 6: Filter and Mark Specific Emails

If you only want to mark specific emails as read, such as those from a particular sender or containing a certain keyword, use the Gmail search bar at the top. Type your query (e.g., "from:example@domain.com" or "label:promotions"), then follow steps 2-4 to select and mark only those filtered results as read. This allows for more targeted inbox management.


## Tips & Tricks

- This method works for any label, category tab (like Promotions or Social), or search result in Gmail, not just the primary inbox.
- To quickly undo the action, look for the "Undo" notification that briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen after marking emails as read and click it immediately.
- Regularly marking emails as read can significantly help in maintaining an organized inbox and reducing digital clutter, making it easier to find important messages.
- You can also mark individual emails as read by right-clicking on them (on desktop) and selecting "Mark as read" from the context menu.
- Utilize Gmail's search operators (e.g., `is:unread`, `before:YYYY/MM/DD`) to refine your selection before marking emails as read.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Exercise caution when marking a large number of emails as read, especially if you have important unread messages that still require your attention. The 'Undo' notification is time-sensitive and once it disappears, reversing the action for many emails can be tedious.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can select multiple emails by long-pressing one, then tapping others. However, selecting all emails across multiple pages is generally easier and more efficient using the web interface on a computer.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

After marking emails as read, a small "Undo" notification typically appears at the bottom-left of your screen for a few seconds. Click it immediately to reverse the action. If it disappears, you'll need to manually search for the email and mark it as unread again.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, archive them, or move them to the trash. They will remain in your inbox or their respective labels.

**How do I mark emails as unread after I've marked them as read?**

You can select the specific email(s) you wish to mark as unread (even if they are already read) and then click the "Mark as unread" icon in the toolbar, which typically looks like a closed envelope.

**Can I mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions, Social) as read?**

Yes, navigate to the specific category tab (e.g., Promotions or Social) within your Gmail inbox. Once in that tab, follow the same steps (select all visible, then select all matching search) to mark all emails within that category as read.

**Why does Gmail only select 50 emails at a time initially?**

Gmail defaults to displaying 50 conversations per page to optimize loading times and ensure a smooth user interface experience. This is why you need to explicitly click the "Select all conversations that match this search" link to extend the selection beyond the current page.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut to mark emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in your Gmail settings, you can select the desired emails and then press `Shift + I` to mark them as read. To enable shortcuts, go to Settings > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts and ensure they are turned on.
