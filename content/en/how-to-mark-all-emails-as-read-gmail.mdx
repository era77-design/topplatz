Marking all emails as read in Gmail is a quick way to clear your inbox of old, unread messages and start fresh. This process is particularly useful when you've been away, have a backlog of promotional emails, or simply want to achieve an 'inbox zero' appearance. Follow these easy steps to manage your Gmail inbox effectively.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Browser

Start by navigating to mail.google.com in your web browser and log in to your Gmail account. You'll typically land in your 'Primary' inbox tab, but these steps can be applied to any category (Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) or custom labels you have.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

In your inbox, locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner, just above your email list. Click this checkbox to select all the emails currently visible on your page. Gmail usually displays 50 conversations per page by default.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in Your Inbox

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear just above your inbox, stating 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, you'll also see an option that says, 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to select *all* unread emails in your current view or category, not just the ones on the first page.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Button

Once all relevant emails are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click it to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Bulk Action (If Prompted)

For very large selections, Gmail might ask you to confirm this bulk action. A pop-up will appear asking 'Are you sure you want to take this action on X conversations?' Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. This ensures you intend to mark a large number of emails as read.

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Categories or Labels (Optional)

If you use Gmail's tabbed inbox (Primary, Social, Promotions, etc.), you will need to repeat these steps for each tab to mark all emails as read across your entire inbox. Similarly, if you want to mark emails within a specific label as read, navigate to that label and follow the same process.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox Status

After completing the steps, your inbox should now show significantly fewer, or no, unread emails in the selected category or label. Take a moment to scroll through and ensure the action was applied as intended, giving you a cleaner, more organized view.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use the search bar with `is:unread` to quickly filter and view only your unread emails across all categories or labels before marking them as read.
- If you accidentally mark important emails as read, you can usually undo the action immediately after by clicking the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom left of your screen.
- To mark only specific emails as read, individually select their checkboxes and then click the 'Mark as read' icon.
- This method marks emails as read but does not archive or delete them. They will still be accessible in your inbox or respective labels.
- Consider setting up filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read if you consistently receive messages you don't need to open.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when selecting 'Select all conversations that match this search' as it applies the action to *all* emails, potentially thousands, and can take a moment to process.
- ⚠️ Always double-check your selection before performing a bulk action like 'Mark as read' to avoid missing potentially important emails.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can long-press an email to select it, then tap the checkbox icon to select multiple. To select all, tap the three-dot menu, but you might need to scroll down to load more emails to select them all in one go, as there isn't a 'select all conversations' option like on desktop.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

You can use Gmail's search function for this. Type `from:sender@example.com is:unread` into the search bar. This will display only unread emails from that specific sender. Then, use the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link and click 'Mark as read'.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read simply changes their status from unread to read. The emails remain in your inbox or their respective labels/folders. It does not delete, archive, or move them.

**I just created a new Gmail account, and it feels overwhelming already. How can I keep it organized from the start?**

When you [create a new Gmail account](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account), it's a great opportunity to set up good habits. After marking any initial welcome emails as read to get a clean slate, consider immediately creating filters and labels for incoming mail. This helps sort emails as they arrive, reducing future clutter.

**How can I mark emails in different tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions) as read?**

You need to navigate to each tab individually. Click on the 'Social' tab, then follow the steps to select all and mark as read. Repeat this for 'Promotions,' 'Updates,' and any other tabs you use to ensure all emails across your inbox are marked as read.

**Is there a way to automatically mark future emails as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a direct feature to automatically mark *all* future emails as read. However, you can create filters for specific senders or keywords to automatically mark *those* incoming emails as read, or even archive them, bypassing your inbox entirely.

**Can I mark emails as read without opening them?**

Yes, the method described in this guide allows you to mark multiple emails as read without needing to open each one individually. You simply select them using the checkboxes and then apply the 'Mark as read' action.
