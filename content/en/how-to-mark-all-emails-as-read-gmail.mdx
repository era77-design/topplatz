To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first navigate to your desired inbox or label on a desktop browser. Then, use the select-all checkbox at the top of your email list and click the subsequent "Select all X conversations in this [label/search]" link. Finally, click the "Mark as read" icon (an open envelope) to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Log In to Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and go to mail.google.com. Enter your email address or phone number and password to log in. If you're new to Gmail, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) to get started. Ensure you are accessing the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail profiles.

> 💡 **Tip:** Always verify you are logging into the intended Gmail account to avoid marking the wrong emails as read.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox or Label

Once logged in, you'll see your primary inbox. If you wish to mark emails as read within a specific category (like Promotions, Social, or a custom label you've created), click on that category or label in the left sidebar. You can also use the search bar to filter for a particular set of emails you want to manage.

> 💡 **Tip:** Using specific labels helps you manage distinct email categories without affecting your entire inbox.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails on the Page

At the top of your email list, just above the first email, locate and click the small square checkbox. This action will select all emails currently displayed on the page, which typically defaults to 50 conversations. This is the initial step before selecting all emails in the entire category.

> 💡 **Tip:** The checkbox is a quick way to initiate a bulk action on a visible set of emails.

### Step 4: Select All Emails in the Current View/Category

After clicking the initial checkbox, a message will appear just above your emails, stating something like "All 50 conversations on this page are selected." Crucially, you will then see a clickable link that says, "Select all X conversations in this [Primary/Promotions/label name] / [search results]." Click this link to extend your selection to *all* emails within that specific inbox, label, or search result, not just the first page.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is essential to mark all emails as read across your entire selected category, not just the current page.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all relevant emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your email list. Identify the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically resembles an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action will clear their bold formatting and remove them from your unread count.

> 💡 **Tip:** Hovering your mouse over the icons in the toolbar will display their function, helping you find the correct one quickly.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

A small notification will momentarily appear in the bottom-left corner of your screen, confirming that 'X conversations have been marked as read.' Depending on the total number of emails, this process might take a few seconds to complete. Your unread count for that inbox or label should now reflect the change.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you marked emails as read by mistake, you can often click 'Undo' in the notification bar immediately after the action.


## Tips & Tricks

- Regularly clear your inbox by marking emails as read to maintain a clutter-free and organized email environment.
- Utilize Gmail's powerful search operators like `is:unread` or `label:unread` to quickly locate specific unread messages or categories.
- Consider setting up Gmail filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read or move them to designated labels, reducing manual effort.
- On mobile devices, the process is similar: long-press an email to select it, then tap other emails, and use the menu (often three dots) to find the 'Mark as read' option.
- Be cautious when marking a large number of emails as read; quickly scan for anything critical you might need to address before performing the bulk action.
- If you want to create a new Gmail account for better organization, refer to our guide on [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Once emails are marked as read, they will no longer appear in your 'Unread' filter. Ensure you've reviewed anything critical before performing a mass mark as read.
- ⚠️ The 'Undo' option is only available for a short time after the action is performed. If you miss it, you'll need to manually mark individual emails as unread again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, you can. In the Gmail app, long-press on an email to select it, then tap the selection circles for other emails you wish to include. Finally, tap the 'Mark as read' icon (often an open envelope or found within a three-dot menu) to change their status.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

If you act quickly, an 'Undo' option might appear in a notification bar after the action. Otherwise, you can use the Gmail search bar to find the specific emails and manually mark them as unread again by selecting them and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, move them to the trash, or archive them. They remain in your inbox or their respective labels.

**How can I find all unread emails after marking everything as read?**

You can easily find all currently unread emails across your entire account by typing `is:unread` into the Gmail search bar and pressing Enter. This will display every message still marked as unread.

**Can I mark emails in a specific label as read without affecting my entire inbox?**

Absolutely. Navigate to the specific label or category in the left sidebar (e.g., 'Promotions' or a custom label). Then, follow the steps outlined above to select all emails within that particular label and mark them as read. This action will only apply to emails within that specific category.

**Why does Gmail only select 50 emails at first?**

Gmail displays emails in pages, usually 50 per page, to optimize loading times and performance, especially for users with large inboxes. The crucial 'Select all X conversations in this [label/search]' link is what allows you to extend the selection beyond the current page to the entire category.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut to mark emails as read in Gmail?**

Yes, if keyboard shortcuts are enabled in your Gmail settings. You can select one or more emails and then press `Shift + I` on your keyboard to mark them as read. To enable shortcuts, go to Settings (gear icon) > See all settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts.
