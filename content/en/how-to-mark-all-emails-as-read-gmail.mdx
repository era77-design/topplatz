Marking all emails as read in Gmail is a fast and effective way to declutter your inbox and achieve an 'inbox zero' state, especially when dealing with a large volume of unread messages. This action changes the status of unread emails to read, helping you visually organize your communication without deleting any important information. If you've recently gone through the process of [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account), keeping your inbox tidy from the start is a great habit.

## What You Need

- Computer or smartphone with internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Start by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on a computer. Log in to your Gmail account if you are not already signed in. This method works best for managing a large volume of emails efficiently.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Folder or Label

Decide which section of your Gmail you wish to clear. This could be your main 'Inbox', a specific category like 'Promotions' or 'Social', or a custom label you've created. Click on the relevant folder or label from the left-hand menu to view its contents.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Look for the small square checkbox located at the top-left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all the emails currently visible on your screen. This typically selects up to 50 conversations at once.

### Step 4: Select ALL Emails in the Folder

After selecting the visible emails, a banner will appear at the top of your email list, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also offer an option: 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click on this link to extend your selection to *all* emails within that specific folder or label, not just those currently displayed.

### Step 5: Locate the 'Mark as Read' Button

Once all your desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your email list. You'll find several icons here. The 'Mark as read' icon typically looks like an open envelope. Alternatively, you might need to click the 'More' icon (three vertical dots) to reveal a dropdown menu containing the 'Mark as read' option.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Click on the 'Mark as read' icon or select it from the 'More' menu. Gmail will then begin processing your request. For a very large number of emails, this might take a few moments to complete. You may see a notification at the bottom left of your screen indicating the progress.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox

After the process is complete, check your inbox or the specific folder you targeted. All the selected emails should now appear in plain text (not bold), indicating they have been marked as read. Your inbox should now look much tidier.


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark only specific unread emails as read, simply click the checkbox next to each email you want to select before clicking the 'Mark as read' button.
- Consider using Gmail's search filters (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com`) to narrow down your selection before marking emails as read.
- Regularly organize your inbox by archiving or deleting emails you no longer need, rather than just marking them as read, to maintain long-term email hygiene.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking all emails as read, especially in your main inbox. Ensure you don't inadvertently overlook important unread messages that require your attention.
- ⚠️ While you can typically mark individual emails back as unread, marking a large batch of emails as read simultaneously can make it harder to identify specific important messages you might have missed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps to declutter your inbox, reduce visual noise, and achieve 'inbox zero.' It can provide a fresh starting point for managing new incoming messages without deleting older, potentially useful emails.

**Can I undo marking emails as read?**

Yes, you can mark emails back as unread. After performing the 'mark as read' action, a small 'Undo' notification briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen. If you miss this, you'll need to manually select the emails and click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).

**How do I mark only specific emails as read in Gmail?**

To mark specific emails as read, simply click the individual checkbox next to each email you wish to select. Once your desired emails are checked, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your email list.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them from your Gmail account. The emails remain in their respective folders or labels until you choose to archive or delete them.

**How can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, you generally need to select emails individually by tapping and holding one email, then tapping others to select multiple. There isn't a direct 'select all' button for an entire folder. However, you can use the search bar (e.g., `is:unread`) to find unread emails, then select and mark them as read.

**What's the difference between 'Inbox' and 'All Mail' when marking as read?**

Your 'Inbox' shows new incoming emails and those you haven't archived. 'All Mail' contains every email in your account, including those in your Inbox, Sent Mail, and archived messages. Marking all emails as read in 'All Mail' will affect every email, whereas doing so in 'Inbox' will only affect emails currently in your inbox view.

**Can I mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

Yes, you can. Use the Gmail search bar to filter emails from a specific sender (e.g., `from:example@domain.com`). Once the search results appear, you can then use the 'Select all' checkbox and the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link to mark those specific emails as read.
