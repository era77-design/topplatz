To mark all emails as read in Gmail, use the 'Select all' checkbox at the top left of your inbox, then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link, and finally click the 'Mark as read' icon. This process allows you to quickly clear notifications and manage your inbox efficiently.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Computer or mobile device with internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to Gmail. Log in to your account using your credentials if you haven't already. Ensuring you are logged in is the first step to accessing your emails and managing your inbox effectively. If you don't have a Gmail account yet, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

In your Gmail inbox, locate the small square checkbox positioned at the top left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox once. This action will select all emails currently displayed on the page, typically around 50 conversations, making them ready for a bulk action.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear at the top of your inbox, for example, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, look for a link within this message that says, 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection to *all* emails in your current view, not just those on the first page.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all desired emails are selected, a toolbar will appear above your email list. In this toolbar, locate the 'Mark as read' icon. This icon typically looks like an open envelope or a closed envelope with a checkmark. Click on it to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

For bulk actions involving many emails, Gmail will usually prompt you for confirmation. A small pop-up or notification might appear asking, 'Are you sure you want to take this action on all conversations in this search?' Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed and finalize the process. This ensures you intend to mark a large number of emails as read.

### Step 6: Verify Your Inbox

After confirming, all selected emails will be marked as read, and their bold formatting (which indicates unread status) will disappear. Your unread email count should significantly decrease, giving you a clearer and more organized overview of your inbox. This helps in achieving a sense of inbox clarity.


## Tips & Tricks

- This method works for specific labels or search results too. Apply a filter or search query first (e.g., 'is:unread'), then select all matching conversations.
- If you only want to mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions or Social) as read, navigate to that category tab before following the selection steps.
- Marking emails as read doesn't delete them; they remain in your inbox or archives. It simply removes the 'unread' notification.
- To undo marking emails as read, you can quickly click the 'Undo' button that appears briefly after the action, or manually select them and mark them as unread.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you maintain an organized inbox and reduce digital clutter, especially for non-critical notifications.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. Open the app, tap the menu icon (three lines), then tap 'All inboxes' or a specific label. Tap and hold one email to select it, then tap the 'Select all' checkbox at the top. Finally, tap the three-dot menu and choose 'Mark as read'.

**What if I accidentally mark emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification usually appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click this to reverse the action. If you miss it, you'll need to manually select the emails you wish to revert and mark them as unread.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. The emails remain in your inbox or whichever folder they were in. If you wish to remove them from your inbox view, you would need to archive or delete them separately.

**Can I mark only unread emails as read?**

Yes, you can easily filter your inbox to show only unread emails. In the Gmail search bar, type `is:unread` and press Enter. This will display only your unread messages. Then, follow the steps provided to select all conversations and mark them as read.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is useful for quickly clearing a backlog of notifications, especially in categories like 'Promotions' or 'Social' where you might not need to read every single email but want to acknowledge them. It helps achieve an 'inbox zero' state without deleting important messages, reducing visual clutter.

**Is there a way to automatically mark emails as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a direct feature to automatically mark *all* incoming emails as read by default. However, you can create filters to automatically mark certain types of emails (e.g., from specific senders or with certain keywords) as read upon arrival, helping to manage your inbox proactively.

**What if 'Select all conversations that match this search' doesn't appear?**

This option usually appears only after you've clicked the top-left checkbox and selected the current page of emails. If it's still not visible, ensure you're in a standard inbox view (like Primary, Social, Promotions) and not a single conversation view. Also, try refreshing your browser or app.
