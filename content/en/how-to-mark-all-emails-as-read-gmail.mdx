To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the 'Select all' checkbox and then the 'Mark as read' option found in the toolbar above your emails. This allows you to efficiently clear your inbox of unread messages, helping you achieve inbox zero or simply tidy up your email list.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- An active Gmail account
- Internet connection

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Enter your Gmail address and password to log in. If you are already logged in, you will be taken directly to your inbox.

### Step 2: Navigate to Your Inbox or Specific Label

Once logged in, you will typically land in your 'Primary' inbox. If you want to mark emails as read in a different category (like Social or Promotions) or a specific label, click on that category or label in the left-hand menu. This ensures you are targeting the correct set of emails.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Look for the small checkbox located at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the 'Compose' button and below the search bar. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on the first page of your inbox or selected label, usually around 50 conversations.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations (If Applicable)

After clicking the checkbox, a message will appear above your email list stating, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately following this, you'll see an option: 'Select all [number] conversations that match this search.' Click on this text to extend your selection beyond the current page and include all emails within your current view (e.g., all emails in your Primary inbox or all unread emails).

### Step 5: Mark Emails as Read

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your email list. Find the 'More' menu, often represented by three vertical dots (⋮) or an open book icon. Click on this icon, and from the dropdown menu, select 'Mark as read.' This action will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Gmail will process your request, and you might see a confirmation message at the bottom-left of your screen, such as 'Conversations marked as read.' All selected emails will now appear in a normal, non-bold font, indicating they have been read. You can repeat this process for other labels or categories if needed.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use the search operator `is:unread` in the Gmail search bar to quickly find all unread emails across your entire account. Then, you can apply the 'Select all conversations that match this search' and 'Mark as read' steps.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' button that briefly appears at the bottom-left of your screen immediately after the action. Click it to revert the change.
- On the Gmail mobile app, the process is similar: tap the checkbox next to one email, then tap 'Select all' (if available), and look for the three-dot menu to find 'Mark as read'.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, you can. On the Gmail mobile app, select at least one email by tapping its sender icon, then look for a 'Select all' option or manually select more. After selecting, tap the three-dot menu (⋮) and choose 'Mark as read'.

**What if I only want to mark certain types of emails as read?**

You can use Gmail's powerful search operators. For example, type `is:unread category:promotions` into the search bar to find all unread promotional emails, then select them all and mark them as read.

**Will marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them or move them to the trash. They will remain in your inbox or their respective labels.

**How do I undo marking emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom-left of your Gmail screen. Click this button quickly to revert the action and mark the emails as unread again.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users mark all emails as read to achieve 'inbox zero,' clear notification clutter, or focus only on new, incoming messages. It can help in decluttering your view and reducing mental load.

**Does this work for all labels or just the primary inbox?**

This method works for any label or category you navigate to in Gmail. Whether you're in 'Primary,' 'Social,' 'Promotions,' or a custom label, you can apply the same steps to select and mark all emails within that specific view as read.

**What if I don't have a Gmail account yet?**

If you don't have a Gmail account, you'll need to create one before you can manage your emails. You can follow our guide on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) to get started.
