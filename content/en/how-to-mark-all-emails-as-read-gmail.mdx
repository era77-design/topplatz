To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first select the checkbox at the top of your inbox to select all visible emails. Then, click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link if you have more than one page of emails. Finally, click the 'More' (three dots) icon and choose 'Mark as read' to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device with internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to Gmail. If you're not already logged in, enter your email address and password to access your inbox. This is the starting point for managing all your email conversations. If you don't have an account yet, you can learn how to [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

In your Gmail inbox, locate the small square checkbox at the top left, just above your email list. Clicking this box will instantly select all the emails currently visible on your page. This typically includes 50 or 100 conversations, depending on your display settings. You'll see a confirmation message indicating how many conversations are selected on the current page.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations (If Applicable)

If you have more than a single page of emails you wish to mark as read, a message will appear just above your emails after you select the checkbox. It will state something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all X conversations that match this search.' Click on the 'Select all X conversations that match this search' link to extend your selection beyond the current page and include all emails in your current view.

### Step 4: Locate the 'More' Options Menu

Once your desired emails are selected, look for the 'More' icon in the toolbar. This is usually represented by three vertical dots (⋮) and is typically found among other action icons like archive, delete, or snooze. The toolbar appears above your selected emails, offering various actions you can perform on them.

### Step 5: Choose 'Mark as Read'

Click on the 'More' icon to reveal a dropdown menu of available actions. From this menu, select 'Mark as read.' Gmail will then process your request, and all the selected emails will be marked as read, disappearing from your unread count. Depending on the number of emails, this may take a few moments.

### Step 6: Confirm and Refresh (Optional)

After clicking 'Mark as read,' you might see a confirmation message at the bottom of your screen. Your inbox should now reflect the change, with the selected emails no longer appearing bold or highlighted as unread. If you don't see the change immediately, a quick page refresh can often update the display, showing your newly organized inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use search filters (e.g., `is:unread` or `label:inbox is:unread`) to target specific unread emails before marking them as read, for more precise control.
- Regularly archiving or deleting old emails can help keep your inbox manageable and reduce the need for mass 'mark as read' actions.
- Consider using Gmail's 'Categories' (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) to automatically sort incoming emails, making it easier to manage unread messages in specific sections.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, you can quickly undo the action using the 'Undo' notification that appears at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action.
- On the Gmail mobile app, the process is similar: long-press an email to select it, then tap the three dots or envelope icon to find the 'Mark as read' option.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when using the 'Select all conversations that match this search' option, especially if you haven't filtered your search. This can affect thousands of emails across your entire Gmail account, including those in other labels or archives, potentially marking important emails as read unintentionally.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails in a specific label as read?**

Yes, first navigate to the desired label (e.g., 'Promotions' or a custom label) from the left sidebar. Then, follow the same steps to select all emails within that label and choose 'Mark as read' from the 'More' menu.

**Will marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them or move them to the trash. They will remain in your inbox or their respective labels, just without the unread indicator.

**Is there a keyboard shortcut for marking emails as read?**

Yes, if you have keyboard shortcuts enabled in Gmail settings, you can select emails and then press 'Shift + I' to mark them as read. This can speed up your workflow significantly, especially if you manage a large volume of emails.

**How do I mark emails as unread again if I made a mistake?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification appears at the bottom left of your screen; click it to revert the action. Alternatively, you can select the emails again and choose 'Mark as unread' from the 'More' (three dots) menu.

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can long-press an email to select it, then tap additional emails to select more. Once selected, tap the envelope icon (sometimes with a checkmark) or the three-dot menu to find the 'Mark as read' option.

**Why aren't all my emails being selected when I click the top checkbox?**

The top checkbox only selects emails visible on the current page (typically 50 or 100). If you have more emails, you'll see a prompt like 'Select all X conversations that match this search' to extend the selection to your entire inbox or current view.

**Does this method work for emails in the Spam or Trash folders?**

Generally, emails in Spam are automatically marked as read or filtered out, and emails in Trash are already considered discarded. This method is primarily for managing active inbox emails or those in other labels, not for Spam or Trash.
