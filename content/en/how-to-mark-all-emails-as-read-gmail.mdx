To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically select all visible messages in your inbox or a specific folder, then click the 'Mark as read' button. This process can be done quickly on both desktop and mobile devices to help you manage your inbox more efficiently.

## What You Need

- A Gmail account
- Internet connection
- Computer or mobile device

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail on Your Desktop Browser

Access your Gmail account by navigating to mail.google.com in your web browser. If you don't already have one, you'll need to [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) first. Ensure you are logged into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails on the Page

In your inbox, locate the checkbox at the top-left of your email list, just above the first message. Click this checkbox to select all emails currently displayed on the page. By default, Gmail shows 50 conversations per page.

### Step 3: Select All Emails in Your Inbox (Across Pages)

After checking the box in the previous step, a message will appear above your emails stating, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately following this, you'll see a link that says, 'Select all [number] conversations in Primary' (or whatever tab you're in). Click this link to select every email in that specific inbox tab, regardless of how many pages they span.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all desired emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope or an eye. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up asking if you want to apply this action to all selected conversations. Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. This action will mark all the selected emails as read, clearing them from your unread count.

### Step 6: Mark Emails as Read in the Gmail Mobile App

Open the Gmail app on your smartphone or tablet. Tap and hold on one unread email to select it, then tap on any other emails you wish to mark as read. Once selected, tap the three-dot menu icon (or the envelope/eye icon directly) usually found at the top of the screen, and choose 'Mark as read' from the options.

### Step 7: Mark Emails as Read in Specific Folders or Labels

If you want to mark emails as read within a particular folder or label (e.g., 'Promotions,' 'Social,' or a custom label), first navigate to that specific folder or label from the left-hand menu. Then, follow steps 2-5 to select all emails within that view and mark them as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use search filters (e.g., `is:unread from:sender@example.com`) to narrow down emails before marking them as read.
- You can undo marking emails as read immediately after the action using the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom left of your screen.
- Consider creating custom filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read or move them to specific labels.
- Regularly clearing your unread count can help you focus on new, important messages.
- If you only want to mark emails in a specific category (like 'Promotions' or 'Social') as read, navigate to that category tab first before selecting all emails.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark only some unread emails as read?**

Yes, on desktop, you can manually check individual boxes next to the emails you want to mark. On mobile, tap and hold to select the first email, then tap others to select them before choosing 'Mark as read'.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it to revert the action. If you miss it, you'll need to manually search for the email and mark it as unread again.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking an email as read only changes its status from unread to read. The email remains in your inbox or its respective folder/label until you choose to archive or delete it.

**How can I mark emails as unread again?**

Select the read email(s) you wish to change, then click the 'Mark as unread' icon in the toolbar (it often looks like a closed envelope) or choose the option from the three-dot menu on mobile.

**Why do I have so many unread emails in Gmail?**

A high unread count often results from being subscribed to many newsletters, receiving numerous promotional emails, or not checking your inbox regularly. Using Gmail's categories (Primary, Social, Promotions) and filters can help manage this.

**Can I mark emails as read for a specific date range?**

Yes, you can use Gmail's search operators to filter emails by date (e.g., `before:YYYY/MM/DD` or `after:YYYY/MM/DD`) combined with `is:unread`. Once filtered, you can select all results and mark them as read.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Yes, you can create a filter in Gmail. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses. Create a new filter based on sender, subject, or keywords, and then choose the action 'Mark as read'.
