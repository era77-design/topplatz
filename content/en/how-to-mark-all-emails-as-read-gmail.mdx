To mark all emails as read in Gmail, simply navigate to your inbox, click the 'Select all' checkbox, and then choose the 'Mark as read' option from the action bar. This process can be applied to emails on a single page or across your entire inbox using a specific selection prompt.

## What You Need

- Gmail account
- Computer or mobile device with internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser and go to mail.google.com. If you're not already logged in, enter your Gmail username and password to access your inbox. If you don't have an account yet, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure you are in the correct inbox or tab (e.g., Primary, Social, Promotions) where you wish to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

In your Gmail inbox, locate the small checkbox at the top of the email list, just below the search bar and above the first email. Clicking this checkbox will select all emails currently visible on the page (typically 50 conversations).

### Step 3: Select ALL Unread Emails (Across Pages)

After selecting the emails on the current page, a message will appear above your email list. It will typically say something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all X conversations in Primary.' Click on the blue text link within this message to extend your selection to ALL unread emails in that specific category or tab, regardless of how many pages they span.

### Step 4: Mark as Read

With all your desired emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appears just above your email list. This icon usually looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Gmail will process your request. A small notification will typically appear at the bottom left of your screen, confirming that 'X conversations have been marked as read.' All the selected emails will now display as read, and their bold formatting (if unread) will disappear.

### Step 6: Alternative: Filter by Unread First

If you only want to deal with unread emails, type `is:unread` into the Gmail search bar at the top of your screen and press Enter. This will display only your unread messages. You can then proceed with steps 2-4 to select and mark all these filtered unread emails as read.

### Step 7: Mark an Entire Category/Tab as Read

If you use Gmail's category tabs (e.g., Primary, Social, Promotions), you can navigate directly into a specific tab. Once inside, follow steps 2-4 to select all emails within that particular category and mark them as read. This is useful for quickly clearing out a specific type of message, like promotional emails.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail search operators like `is:unread in:inbox` to refine your selection even further before marking emails as read.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, a small 'Undo' option will appear at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action. Click it quickly to revert the change.
- Consider using keyboard shortcuts for faster navigation and actions. For example, press `Shift + I` to mark selected emails as read (ensure keyboard shortcuts are enabled in Gmail settings).
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you maintain a cleaner inbox and easily identify truly new messages.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps declutter your inbox, making it easier to identify new, incoming messages that require your attention. It can also reduce visual noise for better organization.

**Can I undo marking emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification appears at the bottom-left of your Gmail screen. You must click this 'Undo' option quickly before it disappears to revert the action.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, archive them, or move them from their current folder or category.

**How do I mark only *some* unread emails as read?**

To mark specific emails as read, simply click the individual checkbox next to each message you want to select. Once you've selected all desired emails, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar.

**What if I have thousands of unread emails across many pages?**

The key is to use the 'Select all X conversations in [Category]' link that appears after you select all emails on the current page. This feature is designed to select all matching emails, regardless of how many pages they span, allowing for a single action.

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. You can long-press on an email to select it, then tap the top-left checkbox to select all visible emails. Look for the 'Mark as read' icon (often an open envelope or found within a three-dots menu) to apply the action.

**Will marking emails as read affect my starred or important emails?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their read status. It will not remove stars, important markers, or labels you've applied to those emails. They will retain their other classifications.
