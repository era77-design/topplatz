To mark all emails as read in Gmail, use the search bar to find unread messages, select all visible results, and then click the 'Mark as read' button. For larger quantities, ensure you click the prompt to 'Select all conversations that match this search' to apply the action across multiple pages.

## What You Need

- Active Gmail account
- Computer or mobile device with internet access

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in with your credentials if you're not already signed in. Ensure you're viewing the correct inbox where you want to mark emails as read.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you're new to Gmail or need to set up another account, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface, type `is:unread` and press Enter. This command will filter your inbox to display only messages that are currently marked as unread. This helps you target all unread messages regardless of their location within your various labels or categories.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Unread Emails

Once the search results appear, locate the small checkbox at the top-left of your email list, just above the first message. Click this checkbox to select all the unread emails currently displayed on that page (typically 50 emails). A notification will confirm how many conversations are selected.

### Step 4: Select All Matching Conversations (if necessary)

If you have more unread emails than can fit on a single page, a yellow banner will appear just above your email list after you've selected the visible emails. This banner will say, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all conversations that match this search.' Click on the second part of this statement to select *all* unread emails in your entire inbox, across all pages.

### Step 5: Mark Emails as Read

With all your desired unread emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click it to change the status of all selected messages from unread to read. This action will process all the emails you've chosen.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

A small notification will usually appear at the bottom-left of your screen, confirming that the emails have been marked as read. For a large number of emails, this action might take a few moments to complete. Be patient as Gmail updates your inbox.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox

After the process, refresh your Gmail page or navigate back to your primary inbox. You should now see significantly fewer, or no, unread emails. If some remain, they might be in different categories or labels, and you may need to repeat the process for those specific sections.


## Tips & Tricks

- **Use Specific Search Operators**: For more granular control, combine `is:unread` with other operators like `label:promotions is:unread` to target unread emails specifically in your promotions category.
- **Check All Inbox Categories**: Gmail organizes emails into Primary, Social, Promotions, and Updates. Ensure you check each category for unread messages if you want to clear them all.
- **Undo an Accidental Action**: If you accidentally mark emails as read, a small 'Undo' button appears briefly after the action. Click it immediately to revert the change.
- **Mark Individual Emails**: To mark just a few specific emails as read, simply check their individual boxes and then click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar.
- **Periodically Clear Unread Emails**: Make it a habit to use this method periodically to keep your inbox organized and reduce clutter, improving your overall email management and focus.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be absolutely sure you want to mark all selected messages as read before confirming. While an 'Undo' option appears briefly, it's not a long-term solution, and finding specific emails again if they were important might be harder once they are no longer highlighted as unread.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark only some unread emails as read?**

Yes, you can. Instead of using the 'select all' checkbox, manually check the boxes next to the specific unread emails you wish to mark as read, then click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button appears at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it quickly to revert the action. If you miss it, you'll need to manually search for those emails using keywords or sender information.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not move them to the Trash or Archive. Your emails remain in your inbox or their respective labels, just without the 'unread' highlight.

**Can I do this on my phone using the Gmail app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. You can search for `is:unread`, then long-press one email to select it, tap the 'select all' checkbox at the top, and finally tap the 'Mark as read' icon (often three dots or an envelope icon).

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users do this to achieve 'inbox zero,' clear old notifications, or simply declutter their inbox visually. It helps focus on new, incoming messages without being distracted by a high unread count from older, less critical emails.

**How do I find specific emails after marking them all as read?**

You can use Gmail's powerful search features. Search by sender, subject, keywords, or date. For example, `from:sender@example.com` or `subject:meeting` will help you locate specific messages quickly.

**What if the 'Select all conversations that match this search' option doesn't appear?**

This typically means that all your unread emails are already displayed on the current page (usually 50 or fewer). Simply clicking the main 'select all' checkbox will select every unread email in your inbox, making the yellow banner unnecessary.
