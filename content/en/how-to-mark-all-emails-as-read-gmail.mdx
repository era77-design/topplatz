To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can utilize a specific search query combined with the 'Select all' and 'Mark as read' functions. This efficient process allows you to quickly clear your unread count across your entire inbox or specific categories, helping you achieve inbox zero with ease.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device
- Internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Begin by opening your preferred web browser and navigating to mail.google.com. If you aren't already logged in, enter your Gmail username and password to access your inbox. Ensure you are on the correct account if you manage multiple Gmail profiles.

### Step 2: Search for All Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface, type 'is:unread' and press Enter or click the search icon. This query will filter your entire inbox, displaying only the emails that Gmail currently considers unread. This is the crucial first step to gather all relevant messages.

### Step 3: Select All Matching Conversations

After your search results load, locate the 'Select' checkbox at the top left of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all emails currently visible on the page. A banner will appear at the top, often stating 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.'

### Step 4: Extend Selection to All Unread Emails

If you have more than 50 unread emails, the banner mentioned in the previous step will also include a link that says 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click on this link to ensure that every single unread email in your entire Gmail account is selected, not just those visible on the current page. This step is critical for marking *all* emails as read.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all unread emails are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click on it. Gmail will then process the request and mark all selected emails as read, removing them from your unread count.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action and Check Your Inbox

After clicking 'Mark as read,' you might see a confirmation message at the bottom left of your screen, like 'Conversations marked as read.' Your unread count should now reflect zero, or significantly fewer unread messages if you only targeted a specific subset. Navigate back to your primary inbox to confirm the change.


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark emails as read only within a specific tab (e.g., Promotions or Social), first click on that tab, then type 'is:unread' into the search bar.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you achieve 'inbox zero' and reduce digital clutter, making it easier to focus on important messages.
- You can undo this action within a few seconds after performing it by clicking the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom left of your screen.
- Consider using Gmail's filtering and labeling features to automatically sort incoming emails, preventing future build-ups of unread messages.
- If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, simply check their individual boxes and then click the 'Mark as read' icon.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be certain you want to mark *all* unread emails as read. This action can make it harder to identify truly new or urgent messages that you haven't yet reviewed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' button will appear at the bottom left of your Gmail screen. Click it quickly to reverse the action. However, this option is only available for a short period.

**How do I mark only unread emails in a specific tab (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

First, click on the specific tab you want to manage (e.g., 'Promotions'). Then, in the search bar, type 'is:unread' and press Enter. Follow the same steps to select all and mark as read, but the action will only apply to emails within that tab.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read.' It does not delete them from your inbox or archive them. They will remain in your Gmail account unless you explicitly choose to delete or archive them.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Users often do this to clear a large backlog of unread messages, especially if they've been away or if their unread count has become overwhelming. It helps achieve a fresh start and makes it easier to spot new, incoming emails.

**Can I mark all emails as read on my mobile device?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. Use the search bar for 'is:unread', then long-press one email to select it, tap the 'select all' checkbox (often at the top), and then tap the three-dot menu icon to find the 'Mark as read' option.

**What if I don't have a Gmail account yet?**

If you don't have a Gmail account, you'll need to create one before you can manage emails within Gmail. You can easily set up a new account by following a guide like [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

**Is there a way to prevent so many unread emails from accumulating?**

Yes, you can use Gmail's powerful filtering rules to automatically label, archive, or even delete certain types of incoming mail. Regularly unsubscribing from unwanted newsletters also significantly reduces clutter.
