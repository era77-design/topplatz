To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first navigate to your inbox in a web browser. Select the checkbox at the top of your email list, then click the option to 'Select all conversations that match this search' from the banner that appears. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- Computer or laptop with internet access
- Gmail account
- Web browser (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Edge)

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Start by opening your preferred web browser on your computer or laptop. Go to mail.google.com and log in to your Gmail account. This method is most effective on the desktop version of Gmail, as mobile apps may have different interfaces or limitations for selecting all emails beyond the current view. If you're new to Gmail, you might find it helpful to review [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account) before managing your inbox.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox View

Once logged in, ensure you are in the inbox view where you want to mark emails as read. This could be your 'Primary' tab, 'Social' tab, 'Promotions' tab, or any other category. You can also navigate to a specific label or search result if you only want to mark emails within that selection as read. For example, if you want to clear your Promotions tab, click on it first.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

At the top of your email list, just above the first email, you'll see a small square checkbox. Click this checkbox. This action will select all the emails currently visible on the first page of your inbox or the current view. You'll notice that all the visible emails become highlighted.

### Step 4: Extend Selection to All Conversations

After clicking the initial checkbox, a banner will appear above your emails. This banner will typically say something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also offer an option: 'Select all [number] conversations in Primary' (or 'in Promotions,' etc.). Click this link to extend your selection beyond just the visible page to include every email in that specific inbox category or label.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all your desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar just above the emails. You will see several icons. Find the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon. Gmail will then process your request, marking all the selected emails as read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

A small notification will usually appear at the bottom-left of your screen, confirming 'Conversations marked as read.' Your unread count for that category or your entire inbox should now be zero or significantly reduced. If you made a mistake, this notification often includes an 'Undo' option that you can click immediately to revert the action.


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions) as read, navigate to that category first, then follow the steps.
- You can use the search bar to filter emails (e.g., `is:unread from:example.com`) then select all and mark as read for specific senders.
- If you accidentally mark important emails as read, use the 'Undo' option that appears briefly after the action, or manually mark them as unread again.
- Regularly marking emails as read helps maintain an organized inbox and can reduce stress from a high unread count.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking all emails as read will remove the 'unread' indicator from all selected messages, so ensure you have reviewed any critical emails first or are comfortable with them being marked as read without individual attention.
- ⚠️ This action cannot be undone if you close your browser or navigate away from the page after the 'Undo' option disappears.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

While the Gmail mobile app allows you to select multiple emails and mark them as read, it typically does not offer a direct 'select all conversations' option for your entire inbox beyond what's currently loaded. You would need to scroll to load more emails and then select them in batches, or use the desktop version for a complete 'mark all as read' action.

**What happens if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' option appears at the bottom-left of your screen. Click this option to revert the action. If the 'Undo' option is gone, you will need to manually locate the important emails and mark them as 'unread' again by selecting them and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read.' It does not delete them or move them to the trash. The emails will remain in your inbox or whichever folder they were in.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

People often mark all emails as read to achieve 'inbox zero,' clear a backlog of notifications, or reduce the visual clutter of a high unread count. It can help in resetting your focus, especially after a vacation or a busy period, allowing you to start fresh with new incoming messages.

**How can I mark only specific types of emails as read?**

You can use Gmail's search bar to filter emails by sender, subject, date, or other criteria (e.g., `label:promotions is:unread`). After applying your filter, use the 'Select all' checkbox and the 'Select all conversations that match this search' option, then click 'Mark as read' to target only those specific emails.

**Is there a way to mark only unread emails as read without affecting read ones?**

Yes, when you use the 'Select all conversations' feature and then 'Mark as read,' Gmail intelligently only changes the status of those emails that are currently unread. Emails that are already read will remain in their read status, so you don't need to worry about inadvertently changing their status.
