To mark all emails as read in Gmail, simply navigate to your desired inbox or label, select all visible emails using the checkbox, and then click the option to select all conversations. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon to instantly clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- A computer, smartphone, or tablet with internet access
- An active Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Browser

Start by opening your preferred web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) and navigating to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account if you aren't already. If you need help setting up an account, you can learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox or Label

Decide which set of emails you want to mark as read. You can choose your primary 'Inbox', or select a specific 'Label' (folder) from the left sidebar, or even use the search bar to filter emails by sender, subject, or date. This allows you to target only the emails you wish to mark.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small square checkbox at the top of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox once. This will select all emails currently visible on the page (usually 50 emails).

### Step 4: Select All Conversations in the View

After clicking the initial checkbox, a message will appear just above your emails, typically saying, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Next to this, you'll see an option: 'Select all [number] conversations in [Inbox/Label Name]'. Click on this link to select every email in your current view, not just those on the first page.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click it to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action (if prompted)

For very large selections, Gmail might briefly display a confirmation message or a progress bar. Allow a few moments for the action to complete. Your unread email count for that inbox or label should now be significantly reduced, or even zero.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail's powerful search filters (e.g., 'is:unread from:sender@example.com') to narrow down your selection before marking emails as read, ensuring you only affect specific messages.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom left of your screen after the action. Clicking it will revert the change.
- Marking emails as read does not delete them. They remain in your inbox or label. If you wish to remove them from your primary view, consider archiving or deleting them instead.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you maintain a cleaner inbox and focus on truly new or important communications.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is useful for decluttering your inbox, reducing a high unread count, or when you've returned from a long break and want to clear out old notifications quickly to focus on new messages.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom left of your screen. Click it within a few seconds to revert the action.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from 'unread' to 'read'. The emails remain in your inbox or the label they were in; they are not deleted or archived.

**Can I mark emails as unread after I've marked them as read?**

Yes, you can. Simply select the emails you want to mark as unread and then click the 'Mark as unread' icon (which usually looks like a closed envelope) in the toolbar above your emails.

**What if I have thousands of emails? Will this still work?**

Yes, this method works for thousands of emails. After selecting the visible emails on the page, remember to click the 'Select all [number] conversations in [Inbox/Label Name]' link to ensure all emails in that view are selected, not just the first 50.

**Does this method work on the Gmail mobile app?**

The process is slightly different on the Gmail mobile app. You typically cannot select all emails in a label or inbox at once. You have to long-press to select individual emails, then tap others, and then choose 'Mark as read' from the menu.

**Can I mark only emails from a specific sender as read?**

Absolutely! Use Gmail's search bar to filter emails (e.g., 'from:sender@example.com'). Once the search results appear, follow the steps to select all conversations in that view and mark them as read.
