To mark all emails as read in Gmail, navigate to your desired inbox category or search results, select all emails, and then click the 'Mark as read' icon. This action helps declutter your inbox and ensures a fresh start, especially after a long period away or when dealing with a large volume of non-essential messages.

## What You Need

- An active Gmail account
- Internet access
- A computer or mobile device with a web browser

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) and go to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account using your email address and password if you aren't already signed in. Ensure you are on the main inbox view or the specific category you wish to manage.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Email View

Decide which emails you want to mark as read. You can choose to clear your primary inbox, a specific tab like 'Promotions' or 'Social,' or even the results of a search query. For example, click on the 'Promotions' tab to view only those emails, or use the search bar to find emails from a particular sender or with specific keywords.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small square checkbox at the top-left of your email list, just above the email headers. Click this checkbox. This action will select all the emails currently displayed on your screen, typically around 50 conversations per page.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations in the Category/Search

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear at the top of your inbox, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Immediately following this, you'll see a crucial link: 'Select all conversations that match this search' or 'Select all [Category Name] conversations.' Click this link to extend your selection to *all* emails within that category or search result, not just those on the current page.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all your desired emails are selected (you'll see a confirmation message indicating the total number), look for the toolbar located just above your emails. Click the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. This icon indicates that all selected messages will transition from an unread to a read state.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action (If Prompted)

For very large batches of emails, Gmail might display a pop-up window asking you to confirm your action, especially when marking thousands of emails as read. Read the confirmation message carefully and click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. The process may take a moment to complete depending on the number of emails.

### Step 7: Verify Your Inbox Status

After the action is complete, your selected emails will no longer appear bold (if they were unread) and the unread count for that category or your overall inbox should significantly decrease or disappear. You can now enjoy a cleaner, more organized inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail's powerful search filters to mark specific groups of emails as read. For instance, search for 'label:unread older_than:6m' to find all unread emails older than six months.
- If you accidentally mark an important email as read, you can quickly undo the action. Immediately after marking emails as read, look for the 'Undo' notification that appears briefly at the bottom-left of your screen.
- If you're new to the platform and need to get started, you can easily [create a Gmail account](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) to manage your communications efficiently.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Always double-check your selection before marking emails as read, especially when selecting 'all conversations that match this search.' You might inadvertently mark important unread emails as read, making them harder to spot later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps achieve 'inbox zero,' reducing visual clutter and the mental burden of a large unread count. It's useful after vacations, when returning to a long-neglected account, or simply to clear promotional emails you don't intend to read.

**Can I mark emails as unread after doing this?**

Yes, you can. If you realize you marked an important email as read by mistake, you can either click the 'Undo' button that appears briefly after the action or manually select the email(s) and click the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) in the toolbar.

**How do I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, you can select multiple emails by tapping and holding one, then tapping others. To select all, you'd typically need to scroll down and keep selecting. There isn't a direct 'select all conversations' option like on the desktop for an entire category. Once selected, tap the three-dot menu and choose 'Mark as read'.

**Will marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read does not delete them. It simply changes their status from unread to read. The emails will remain in your inbox, archives, or labels until you manually delete or move them.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

To mark emails from a specific sender as read, use the Gmail search bar. Type 'from:sender@example.com' (replacing with the actual email address) and then follow steps 3-5 to select all matching conversations and mark them as read.

**I only want to mark emails in my 'Promotions' tab as read. How?**

Navigate directly to your 'Promotions' tab in Gmail. Then, follow steps 3-5: click the top-left checkbox to select visible emails, click the 'Select all conversations in Promotions' link, and finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a direct feature to automatically mark *incoming* emails as read. However, you can create filters that archive or skip the inbox for certain emails, effectively removing them from your unread count without deleting them. This achieves a similar goal for managing less important messages.
