To mark all emails as read in Gmail, first navigate to your inbox. Use the 'Select all' checkbox at the top left, then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link if you want to include emails beyond the current page. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device with internet access
- An active Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and go to mail.google.com. Log in with your Google account credentials if you aren't already signed in. Ensure you are in the inbox view where you want to perform this action, such as your Primary, Social, or Promotions tab.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you're new to Gmail and need to set up an account, you can find guides like [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account) to help you get started.

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

At the top of your inbox, look for a small checkbox. Click this checkbox to select all the emails currently visible on the page. This typically selects up to 50 conversations at once. A message will appear above your emails indicating how many conversations are selected.

### Step 3: Select All Matching Conversations (Optional, but Recommended)

After selecting the emails on the current page, a banner will appear at the top, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, if you want to mark *all* emails in your entire inbox (or current view) as read, click the link within this banner that says 'Select all conversations that match this search.' This expands your selection beyond just the current page.

> 💡 **Tip:** This step is vital for marking a large number of emails as read across multiple pages, not just the ones immediately visible.

### Step 4: Locate the 'Mark as Read' Button

Once your desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar just above your emails. Among the various icons, find the 'Mark as read' icon. It typically looks like an open envelope. Hovering over the icon will usually display its function as 'Mark as read'.

### Step 5: Click 'Mark as Read' and Confirm

Click the 'Mark as read' icon. Gmail will then process your request. For large numbers of emails, you might see a confirmation pop-up asking 'Are you sure you want to mark all X conversations as read?' Confirm your action. Gmail will then update your inbox, removing the 'unread' bolding and indicators from the selected emails.

### Step 6: Mark Emails as Read in Specific Categories or Labels

If you only want to mark emails as read within a specific category (e.g., Promotions) or a custom label, first navigate to that category tab or label from the left sidebar. Then, follow steps 2-5 to select and mark all emails within that specific view as read. This allows for targeted inbox management.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can also use the search bar to filter emails (e.g., 'is:unread in:promotions') before selecting all, to be very precise about which emails get marked as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark individual emails as read without opening them, hover over the email in your inbox and click the 'Mark as read' icon that appears on the right side.
- If you accidentally mark emails as read, look for the 'Undo' notification that temporarily appears at the bottom left of your screen immediately after the action. Click it to revert.
- Use Gmail's search operators (e.g., 'is:unread', 'label:important is:unread') to filter emails precisely before performing a 'mark all as read' action for more control.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking a large number of emails as read cannot be easily undone if you miss the brief 'Undo' notification. Be certain before confirming the action, especially if you rely on the unread status to track important communications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps achieve 'inbox zero,' reducing visual clutter and making it easier to focus on new incoming messages. It can also be useful for clearing out old, less important emails that you've already seen but haven't formally marked as read.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom left of your Gmail screen. You have a few seconds to click this to revert the action. If you miss this window, there is no direct 'undo all' feature.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete the emails, archive them, or move them to any other folder. They will remain in your inbox or their current label/category.

**How do I mark specific emails as read, not all of them?**

To mark specific emails as read, simply click the checkbox next to each individual email you wish to select. Once you've selected all desired emails, then click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your inbox.

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, on the Gmail mobile app, you can long-press an email to select it, then tap additional emails to select more. Once selected, tap the three-dot menu icon (⋮) or a similar options menu, and choose 'Mark as read' from the options.

**What if I only want to mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions) as read?**

Navigate to the specific category tab (like 'Promotions' or 'Social') in your Gmail inbox. Once in that tab, follow the steps outlined for selecting all emails on the page and then selecting all conversations that match the search (within that category) before clicking 'Mark as read'.

**How can I quickly find unread emails after doing this?**

You can always find unread emails by typing 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar. This will display all emails that are still marked as unread, regardless of their location or when they were received.
