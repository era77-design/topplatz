To mark all emails as read in Gmail, simply navigate to your inbox, use the top-most checkbox to select all visible emails, and then click the 'Mark as read' icon. This action helps declutter your inbox and gives you a clear view of new, important messages.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

First, open your web browser and go to gmail.com. Log in with your Google account credentials if you are not already signed in. Ensure you are in the inbox view where you can see your list of emails.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you're having trouble logging in, ensure your internet connection is stable. If you've recently gone through the process to [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) and are now facing an inbox full of initial notifications, marking them all as read can give you a fresh start.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner of your inbox, just above your emails. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently visible on the page, typically up to 50 conversations.

### Step 3: Select All Emails in Your Inbox (If Applicable)

If you have more than 50 emails in your inbox, after clicking the initial checkbox, a message will appear just above your emails. It will say something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all X conversations in Primary.' Click on the 'Select all X conversations in Primary' link to select every single email in that category, not just those on the current page.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope or a letter with a checkmark. Click on this icon to execute the action.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

After clicking 'Mark as read,' Gmail will process your request. A notification will briefly appear at the bottom-left of your screen confirming that 'Conversations marked as read.' All the selected emails will now appear as read, meaning their bold formatting will be removed.

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Categories (Optional)

If you use Gmail's category tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums), you might need to repeat these steps for each tab. Switch to the Social, Promotions, or other tabs and follow steps 2-4 to clear unread counts in those sections as well, ensuring your entire inbox is organized.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly find unread emails only, type 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar and press Enter. Then you can select and mark them as read.
- If you only want to mark specific emails as read, individually check the boxes next to those emails before clicking the 'Mark as read' icon.
- You can also right-click on an individual email to see a context menu with the 'Mark as read' option.
- Consider using Gmail's archiving feature (the 'Archive' icon) to remove emails from your inbox without deleting them, keeping your inbox cleaner.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you maintain inbox zero and focus on new communications.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking all emails as read cannot be directly undone with a single 'undo' button for the entire batch. If you accidentally mark important unread emails as read, you'll need to manually search for them and mark them as unread again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps declutter your inbox, remove the distracting unread count, and allows you to focus on genuinely new or important messages. It's a common practice for achieving 'inbox zero'.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Gmail offers a brief 'Undo' option immediately after the action for a few seconds. If you miss that, there isn't a single button to reverse marking all emails as read. You would need to manually search for specific emails and mark them as unread again if needed.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read does not delete them. It simply changes their status from unread to read. The emails remain in your inbox or whichever folder they were originally in.

**How can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, you cannot mark all emails as read with one tap. You need to long-press an email to select it, then tap additional emails, and finally tap the three-dot menu icon (or envelope icon) to find the 'Mark as read' option. For bulk actions, the desktop interface is more efficient.

**What if I only want to mark emails in a specific folder or label as read?**

Navigate to that specific folder or label (e.g., 'Promotions' tab, or a custom label). Once you are viewing only those emails, you can then use the 'Select all' checkbox and 'Mark as read' icon as described for the main inbox.

**What's the difference between 'Mark as read' and 'Archive'?**

'Mark as read' changes the status of an email but keeps it in your inbox. 'Archive' removes the email from your inbox view and moves it to the 'All Mail' folder, effectively hiding it from your primary view without deleting it. Both help declutter, but 'Archive' provides a cleaner inbox.

**Will marking emails as read affect my storage space?**

No, changing an email's read status has no impact on your Gmail storage space. Emails consume space whether they are marked as read or unread. To free up space, you would need to delete emails, especially those with large attachments.
