To quickly mark all emails as read in Gmail, navigate to your desired inbox or category, click the 'Select all' checkbox at the top of your email list, and then choose 'Select all conversations that match this search' if it appears. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) from the toolbar above your emails to clear your unread count.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your web browser and go to mail.google.com. Enter your Gmail email address and password to sign in. Ensure you are logged into the correct account where you wish to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox or Category

Once logged in, you'll see your inbox. You can choose to mark emails as read in your main 'Primary' tab, or switch to other categories like 'Social' or 'Promotions'. You can also select a specific label or folder from the left-hand menu.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Look for the small square checkbox located at the top-left of your email list, just above the first message. Click this checkbox. This action will instantly select all the emails currently visible on that page (typically 50 conversations).

### Step 4: Select All Matching Conversations (Important for Bulk Action)

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear just above your email list, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will be followed by a link: 'Select all X conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection to every single email in that category or search result, not just those on the current page.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all desired emails are selected, locate the toolbar that appears above your email list. Find the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

After clicking 'Mark as read,' a small black notification box will briefly appear at the bottom-left of your screen, confirming that the action has been completed. For example, it might say 'X conversations marked as read.' This indicates your unread count for those emails has been cleared.

### Step 7: Optional: Use Search for Targeted Marking

If you only want to mark specific types of emails as read, use the search bar at the top of Gmail. For instance, type `is:unread` to see only unread emails, or `from:sender@example.com` to find emails from a particular sender. After performing your search, follow steps 3-5 to select and mark those filtered results as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly undo marking emails as read, click the 'Undo' button that appears in the black notification bar at the bottom-left of your screen immediately after the action.
- Regularly checking and clearing your 'Promotions' and 'Social' tabs can help keep your main inbox clutter-free.
- If you've just started with Gmail, marking initial system emails as read can help you achieve 'inbox zero' from day one. Find out more about getting started here: [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).
- To mark individual emails as read without opening them, simply hover over the email in your list and click the 'Mark as read' icon (the open envelope) that appears on the right side.
- Consider setting up filters to automatically mark certain types of emails as read or archive them upon arrival, reducing manual effort.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I undo marking emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, a black notification bar will appear at the bottom-left of your screen with an 'Undo' option. Clicking this will revert the action. If you miss this window, you will need to manually mark them as unread again.

**Does marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, archive them, or move them to any other folder. Your emails will remain in their original location.

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

The Gmail mobile app does not offer a direct 'Select all conversations that match this search' feature for an entire inbox like the desktop version. You can long-press an email to select it, then manually tap other emails, or use the three-dot menu within certain labels to 'Mark all as read' for that specific label's visible emails.

**How do I mark only unread emails as read?**

To specifically target only unread emails, type `is:unread` into the Gmail search bar. This will filter your inbox to show only unread messages. Then, follow steps 3-5 (select all visible, then select all matching, then mark as read) to clear just your unread count.

**How can I mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

Use the search bar at the top of Gmail and type `from:sender@example.com` (replacing with the actual sender's email address). Once the search results are displayed, follow steps 3-5 to select all matching emails and mark them as read.

**What's the fastest way to mark everything in my Promotions tab as read?**

Go to your 'Promotions' tab. Click the 'Select all' checkbox at the top of the email list. A link will appear saying 'Select all conversations in Promotions' (or similar); click it. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon to clear that tab.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Users often mark all emails as read to achieve 'inbox zero,' clear a large backlog of unread messages, or reset their inbox after a busy period or vacation. It helps reduce visual clutter and allows you to focus on truly new, incoming emails.
