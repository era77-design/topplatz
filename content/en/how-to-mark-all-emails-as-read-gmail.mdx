To mark all emails as read in Gmail, navigate to your inbox, select all visible messages using the top checkbox, and then click the 'Select all conversations that match this search' link if you have more than one page. Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) to clear your unread count. This process helps you quickly manage an overflowing inbox.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail and Log In

Start by opening your web browser and going to mail.google.com. If you're not already signed in, enter your Gmail address and password to access your inbox. This is the starting point for managing all your email activities, including marking messages as read. If you're new to Gmail, you might find our guide on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) helpful for setting up your account initially.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails

Once in your inbox, locate the small checkbox at the very top of your email list, usually above the first message. Click this checkbox. This action will instantly select all the emails currently visible on that page of your inbox. You'll see the selected emails highlighted, and a new toolbar will appear above them.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations (If Applicable)

If you have more than 50 emails in your inbox that are unread, selecting the top checkbox will only select the emails on the current page. To select ALL emails in your inbox, look for a message that appears above the email list saying, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all [Number] conversations that match this search.' Click the 'Select all [Number] conversations that match this search' link to extend your selection to your entire inbox.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

After selecting all desired emails, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appeared above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Clicking this icon will change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action moves them out of your unread count, helping to tidy up your inbox view.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails, Gmail might ask you to confirm this bulk action. A small pop-up or notification might appear, especially if you're marking a very large number of emails. Confirm your choice to proceed. Once confirmed, all the selected emails will no longer appear bold (the visual indicator for unread emails) and your unread count should decrease significantly or disappear.

### Step 6: Verify and Undo (If Needed)

After the process completes, quickly scan your inbox to ensure the desired emails are marked as read. If you made a mistake or marked an important email as read accidentally, look for the 'Undo' notification that briefly appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click 'Undo' immediately to revert the action. If the 'Undo' option disappears, you can manually mark specific emails as unread by selecting them and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope).


## Tips & Tricks

- To mark emails in a specific category (e.g., Promotions, Social) as read, navigate to that category tab first, then follow the steps.
- Use the search bar with 'is:unread' to quickly filter and view only your unread emails before applying the 'Mark as read' action.
- Consider creating filters to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read if you know you don't need to review them individually.
- Regularly marking all emails as read can help you achieve 'inbox zero' and reduce digital clutter, making it easier to focus on new, important messages.
- On mobile, the process is similar: tap the three-line menu, go to your inbox, tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the top-right checkbox for all, and finally, tap the 'Mark as read' icon (open envelope).

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be careful when selecting 'all conversations that match this search' as it applies the action to ALL emails in your current view, which might include emails you haven't opened yet but intended to read later.
- ⚠️ There is no 'Are you sure?' prompt for marking all emails as read, so double-check your selection before clicking the icon.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will marking all emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete, archive, or move them from your inbox or any other folder. Your emails will remain accessible.

**Can I mark only specific unread emails as read, not all of them?**

Yes, you can. Instead of using the top checkbox to select all, simply click the individual checkbox next to each email you wish to mark as read. Once selected, click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**How can I find my unread emails after marking everything as read?**

You can easily find previously unread emails by using Gmail's search bar. Type 'is:unread' into the search bar and press Enter. This will display all emails that are currently marked as unread.

**Does this process work on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. You typically tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the square icon at the top to select all, and finally tap the 'Mark as read' icon (open envelope) in the top toolbar.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

If you act quickly, you can use the 'Undo' button that appears at the bottom-left of your screen immediately after the action. If you miss the 'Undo' option, you can search for the email and manually mark it as unread by selecting it and clicking the closed envelope icon.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read can help declutter your inbox, reduce the psychological burden of a high unread count, and allow you to start fresh. It's particularly useful after a vacation or if you've been away from your inbox for a while.

**Does this affect emails in my Sent, Drafts, or Spam folders?**

No, this action specifically applies to the emails visible in your current inbox view or selected category. It will not affect emails in other folders like Sent, Drafts, Spam, or Trash, as those are managed separately.
