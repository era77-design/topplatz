To mark all emails as read in Gmail, open your inbox, select the 'Select all' checkbox at the top, and then click the 'Mark as read' icon. This action helps you quickly clear your unread count and achieve an organized inbox view.

## What You Need

- A computer or mobile device with internet access
- A Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Browser

Start by navigating to mail.google.com in your preferred web browser on a desktop computer. Ensure you are logged into the Gmail account you wish to manage. If you're new to Gmail or need to set up an account, you can find helpful guides on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Folder or Tab

Once Gmail is open, go to the specific folder or tab where the emails you want to mark as read are located. This could be your 'Inbox', 'Promotions', 'Social', 'Updates', or any other custom label. The process works best when you are within the specific view you want to clear.

### Step 3: Select All Emails on the Current Page

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left of your email list, just above the 'Compose' button. Click this checkbox to select all the emails currently visible on your screen. This typically selects up to 50 conversations at a time.

### Step 4: Select All Conversations in the Folder (Important Step)

After selecting the emails on the current page, a message will appear just above your email list stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link that says 'Select all [number] conversations that match this search'. Click this link to extend your selection to every email within that specific folder or tab, not just those visible on the current page.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

With all desired emails selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar that appears above your email list. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click it to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Gmail will usually display a small notification at the bottom-left of your screen confirming that the action has been completed. For instance, it might say 'Conversations marked as read.' If you made a mistake, you often have a few seconds to click 'Undo' in this notification.

### Step 7: Repeat for Other Folders or Labels (If Necessary)

If you have unread emails in other folders, such as 'Spam,' 'Trash,' or custom labels, you will need to navigate to each of those sections and repeat the selection and 'Mark as read' process. This method applies only to the current view you are in.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly find all unread emails across your entire inbox, type 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar and then apply the 'Select all' method.
- If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, simply click the checkbox next to each email individually before clicking the 'Mark as read' icon.
- Utilize Gmail's filtering capabilities to automatically mark certain types of incoming emails as read, helping to manage your inbox proactively.
- Regularly marking emails as read can help you achieve 'inbox zero' and reduce digital clutter, making it easier to focus on important messages.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Marking all emails as read can sometimes lead to missing important messages if you rely solely on the 'unread' status for new information. Always skim your inbox or use search to ensure you don't overlook critical communications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Users often mark all emails as read to clear their unread count, achieve 'inbox zero,' or simply declutter their inbox view. It helps in managing overwhelming email volumes and focusing on current, relevant messages.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Yes, immediately after marking emails as read, Gmail provides an 'Undo' option at the bottom-left of your screen for a short period. Clicking this will revert the action. If you miss this window, you would need to manually mark specific emails as unread again.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them, archive them, or move them to any other folder. The emails remain in their original location.

**How do I mark only some emails as read?**

To mark only specific emails as read, simply click the individual checkbox next to each email you wish to select. Once you've chosen all the desired emails, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar.

**Does this method work on the Gmail mobile app?**

The general principle is the same on the Gmail mobile app, but the interface differs slightly. You typically tap and hold an email to select it, then tap the 'select all' option (often a checkbox at the top) and look for a 'Mark as read' or envelope icon in the top toolbar to apply the action.

**What if I have multiple inbox tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions)?**

The 'Mark as read' action applies only to the specific tab or category you are currently viewing. You will need to navigate to each tab (e.g., Social, Promotions) and repeat the 'Select all' and 'Mark as read' process for each one individually.

**Can I set up a filter to automatically mark certain emails as read?**

Yes, Gmail's powerful filter system allows you to create rules to automatically mark incoming emails as read based on sender, subject, keywords, and more. Go to Gmail Settings > 'See all settings' > 'Filters and Blocked Addresses' to set this up.
