To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically use the 'select all' checkbox at the top of your inbox, followed by the 'Mark as read' option. This process quickly clears your unread email count, helping you manage your inbox more efficiently.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Log In to Your Gmail Account

First, open your web browser and navigate to gmail.com. Enter your email address or phone number and your password to log in. If you're new to Gmail and haven't set up an account yet, you might find our guide on [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) helpful. Once logged in, you should see your inbox displaying your emails.

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure you're using a secure and trusted network when logging into your email.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Email View

Before marking emails as read, decide which emails you want to affect. You can apply this action to your primary inbox, a specific tab (like Social or Promotions), or even search results. For marking all emails across your entire inbox, ensure you are viewing the main 'Inbox' or 'All Mail' section. If you only want to clear a specific category, click on that tab first.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

Locate the small checkbox at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the 'Compose' button. Clicking this checkbox will select all emails currently visible on the page. This typically selects 50 emails at a time, depending on your display settings. A message will appear indicating how many conversations are selected.

### Step 4: Select All Emails Across All Pages

After selecting the visible emails, a notification will appear at the top of your inbox, usually stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also offer an option that says 'Select all [number] conversations in Primary' (or whichever category you're in). Click this link to extend your selection to every email in that category, not just those on the current page.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all desired emails selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your email list. It contains several icons. Find the 'Mark as read' icon, which often looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. Gmail will process this action, and your unread count will update accordingly.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

A small notification will usually pop up at the bottom-left of your screen, confirming that the emails have been marked as read. You can also visually inspect your inbox to see that the bold formatting (indicating unread status) has been removed from the emails. If you made a mistake, this notification often includes an 'Undo' option that you can click immediately to revert the change.


## Tips & Tricks

- Use Gmail's search bar (e.g., 'is:unread') to find all unread emails across your entire account, then apply the 'mark as read' action.
- Regularly review your unread emails in different categories to prevent your inbox from becoming overwhelming.
- Consider using Gmail filters to automatically mark certain types of emails as read upon arrival, especially newsletters or notifications you don't need to read immediately.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be careful when using the 'Select all conversations' option, especially in your main inbox, as it will affect every email. Ensure you truly want to mark all of them as read.
- ⚠️ Marking emails as read does not archive or delete them; it only changes their status. They will still occupy space in your inbox or labels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark specific emails as read without selecting all?**

Yes, you can individually select emails using the checkbox next to each email, or open an email and click the 'Mark as read' button in the toolbar. This gives you more granular control over your inbox.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

If you act quickly after marking emails as read, you might see an 'Undo' option appear at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it to revert the action. Otherwise, you can locate the email and manually mark it as unread again.

**Does marking all emails as read also archive them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not archive, delete, or move them from your inbox. They will remain in their current location.

**Can I mark all emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the mobile app. You can long-press an email to select it, then tap the top-left checkbox to select all visible emails. Then, tap the three-dot menu icon and choose 'Mark as read'.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read can help you achieve 'Inbox Zero' and reduce visual clutter, making it easier to focus on new, incoming messages. It's also useful for clearing out old notifications or promotional emails you don't intend to read.

**Will marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status. To delete emails, you would need to select them and then click the 'Delete' (trash can) icon in the toolbar. These are separate actions in Gmail.

**How can I find only unread emails to mark them as read?**

You can use the search bar at the top of Gmail. Type 'is:unread' and press Enter. This will display all unread emails across your entire Gmail account, allowing you to select and mark them as read efficiently.
