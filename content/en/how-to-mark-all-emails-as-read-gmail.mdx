To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you can use the "Select all" checkbox at the top of your inbox, then click "Select all conversations that match this search" if applicable, and finally click the "Mark as read" icon. This process helps you quickly clear your unread count across your various email categories.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account (or learn [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account) if you don't have one)

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account if you haven't already. Ensure you are viewing the inbox or a specific label where you intend to mark emails as read, such as your Primary, Social, or Promotions tab.

### Step 2: Select Emails on the Current Page

In your inbox, locate the small checkbox positioned at the top left, just above your list of emails. Click this checkbox to select all conversations currently displayed on that particular page. A message will appear, indicating how many conversations have been selected.

### Step 3: Select All Matching Conversations (Optional)

If you have more than 50 conversations (the default number displayed per page) or are in a specific search result, a banner will appear at the top. This banner will state something like, "All 50 conversations on this page are selected. Select all [number] conversations that match this search." Click this link to extend your selection to *all* emails in that specific view, not just those visible on the current page.

### Step 4: Mark Selected Emails as Read

Once all your desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar that appears above your inbox. Find the "Mark as read" icon, which typically resembles an open envelope. Click this icon to instantly change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action

Gmail will quickly process your request. You might see a brief notification confirming that the conversations have been marked as read. The bold formatting, which indicates an unread status, will disappear from the selected emails, and your overall unread count will update accordingly.

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Categories/Labels (If Needed)

If you utilize Gmail's category tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums) or have created custom labels, you may need to navigate to each category or label separately. Repeat steps 2-5 within each section to ensure all emails are marked as read across your entire mailbox. This comprehensive approach helps you achieve a truly clear inbox.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly find only unread emails, type 'is:unread' into the Gmail search bar and press Enter.
- You can select multiple individual emails by holding down the Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac) key and clicking each email, or by holding Shift and clicking the first and last email in a range.
- Enable keyboard shortcuts in Gmail settings (Settings > See all settings > General) to use `*` then `a` to select all, then `Shift + i` to mark as read.
- Regularly marking emails as read helps maintain an organized inbox and can significantly reduce visual clutter and mental stress.
- Consider creating filters to automatically mark certain incoming emails as read or move them to specific labels, especially for newsletters or notifications you don't need to read immediately.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking a large volume of emails as read, especially if you haven't reviewed them recently. You might inadvertently skip important unread messages. Always do a quick scan if you're unsure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark all emails in my entire Gmail account as read at once?**

Yes, you can. Navigate to your inbox, select all emails on the current page using the top checkbox, and then click the banner option to "Select all conversations that match this search." After this, click the "Mark as read" icon.

**What if I accidentally mark an important email as read?**

If you accidentally mark an email as read, you can easily revert this. Simply select the email again (it will no longer be bold) and click the "Mark as unread" icon, which typically looks like a closed envelope, in the toolbar above your inbox.

**How do I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

On the Gmail mobile app, tap and hold an email to select it. Then, you can either manually tap other emails to select them or look for a "Select all" option (often represented by three dots or a checkbox icon). Once selected, tap the three dots menu and choose "Mark as read."

**Can I mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

Absolutely. Use the Gmail search bar to find emails from that specific sender (e.g., type "from:sender@example.com"). Once the search results appear, select all the matching emails using the top checkbox, and then click the "Mark as read" icon.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking an email as read only changes its status from unread to read. It does not delete the email, move it to the trash, or archive it. The email will remain in your inbox or its respective label/category.

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is beneficial for several reasons, such as clearing a large backlog, achieving "inbox zero" for a fresh start, or simply decluttering your inbox visually, especially after a vacation or a period of high email volume. It helps you focus on new, incoming messages.

**Is there a way to automatically mark certain emails as read upon arrival?**

Yes, you can set up a filter in Gmail to do this. Go to Settings > See all settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses. Create a new filter based on criteria like sender, subject, or keywords, and then choose the action "Mark as read" for matching incoming emails.
