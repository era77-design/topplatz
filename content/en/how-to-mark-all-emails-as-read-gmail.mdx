To mark all emails as read in Gmail, navigate to your inbox, select all visible conversations using the checkbox, then click the option to select all conversations in your current view, and finally click the 'Mark as read' icon. This clears your unread count for selected emails.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- A Google Account with Gmail access (if you don't have one, learn [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account))

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Account

Open your preferred web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) and go to mail.google.com. Log in with your Google Account credentials if you're not already signed in. Ensure you are in your main Inbox view or the specific label/folder where you want to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Select All Visible Emails on the Page

Locate the small square checkbox at the top of your email list, just above the 'Compose' button and below the search bar. Click this checkbox. This action will select all emails currently displayed on the first page of your inbox or the selected folder.

### Step 3: Select All Conversations in the Current View

After selecting all visible emails, a banner will appear above your email list. It will typically say something like 'All X conversations on this page are selected. Select all Y conversations in [Primary/Inbox/Label]'. Click on the second part of this banner (e.g., 'Select all Y conversations in Primary') to extend your selection to every email within that specific view, not just those visible on the first page.

### Step 4: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar located just above your email list. This icon typically looks like an open envelope. Click on it to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read.

### Step 5: Confirm the Action (If Prompted)

For very large selections, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up asking if you are sure you want to perform this action on many conversations. Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. The process might take a few moments depending on the number of emails being updated.

### Step 6: Repeat for Other Categories (Optional)

If you use Gmail's categorized inbox (e.g., Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums), you will need to navigate to each tab individually and repeat steps 2-5 for the emails within those specific categories. This ensures all your emails across different sections are marked as read. If you're new to Gmail, understanding these features is key. For help getting started, you can refer to our guide on [How to Create a New Gmail Account - Step-by-Step Guide](/en/how-to-create-new-gmail-account).


## Tips & Tricks

- Use the search bar: To mark specific types of emails as read, use Gmail's powerful search function (e.g., 'is:unread from:sender@example.com' or 'category:promotions is:unread'). Then, select all results and mark them as read.
- Undo functionality: If you accidentally mark emails as read, you can often click the 'Undo' button that appears briefly at the bottom left of your screen after the action. Alternatively, you can manually mark specific emails as unread again.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Enable keyboard shortcuts in Gmail settings for faster navigation and actions. Press 'x' to select an email, then 'Shift + i' to mark it as read. To mark multiple selected emails as read, press 'Shift + i'.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when selecting all emails, especially if you have important unread messages that you still need to review. Marking them as read might make them harder to spot later.
- ⚠️ This action cannot be fully reversed for a large batch of emails with a single click after the undo window closes. You would have to manually mark individual emails as unread again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. It does not delete them or move them from your inbox. They will still be accessible in your folders.

**Can I mark specific emails as read without affecting others?**

Yes, you can individually select specific emails by clicking the checkbox next to each one. Once selected, you can then click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

Immediately after the action, a small 'Undo' button usually appears at the bottom left of your screen. Click it quickly to reverse the action. If you miss the undo window, you'll need to find and manually mark those specific emails as unread again.

**How can I mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

You can use Gmail's search bar. Type 'from:sender@example.com is:unread' (replacing 'sender@example.com' with the actual email address). Then, select all the search results and mark them as read.

**Will this clear my unread count on my mobile app?**

Yes, marking emails as read in the web version of Gmail will synchronize across all your devices, including the Gmail mobile app. Your unread count will update accordingly.

**Is there a way to automatically mark future emails as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a direct feature to automatically mark *all* future emails as read. However, you can create filters that automatically mark specific types of incoming emails (e.g., from certain senders or with specific keywords) as read upon arrival.

**Why would someone want to mark all emails as read?**

Many users do this to clear a backlog of old or less important notifications, to achieve 'inbox zero,' or simply to reduce the visual clutter of a high unread count, making it easier to focus on new, relevant messages.
