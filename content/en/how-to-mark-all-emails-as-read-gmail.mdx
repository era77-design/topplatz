To mark all emails as read in Gmail, navigate to your inbox or a specific category, select the checkbox at the top left, and then click the option to 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon (an open envelope) to clear your unread count. This process helps you quickly manage an overflowing inbox.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device with internet access
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Gmail in Your Web Browser

Start by opening your preferred web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) and navigating to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account if you haven't already. This will take you directly to your primary inbox, where you can begin the process of marking emails as read.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Desired Inbox or Label

Decide which emails you want to mark as read. You can do this for your main 'Primary' inbox, or switch to other categories like 'Social,' 'Promotions,' or specific labels you've created. Use the left-hand menu to select the inbox category or label you wish to clear. For users who might be setting up their first email account, managing these sections can be a big help after following guides like [How to Create a Gmail Account Quickly and Easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account).

### Step 3: Select All Visible Emails

At the top of your email list, just above the first email, you'll see a small square checkbox. Click this checkbox. This action will select all the emails currently visible on your screen, typically about 50 conversations. You'll notice that all the selected emails are highlighted.

### Step 4: Extend Selection to All Emails in the Category

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear just above your email list, stating something like 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, you'll also see a link that says 'Select all [number] conversations in [category name].' Click this link to extend your selection beyond the current page and include every single email in that specific inbox or label.

### Step 5: Click the 'Mark as Read' Icon

Once all desired emails are selected, look for the toolbar just above your emails. You'll find several icons there. Locate the 'Mark as read' icon, which typically looks like an open envelope. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action will remove the bold formatting from the emails and decrease your unread count.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action (if prompted)

For very large selections, Gmail might ask you to confirm this bulk action. A small pop-up window may appear, asking 'Are you sure you want to mark all conversations as read?' Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. This ensures that you don't accidentally mark important emails as read without intending to.

### Step 7: Repeat for Other Categories (Optional)

If you have unread emails in other inbox categories (like Social, Promotions, or Updates) or under different labels, you will need to repeat these steps for each category individually. Navigate to the next category from the left menu and follow steps 3-6 again. This ensures a comprehensive cleanup of your entire Gmail account.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark individual emails as read without opening them, hover over the email in your inbox and click the 'Mark as read' icon that appears on the right side of the email row.
- If you only want to mark a few specific emails as read, check the box next to each individual email you wish to select, then click the 'Mark as read' icon.
- Use Gmail's search bar (at the top) to find specific unread emails. You can type `is:unread` to see all unread messages, or `label:promotions is:unread` to find unread emails in a specific category.
- Consider setting up filters to automatically mark certain incoming emails as read or move them to specific labels, helping you manage your inbox proactively.
- On the Gmail mobile app, navigate to the desired inbox, long-press one email to select it, then tap additional emails to select them. Tap the three-dot menu, then choose 'Mark as read'.

## Warnings

- ⚠️ Be cautious when marking all emails as read, especially if you have a very large inbox, as this action cannot be easily undone for a massive selection. Important emails might get overlooked if you don't review them first.
- ⚠️ Marking all emails as read does not delete them. They will remain in your inbox or respective labels, just without the 'unread' indicator.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why would I want to mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read helps achieve 'inbox zero,' reducing clutter and mental load. It can make it easier to identify truly new and important messages that arrive after the bulk marking, improving overall email management.

**Can I undo marking all emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, you might see an 'Undo' button pop up at the bottom left of your screen. Clicking this within a few seconds will revert the action. However, after this short window, there isn't a simple 'undo all' button for a large selection.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read only changes their status from unread to read. The emails remain in your inbox or whichever folder/label they were in. It does not move them to trash or archive them.

**How can I mark only certain emails as read, not all?**

To mark specific emails as read, simply check the box next to each individual email you want to select. Once you've selected all the desired emails, click the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your inbox.

**Can I mark all emails in a specific Gmail category (like Promotions) as read?**

Yes, you can. First, click on the specific category (e.g., 'Promotions') in your left-hand menu. Then, follow the same steps: click the top-left checkbox, and then click 'Select all conversations in Promotions' to mark them all as read.

**Is there a way to mark emails as read automatically?**

Yes, you can set up Gmail filters to automatically mark certain incoming emails as read. Go to 'Settings' > 'See all settings' > 'Filters and Blocked Addresses' and create a new filter with criteria that specifies 'Mark as read' as an action for matching emails.

**What if I accidentally mark important emails as read?**

If you accidentally mark an important email as read, you can quickly find it by searching for keywords or the sender. Once found, you can manually mark it as unread by checking its box and clicking the 'Mark as unread' icon (a closed envelope) in the toolbar.
