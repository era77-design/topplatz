To mark all emails as read in Gmail, you typically use the search bar to find unread messages, select them all with a single click, and then use the 'Mark as read' option. This process allows you to quickly clear your inbox of unread notifications, whether for all emails or a filtered selection.

## What You Need

- Computer or mobile device
- Internet connection
- Gmail account

## Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Gmail Inbox

Open your preferred web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Edge) and navigate to mail.google.com. Log in to your Gmail account if you are not already signed in. Ensure you are viewing your main inbox or the specific tab (Primary, Social, Promotions) where you want to mark emails as read.

### Step 2: Search for Unread Emails

In the search bar at the top of your Gmail interface, type 'is:unread' and press Enter. This action will filter your entire inbox to display only the emails that are currently marked as unread. This is the crucial first step to gather all unread messages in one view.

### Step 3: Select All Visible Unread Emails

Once the unread emails are displayed, locate the 'Select' checkbox. This is usually found at the top-left corner of your email list, just above the first email. Click this checkbox to select all the unread emails currently visible on your screen. This will typically select up to 50 conversations.

### Step 4: Select All Unread Emails (Beyond Visible)

After selecting the visible emails, a message will appear just above your email list stating, 'All 50 conversations on this page are selected.' Crucially, it will also include a link: 'Select all conversations that match this search.' Click this link to extend your selection to *all* unread emails in your entire inbox, not just those currently displayed.

### Step 5: Mark Selected Emails as Read

With all unread emails now selected, look for the 'Mark as read' icon in the toolbar above your emails. This icon typically looks like an open envelope or an eye. Click this icon to change the status of all selected emails from unread to read. This action will update their status across your Gmail account.

### Step 6: Confirm the Action

Depending on the number of emails, Gmail might display a confirmation pop-up asking, 'Are you sure you want to take this action on all conversations that match this search?' Click 'OK' or 'Confirm' to proceed. The page will then refresh, and your inbox should now reflect that all previously unread emails are marked as read, helping you achieve inbox zero.

### Step 7: Repeat for Other Inbox Tabs (Optional)

If you use Gmail's categorized inbox (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums), the 'is:unread' search typically applies only to the tab you are currently viewing. Navigate to other tabs individually and repeat steps 2-6 for each tab to ensure all unread emails across all categories are marked as read.


## Tips & Tricks

- To quickly mark specific emails as read without searching, simply select them using their individual checkboxes and then click the 'Mark as read' icon.
- Utilize Gmail's filters to automatically manage incoming emails, sending certain types directly to specific labels or even marking them as read upon arrival to prevent future clutter.
- Regularly check your Spam and Trash folders, as emails moved there are not included in the 'is:unread' search and might still contribute to your overall unread count.



## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I mark emails as read on the Gmail mobile app?**

Yes, the process is similar on the Gmail mobile app. You can search for 'is:unread', then tap and hold one email to select it, then tap the three dots or a multi-select icon to 'Select all', and finally choose 'Mark as read' from the options.

**What if I only want to mark emails from a specific sender as read?**

To mark emails from a specific sender as read, use the search bar with 'from:sender@example.com is:unread'. This will filter for only unread emails from that sender. Then, follow the steps to select all and mark them as read.

**Does marking emails as read delete them?**

No, marking emails as read does not delete them. It simply changes their status from 'unread' to 'read'. The emails will remain in your inbox or their respective labels until you explicitly archive or delete them.

**How do I undo marking emails as read?**

Immediately after marking emails as read, a small 'Undo' notification will appear at the bottom-left of your screen. Click it quickly to reverse the action. If you miss the undo option, you'll need to search for those specific emails and manually mark them as unread again.

**Why would I mark all emails as read?**

Marking all emails as read is useful for quickly clearing a cluttered inbox, especially if you've been away or are starting fresh with a new account. It helps achieve 'inbox zero' and focuses your attention on new, incoming messages, making email management more efficient, particularly after you [create a Gmail account quickly and easily](/en/how-to-create-gmail-account) and receive many initial notifications.

**Can I mark emails as read in specific categories (e.g., Promotions)?**

Yes, you can. First, navigate to the specific category tab (e.g., 'Promotions') in your Gmail inbox. Then, follow the same steps: use 'is:unread' in the search bar, select all visible emails, click 'Select all conversations that match this search', and finally, click the 'Mark as read' icon.

**Is there a way to automatically mark emails as read?**

Gmail doesn't have a direct 'auto-mark as read' feature for all incoming mail. However, you can create filters that automatically archive certain emails upon arrival. Archiving effectively removes them from your inbox and unread count, making them behave as if they were read without you manually opening them.
