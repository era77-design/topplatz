Muscle growth, including in your glutes, requires sufficient protein intake. Aim for about 0.7-1 gram of protein per pound of body weight daily, distributed across your meals. Foods rich in protein like chicken breast, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, and legumes are crucial. Combine these with complex carbohydrates (oats, brown rice, sweet potatoes) and healthy fats (avocado, nuts) for overall muscle growth.
