**What role does sleep play in muscle growth?**

Sleep is crucial for muscle growth and recovery. During deep sleep, your body releases growth hormone, which is essential for muscle repair and synthesis. Insufficient sleep can impair recovery, reduce strength and endurance, and negatively impact your ability to build muscle effectively.
